～わりに（は）: considering / for (unexpected for)

わり means "proportion". ～わりに says that the result is different from what you'd expect given a certain fact.

In English it is "considering" or "for", as in "it's cheap for a hotel". The difference can be positive or negative.

1. Better than expected

The first part sets up an expectation, the second goes beyond it.

この店は値段のわりにおいしい。

"This place is tasty for the price."

  • この = this
  • 店（みせ）= restaurant
  • は = topic marker
  • 値段（ねだん）= price
  • のわりに = considering
  • おいしい = tasty

2. Worse than expected

It can also point out a disappointing gap.

よく勉強したわりには、点数が低かった。

"Considering how much I studied, my score was low."

  • よく = a lot
  • 勉強した（べんきょうした）= studied
  • わりには = considering
  • 点数（てんすう）= score
  • 低かった（ひくかった）= was low

How to form it

Noun + のわりに, Plain form + わりに (な-adjectives take な)

  • 年（とし）= age → 年のわりに（としのわりに）= for (one's) age
  • 若い（わかい）= young → 若いわりに（わかいわりに）= for someone so young
  • 有名（ゆうめい）= famous → 有名なわりに（ゆうめいなわりに）= considering how famous (it) is

Key idea

～わりに compares the result with the expectation and notes the gap.
