～おきに: every other / at intervals

おきに after a number says that something repeats with a gap of that size.

In English it maps to "every" or "every other". With days and weeks it skips one unit in between; with hours and minutes it simply gives the interval.

1. A regular interval

With hours, minutes or distances, it gives the space between repetitions.

薬は六時間おきに飲んでください。

"Take the medicine every six hours."

  • 薬（くすり）= medicine
  • は = topic marker
  • 六時間（ろくじかん）= six hours
  • おきに = every
  • 飲んで（のんで）= take (medicine)
  • ください = please

2. Every other day

一日おきに skips one day in between, so it means every second day.

一日おきにジムに行っている。

"I go to the gym every other day."

  • 一日（いちにち）= one day
  • おきに = at intervals of
  • ジム = gym
  • に = to
  • 行っている（いっている）= go (regularly)

How to form it

Number + counter + おきに

  • 十分（じゅっぷん）= ten minutes → 十分おきに（じゅっぷんおきに）= every ten minutes
  • 一週間（いっしゅうかん）= one week → 一週間おきに（いっしゅうかんおきに）= every other week
  • 二メートル（にメートル）= two meters → 二メートルおきに（にメートルおきに）= every two meters

Key idea

おきに measures the gap between repetitions. With days and weeks, 一日おき means every second day.
