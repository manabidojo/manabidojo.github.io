～かしら: I wonder (feminine)

かしら at the end of a sentence means the speaker is wondering, just like かな. It is traditionally feminine speech.

In English it is "I wonder...". Today it sounds soft and somewhat old-fashioned or elegant, and in anime it often marks a refined female character.

1. Wondering to yourself

The speaker is unsure and thinks out loud.

彼、来るかしら。

"I wonder if he'll come."

  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • 来る（くる）= to come
  • かしら = I wonder

2. Soft requests

With a negative, ～てくれないかしら is a gentle request.

ちょっと手伝ってくれないかしら。

"Could you help me a little, I wonder?"

  • ちょっと = a little
  • 手伝って（てつだって）= help
  • くれない = won't (do for me)
  • かしら = I wonder

How to form it

Plain form + かしら (nouns and な-adjectives drop だ)

  • 大丈夫（だいじょうぶ）= okay → 大丈夫かしら（だいじょうぶかしら）= I wonder if it's okay
  • 雨（あめ）= rain → 雨かしら（あめかしら）= I wonder if it'll rain
  • 何（なに）= what → 何かしら（なにかしら）= what could it be

Key idea

かしら is the feminine かな: a soft, wondering ending.
