～ていく: an action or change moving away from the speaker

～ていく is made by taking the て-form of a verb and adding いく（行く, to go）. Literally it means "do something and go", and it also describes changes that continue away into the future. It is the mirror image of ～てくる.

In English it maps to expressions like "take (along)", "go on doing", or "will keep getting".

1. Doing something and going / taking along

Use ～ていく when someone does an action and then moves away from where the speaker is, often when carrying something with you.

お弁当を持っていきます。

"I'll take a boxed lunch with me."

  • お弁当（おべんとう）= boxed lunch
  • を = object marker
  • 持っていきます（もっていきます）= will take (along)

2. A change continuing into the future

～ていく also describes a change that starts from the present and keeps going from now on.

これから寒くなっていく。

"It will gradually get colder from now on."

  • これから = from now on
  • 寒く（さむく）= cold (adverb form of 寒い)
  • なっていく = will go on becoming

Compare: 寒くなってきた looks back at the change up to now, while 寒くなっていく looks forward at the change still to come.

How to form it

Verb て-form + いく

  • 持つ（もつ）= to hold → 持っていく（もっていく）= take along
  • なる = to become → なっていく = go on becoming
  • 変わる（かわる）= to change → 変わっていく（かわっていく）= keep changing

Key idea

～ていく points away from the speaker, away in space (someone leaves with something) or forward in time (a change moves on into the future).
