～にあたって: on the occasion of, at the juncture of

～にあたって attaches to a noun or a verb in dictionary form and marks an important occasion at which something is done, typically the start of a new venture, a ceremony, or a major step. It corresponds to English "on the occasion of" or "at the time of (doing)".

It is a formal expression used in speeches, announcements, and business writing, so it is reserved for significant events rather than everyday actions. Unlike the neutral ～とき, you would not use it for something as ordinary as brushing your teeth. The written variant ～にあたり is even more formal.

Marking a significant juncture

Use ～にあたって to introduce what is done, prepared, or said at the moment of an important undertaking.

新製品の発売にあたって、記者会見を開いた。

"On the occasion of the new product's launch, a press conference was held."

  • 新製品（しんせいひん）= new product
  • の = possessive marker
  • 発売（はつばい）= launch, release
  • にあたって = on the occasion of
  • 記者会見（きしゃかいけん）= press conference
  • を = object marker
  • 開いた（ひらいた）= held, opened

留学するにあたって、いろいろな準備が必要だ。

"When you set out to study abroad, all sorts of preparations are necessary."

  • 留学する（りゅうがくする）= to study abroad
  • にあたって = at the juncture of
  • いろいろな = various
  • 準備（じゅんび）= preparations
  • が = subject marker
  • 必要だ（ひつようだ）= is necessary

How to form it

Noun + にあたって / Verb dictionary form + にあたって (formal written: にあたり)

  • 卒業（そつぎょう）= graduation → 卒業にあたって（そつぎょうにあたって）= upon graduating
  • 始める（はじめる）= to begin → 始めるにあたって（はじめるにあたって）= when beginning
  • 開会（かいかい）= opening (of a meeting) → 開会にあたり（かいかいにあたり）= at the opening (very formal)

Key idea

～にあたって spotlights a big moment (a launch, a departure, an opening) and frames what you do in preparation for or in recognition of that moment. Formal occasions only.
