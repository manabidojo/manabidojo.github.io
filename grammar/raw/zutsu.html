～ずつ: each / at a time

ずつ after an amount says that the same amount goes to each person, each item or each step.

In English it is "each" or "at a time". With 少し it becomes "little by little".

1. The same amount for everyone

It divides things evenly between people or items.

一人二つずつ取ってください。

"Please take two each."

  • 一人（ひとり）= one person
  • 二つ（ふたつ）= two (things)
  • ずつ = each
  • 取って（とって）= take
  • ください = please

2. Little by little

With amounts like 少し or 一つ, it describes slow, steady progress.

日本語が少しずつ分かるようになってきた。

"I'm gradually starting to understand Japanese."

  • 日本語（にほんご）= Japanese
  • が = subject marker
  • 少しずつ（すこしずつ）= little by little
  • 分かる（わかる）= to understand
  • ようになってきた = have come to

How to form it

Number / amount + ずつ

  • 一人（ひとり）= one person → 一人ずつ（ひとりずつ）= one person at a time
  • 一枚（いちまい）= one sheet → 一枚ずつ（いちまいずつ）= one sheet each
  • 少し（すこし）= a little → 少しずつ（すこしずつ）= little by little

Key idea

ずつ spreads an amount evenly: per person, per item, or bit by bit over time.
