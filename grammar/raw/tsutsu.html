～つつ: while doing (literary)

～つつ attaches to the ます-stem of a verb and links it to a second action: 聴きます becomes 聴きつつ, "while listening". It is the written-register counterpart of ～ながら and is typical of essays, news, and formal speech.

It can be translated as "while doing X" or "even while doing X" (often strengthened to ～つつも). Do not confuse it with ～つつある, a separate pattern meaning "in the process of".

1. Two simultaneous actions

The verb before つつ describes a background action accompanying the main verb. Both actions are performed by the same subject.

音楽を聴きつつ勉強する。

"I study while listening to music."

  • 音楽（おんがく）= music
  • を = object marker
  • 聴きつつ（ききつつ）= while listening
  • 勉強する（べんきょうする）= to study

2. Concession: "even while doing"

Sometimes the two clauses contradict each other and then ～つつ（も） means "even though".

体に悪いと知りつつ、たばこを吸ってしまう。

"Even though I know it's bad for my health, I end up smoking."

  • 体（からだ）= body, health
  • に = marks what the badness applies to (for)
  • 悪い（わるい）= bad
  • と = quotation marker (that)
  • 知りつつ（しりつつ）= even while knowing
  • たばこ = cigarettes
  • を = object marker
  • 吸ってしまう（すってしまう）= end up smoking (てしまう adds regret)

How to form it

Verb ます-stem + つつ（も）

  • 聴く（きく）= to listen → 聴きつつ（ききつつ）= while listening
  • 知る（しる）= to know → 知りつつ（しりつつ）= even while knowing
  • 思う（おもう）= to think → 思いつつ（おもいつつ）= while thinking

Key idea

～つつ is a literary "while". When the two clauses cooperate, it reads as simple simultaneity. When they clash, especially with 知る or 思う, it reads as "even though".
