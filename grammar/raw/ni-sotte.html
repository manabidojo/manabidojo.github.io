～に沿って: along / in line with

沿う means "to run along". ～に沿って describes moving or being along something long, like a river or road, and also acting in line with a plan or wish.

In English it is "along" or "in line with".

1. Along a path

With roads, rivers and walls, it describes physical position or movement.

川に沿って歩いた。

"I walked along the river."

  • 川（かわ）= river
  • に沿って（にそって）= along
  • 歩いた（あるいた）= walked

2. Following a plan

With 計画, 方針 or 希望, it means acting in accordance with it.

計画に沿って作業を進めてください。

"Please proceed with the work according to the plan."

  • 計画（けいかく）= plan
  • に沿って（にそって）= in line with
  • 作業（さぎょう）= work
  • を = object marker
  • 進めて（すすめて）= proceed
  • ください = please

How to form it

Noun + に沿って (before a noun: に沿った + noun)

  • 道（みち）= road → 道に沿って（みちにそって）= along the road
  • 線路（せんろ）= railway track → 線路に沿って（せんろにそって）= along the tracks
  • 希望（きぼう）= wishes → 希望に沿った案（きぼうにそったあん）= a plan in line with the wishes

Key idea

～に沿って follows a line: a river on the ground, or a plan on paper.
