～とともに: together with / along with

ともに means "together". ～とともに has two uses: doing something together with someone, and one thing changing along with another.

In English it is "together with", "along with" or "as". It is more formal than と一緒に.

1. Changing along with something

Two changes happen side by side.

時代とともに言葉も変わる。

"Language changes with the times."

  • 時代（じだい）= the times
  • とともに = along with
  • 言葉（ことば）= language
  • も = also
  • 変わる（かわる）= to change

2. Together with someone

It also means doing something in someone's company.

家族とともに新しい年を迎えた。

"I welcomed the new year with my family."

  • 家族（かぞく）= family
  • とともに = together with
  • 新しい年（あたらしいとし）= new year
  • を = object marker
  • 迎えた（むかえた）= welcomed

3. At the same time as

After a verb, it can also mean that two things happen at the same moment.

試合終了の笛が鳴るとともに、観客が立ち上がった。

"As the final whistle blew, the crowd rose to their feet."

  • 試合終了（しあいしゅうりょう）= end of the match
  • の = (of)
  • 笛（ふえ）= whistle
  • が鳴る（がなる）= blows
  • とともに = as
  • 観客（かんきゃく）= spectators
  • 立ち上がった（たちあがった）= stood up

How to form it

Noun / verb dictionary form + とともに

  • 仲間（なかま）= friends → 仲間とともに（なかまとともに）= together with friends
  • 成長する（せいちょうする）= to grow → 成長するとともに（せいちょうするとともに）= as (one) grows
  • 年齢（ねんれい）= age → 年齢とともに（ねんれいとともに）= with age

Key idea

～とともに puts two things side by side: people together, or changes that move together.
