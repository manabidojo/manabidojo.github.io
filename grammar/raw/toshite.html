～として: as / in the capacity of

～として follows a noun and marks the role, status, or capacity in which someone or something acts. It maps almost directly to English "as": "as a doctor", "as a hobby", "as a gift".

Be careful not to confuse it with ～にとって, which gives a point of view for a judgment ("for me, ..."); ～として instead names the hat someone is wearing while they act.

1. A person's role or status

The most common use: the noun before として is a profession, position, or identity, and the rest of the sentence describes what the person does or how they are treated in that role.

彼女はピアニストとして認められた。

"She was recognized as a pianist."

  • 彼女（かのじょ）= she
  • は = topic marker
  • ピアニスト = pianist
  • として = as / in the capacity of
  • 認められた（みとめられた）= was recognized

2. A thing's function or category

として also labels what role a thing plays: something done as a hobby, given as a present, or used as evidence.

趣味として写真を撮っています。

"I take photos as a hobby."

  • 趣味（しゅみ）= hobby
  • として = as
  • 写真（しゃしん）= photo
  • を = object marker
  • 撮っています（とっています）= take (habitually)

How to form it

Noun + として

  • 医者（いしゃ）= doctor → 医者として（いしゃとして）= as a doctor
  • 趣味（しゅみ）= hobby → 趣味として（しゅみとして）= as a hobby
  • 代表（だいひょう）= representative → 代表として（だいひょうとして）= as a representative

Key idea

～として answers the question "in what capacity?" Whenever English uses "as" to name a role, "as a friend, I have to tell you...", Japanese uses として.
