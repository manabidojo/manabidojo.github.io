～によって: depending on / by means of / by

～によって attaches to a noun and has several related jobs: it can mean "depending on", "by means of", or mark the agent "by" in a passive sentence. The shared core is that the noun is what the outcome hinges on.

In formal writing it often appears as ～により, and before a noun it becomes ～による.

1. Depending on

When the noun is something that varies (era, person, country), によって means the result differs case by case.

時代によって考え方が違う。

"Ways of thinking differ depending on the era."

  • 時代（じだい）= era, period
  • によって = depending on
  • 考え方（かんがえかた）= way of thinking
  • が = subject marker
  • 違う（ちがう）= differs

2. By means of

によって can mark the method or cause that brings a result about.

話し合いによって問題を解決した。

"We solved the problem through discussion."

  • 話し合い（はなしあい）= discussion
  • によって = by means of
  • 問題（もんだい）= problem
  • を = object marker
  • 解決した（かいけつした）= solved

3. Agent of a passive

In formal passive sentences, によって marks who did the action, like English "by".

この本は有名な作家によって書かれた。

"This book was written by a famous author."

  • この = this
  • 本（ほん）= book
  • は = topic marker
  • 有名な（ゆうめいな）= famous
  • 作家（さっか）= author
  • によって = by (agent)
  • 書かれた（かかれた）= was written

How to form it

Noun + によって / Noun + による + Noun

  • 人（ひと）= person → 人によって（ひとによって）= depending on the person
  • 国（くに）= country → 国によって（くにによって）= depending on the country
  • 地震（じしん）= earthquake → 地震による被害（じしんによるひがい）= damage caused by the earthquake

Key idea

～によって identifies the deciding factor. Change that noun and the outcome changes too.
