～最中: right in the middle of doing something

～最中（さいちゅう）pinpoints the very middle of an ongoing action, and is almost always used when something unexpected interrupts it. It is formed as Verb ている + 最中に or Noun + の最中に, and maps to English "right in the middle of ...". (Careful with the reading: the same kanji read もなか is a wafer sweet, here it is always さいちゅう.)

1. Noun + の最中に

The noun names the activity that was underway when the interruption hit.

食事の最中に電話が鳴った。

"The phone rang right in the middle of dinner."

  • 食事（しょくじ）= meal
  • の = linking particle
  • 最中（さいちゅう）= the very middle (of)
  • に = time marker
  • 電話（でんわ）= phone (call)
  • が = subject marker
  • 鳴った（なった）= rang

2. Verb ている + 最中に

With a verb, use the ている form before 最中に. The action must be in progress at that moment.

シャワーを浴びている最中に、お湯が止まってしまった。

"Right in the middle of my shower, the hot water cut out."

  • シャワー = shower
  • を = object marker
  • 浴びている（あびている）= am taking (a shower)
  • お湯（おゆ）= hot water
  • が = subject marker
  • 止まってしまった（とまってしまった）= stopped (regrettably)

How to form it

Verb ている + 最中に / Noun + の最中に

  • 食事（しょくじ）= meal → 食事の最中に（しょくじのさいちゅうに）= right in the middle of a meal
  • 話している（はなしている）= is talking → 話している最中に（はなしているさいちゅうに）= right in the middle of talking
  • 会議（かいぎ）= meeting → 会議の最中に（かいぎのさいちゅうに）= right in the middle of a meeting

Key idea

～最中 freezes an action at its midpoint and says "exactly then". What comes next is typically the interruption that broke into it.
