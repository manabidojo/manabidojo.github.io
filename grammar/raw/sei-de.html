～せいで: because of / due to (blame)

～せいで attributes a negative result to its cause: "because of X, something bad happened". After a noun it takes の (Noun + のせいで); after a verb or adjective it follows the plain form.

It always carries a tone of blame or complaint. Its grateful counterpart is ～おかげで, which credits a cause for a good result. Choose せいで for blame and おかげで for thanks.

1. Blaming a thing or situation

With a noun, link it to せいで with the particle の. The main clause states the unwelcome outcome.

雨のせいで試合が中止になった。

"Because of the rain, the match was cancelled."

  • 雨（あめ）= rain
  • の = linking particle
  • せいで = because of (blame)
  • 試合（しあい）= match, game
  • が = subject marker
  • 中止になった（ちゅうしになった）= was cancelled

2. Blaming an action or event

A whole clause can come before せいで, usually in the past tense. Speakers also use it about their own actions when admitting what caused the problem.

夜遅くまでゲームをしたせいで、寝坊した。

"Because I played games until late at night, I overslept."

  • 夜遅く（よるおそく）= late at night
  • まで = until
  • ゲーム = video games
  • を = object marker
  • した = did, played (past of する)
  • せいで = because of (blame)
  • 寝坊した（ねぼうした）= overslept

How to form it

Noun + のせいで / plain clause + せいで (na-adjectives take な: ～なせいで)

  • 雨（あめ）= rain → 雨のせいで（あめのせいで）= because of the rain
  • 食べすぎる（たべすぎる）= to eat too much → 食べすぎたせいで（たべすぎたせいで）= because (I) ate too much
  • 暑い（あつい）= hot → 暑いせいで（あついせいで）= because of the heat

Key idea

～せいで points the finger: the result is bad, and X is to blame. If the result is good and you feel thankful, switch to ～おかげで, the structure stays exactly the same.
