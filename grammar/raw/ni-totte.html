～にとって: from someone's standpoint

～にとって attaches to a noun, usually a person or group, and means "for ..." or "from the standpoint of ...". It is formed as Noun + にとって.

What follows is almost always a judgment or evaluation, such as 大切 (important), 難しい (difficult), or 便利 (convenient), not an action.

Evaluating something from a standpoint

Use ～にとって to say who the following judgment is true for. The same thing can be easy for one person and hard for another; ～にとって names whose perspective you mean.

私にとって家族は大切です。

"For me, family is important."

  • 私（わたし）= I, me
  • にとって = for, from the standpoint of
  • 家族（かぞく）= family
  • は = topic marker
  • 大切（たいせつ）= important, precious

子供にとって、この本は難しいです。

"For children, this book is difficult."

  • 子供（こども）= child, children
  • にとって = for, from the standpoint of
  • この = this
  • 本（ほん）= book
  • は = topic marker
  • 難しい（むずかしい）= difficult

How to form it

Noun + にとって

  • 私（わたし）= I → 私にとって（わたしにとって）= for me
  • 子供（こども）= child → 子供にとって（こどもにとって）= for children
  • 学生（がくせい）= student → 学生にとって（がくせいにとって）= for students

Key idea

～にとって sets the viewpoint: for the person or group named, the statement that follows is true. It introduces an evaluation, not an action.
