～が最後: once (it happens), there's no stopping

最後 means "the end". After a た-form, ～が最後 says that once something happens, a certain result follows inevitably, usually something hard to stop or undo.

In English it is "once..., (there's no going back)" or "the moment..., that's it".

A point of no return

Once the first thing happens, the result follows inevitably and can't be stopped or undone.

彼は寝たが最後、朝まで起きない。

"Once he falls asleep, he won't wake until morning."

  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • は = topic marker
  • 寝た（ねた）= fell asleep
  • が最後（がさいご）= once
  • 朝まで（あさまで）= until morning
  • 起きない（おきない）= won't wake up

一度うそをついたが最後、二度と信じてもらえない。

"Once you tell a lie, nobody will ever trust you again."

  • 一度（いちど）= once
  • うそをついた = told a lie
  • が最後（がさいご）= once
  • 二度と（にどと）= ever again
  • 信じてもらえない（しんじてもらえない）= won't be believed

How to form it

Verb た-form + が最後（たら最後）

  • 見つかる（みつかる）= to be found → 見つかったが最後（みつかったがさいご）= once (you're) found
  • 始める（はじめる）= to start → 始めたが最後（はじめたがさいご）= once (he) starts
  • 言う（いう）= to say → 言ったら最後（いったらさいご）= once (you) say it

The casual variant is ～たら最後.

Key idea

～が最後 marks a point of no return: once it happens, the outcome is locked in.
