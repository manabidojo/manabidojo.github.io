～に従って: as (one change follows another) / in accordance with

従う means "to follow". ～に従って has two uses: one change following another, and acting according to a rule or instruction.

In English the first is "as...", as in "as you get older"; the second is "following" or "in accordance with".

1. One change follows another

As the first thing changes, the second changes along with it.

年を取るに従って、体力が落ちる。

"As you get older, your stamina drops."

  • 年を取る（としをとる）= to get older
  • に従って（にしたがって）= as
  • 体力（たいりょく）= stamina
  • が = subject marker
  • 落ちる（おちる）= to drop

2. Following instructions

After a noun like 指示 or ルール, it means acting according to it.

係員の指示に従って、避難してください。

"Please evacuate following the staff's instructions."

  • 係員（かかりいん）= staff
  • の = ('s)
  • 指示（しじ）= instructions
  • に従って（にしたがって）= following
  • 避難して（ひなんして）= evacuate
  • ください = please

～に従って vs ～につれて

For the "as" meaning, ～につれて is very close: 年を取るにつれて works just as well. For following a rule or an instruction, only ～に従って works.

How to form it

Verb dictionary form / noun + に従って

  • 進む（すすむ）= to advance → 進むに従って（すすむにしたがって）= as (it) advances
  • 上がる（あがる）= to rise → 上がるに従って（あがるにしたがって）= as (it) rises
  • 規則（きそく）= rules → 規則に従って（きそくにしたがって）= following the rules

Key idea

～に従って means following: either a change that follows another change, or behavior that follows a rule.
