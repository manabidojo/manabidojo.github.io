～てならない: can't help feeling

～てならない after the て-form of an emotion or a verb like 思える says that a feeling arises naturally and strongly, beyond the speaker's control.

In English it is "can't help feeling" or "terribly". It is more formal than ～てたまらない.

1. A strong, uncontrollable feeling

It usually describes worry, sadness or regret.

将来が不安でならない。

"I can't help being anxious about the future."

  • 将来（しょうらい）= the future
  • が = subject marker
  • 不安で（ふあんで）= anxious (and)
  • ならない = can't help it

2. A persistent thought

With 思える or 気がする, it expresses an impression that won't go away.

彼が嘘をついているように思えてならない。

"I can't shake the feeling that he's lying."

  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • が = subject marker
  • 嘘をついている（うそをついている）= is lying
  • ように = as if
  • 思えて（おもえて）= seeming
  • ならない = can't help it

How to form it

い-adjective (drop い) + くてならない, な-adjective + でならない, Verb て-form + ならない

  • 悔しい（くやしい）= frustrating → 悔しくてならない（くやしくてならない）= terribly frustrated
  • 心配（しんぱい）= worried → 心配でならない（しんぱいでならない）= terribly worried
  • 気になる（きになる）= to be on one's mind → 気になってならない（きになってならない）= can't stop thinking about it

It is used for feelings and impressions, not for physical states like heat or hunger.

Key idea

～てならない describes a feeling that keeps rising on its own: the speaker can't stop it.
