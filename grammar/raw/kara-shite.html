～からして: judging even from / starting with

～からして after a noun picks one detail, often the first or most basic one, and says that even this already shows the overall picture.

In English it is "to begin with", "just from" or "even (the very)...". It is often critical.

1. Even the most basic detail

The detail named is enough to see what the whole thing is like.

彼は話し方からして偉そうだ。

"Even the way he talks is arrogant."

  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • は = topic marker
  • 話し方（はなしかた）= way of talking
  • からして = even, to begin with
  • 偉そう（えらそう）= arrogant

2. Judging from a clue

It also judges something from a clue.

この店は名前からして高そうだ。

"Just from the name, this shop looks expensive."

  • この = this
  • 店（みせ）= shop
  • は = topic marker
  • 名前（なまえ）= name
  • からして = just from
  • 高そう（たかそう）= looks expensive

How to form it

Noun + からして

  • タイトル = title → タイトルからして = just from the title
  • 顔（かお）= face → 顔からして（かおからして）= even (his) face
  • 態度（たいど）= attitude → 態度からして（たいどからして）= judging from (the) attitude

Key idea

～からして points at one detail and says: you can already tell from this.
