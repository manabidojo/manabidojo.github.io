～やら～やら: what with A and B

AやらBやら lists examples, usually of things that piled up and made a situation hectic or confusing.

In English it is "what with A and B" or "A and B and all sorts of things". It often comes with a feeling of being overwhelmed.

1. A busy, chaotic situation

The items listed are reasons why the speaker was busy or tired.

掃除やら洗濯やらで忙しかった。

"What with cleaning and laundry, I was busy."

  • 掃除（そうじ）= cleaning
  • やら = and (among other things)
  • 洗濯（せんたく）= laundry
  • やら = and so on
  • で = because of
  • 忙しかった（いそがしかった）= was busy

2. Mixed feelings

With adjectives, it describes several feelings at once.

嬉しいやら恥ずかしいやらで、顔が赤くなった。

"Half happy, half embarrassed, I turned red."

  • 嬉しい（うれしい）= happy
  • やら = and
  • 恥ずかしい（はずかしい）= embarrassed
  • やら = and
  • で = because of
  • 顔（かお）= face
  • 赤くなった（あかくなった）= turned red

How to form it

Noun / plain form + やら + noun / plain form + やら

  • 本（ほん）= books → 本やら服やら（ほんやらふくやら）= books, clothes and stuff
  • 泣く（なく）= to cry → 泣くやら笑うやら（なくやらわらうやら）= crying and laughing
  • 痛い（いたい）= painful → 痛いやら悔しいやら（いたいやらくやしいやら）= painful and frustrating

Key idea

～やら～やら throws together a messy list: so many things at once that it was hard to cope.
