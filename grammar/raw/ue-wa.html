～上は: now that (I've committed)

～上は after a verb in the た-form or dictionary form says that since a situation has arisen or a decision has been made, the speaker must act accordingly.

In English it is "now that..." or "since...". It is formal, close to ～からには and ～以上は.

1. Following through on a commitment

Having accepted something, the speaker vows to see it through.

引き受けた上は、最後まで責任を持つ。

"Now that I've taken it on, I'll take responsibility to the end."

  • 引き受けた（ひきうけた）= took on
  • 上は（うえは）= now that
  • 最後まで（さいごまで）= to the end
  • 責任を持つ（せきにんをもつ）= take responsibility

2. Accepting a decision

It also says that once something is decided, there's no turning back.

こうなった上は、やるしかない。

"Now that it's come to this, we just have to do it."

  • こうなった = it's come to this
  • 上は（うえは）= now that
  • やる = to do
  • しかない = there's no choice but

How to form it

Verb た-form / dictionary form + 上は

  • 決めた（きめた）= decided → 決めた上は（きめたうえは）= now that (I've) decided
  • 約束した（やくそくした）= promised → 約束した上は（やくそくしたうえは）= now that (I've) promised
  • 契約する（けいやくする）= to sign a contract → 契約する上は（けいやくするうえは）= since (we're) signing a contract

Key idea

～上は says that a commitment has been made and must now be honored.
