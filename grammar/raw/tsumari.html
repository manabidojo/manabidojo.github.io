つまり: in other words / so

つまり restates or sums up what was said in simpler or shorter words.

In English it is "in other words", "so" or "that is to say".

1. Summing up

It often leads to ～ということだ, spelling out the conclusion.

つまり、君は行かないということだね。

"So in other words, you're not going."

  • つまり = in other words
  • 君（きみ）= you
  • は = topic marker
  • 行かない（いかない）= won't go
  • ということだね = that means, right?

2. Explaining a term

It also rephrases a word or fact in a clearer way.

彼は母の兄、つまり私のおじだ。

"He's my mother's older brother, in other words my uncle."

  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • 母（はは）= mother
  • の = ('s)
  • 兄（あに）= older brother
  • つまり = in other words
  • おじ = uncle

How to form it

Statement. つまり、restatement. つまり never changes form.

  • 来週の月曜日（らいしゅうのげつようび）= Monday next week → つまり、三日後だ（つまり、みっかごだ）= in other words, three days from now
  • 母の妹（ははのいもうと）= my mother's younger sister → つまり、おばだ = in other words, my aunt
  • 誰も来ない（だれもこない）= nobody is coming → つまり、中止だ（つまり、ちゅうしだ）= so it's canceled

Key idea

つまり says "let me put that another way": it squeezes what came before into one clear point.
