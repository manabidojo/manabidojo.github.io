～を込めて: putting one's heart or feelings into an action

～を込めて takes a noun for a feeling like 心 (heart), 愛 (love), 感謝 (gratitude) and says that the action is done with that feeling poured into it. It is the て-form of the verb 込める（こめる）, "to put into, to load".

English equivalents are "with all one's heart" or "filled with (love, gratitude)". It is common in letters and on gifts: 愛を込めて = "with love".

Doing something with feeling

Place feeling + を込めて before the verb to show the action was done sincerely and carefully.

心を込めて作る。

"I make it with all my heart."

  • 心（こころ）= heart
  • を = object marker
  • 込めて（こめて）= putting into (て-form of 込める)
  • 作る（つくる）= to make

感謝の気持ちを込めて、手紙を書いた。

"I wrote a letter filled with gratitude."

  • 感謝（かんしゃ）= gratitude
  • の = linking marker (gratitude's feeling = feeling of gratitude)
  • 気持ち（きもち）= feeling
  • を = object marker
  • 込めて（こめて）= putting into
  • 手紙（てがみ）= letter
  • 書いた（かいた）= wrote

How to form it

Noun (feeling) + を込めて + verb

  • 心（こころ）= heart → 心を込めて（こころをこめて）= with all one's heart
  • 愛（あい）= love → 愛を込めて（あいをこめて）= with love
  • 感謝（かんしゃ）= gratitude → 感謝を込めて（かんしゃをこめて）= with gratitude

Key idea

～を込めて literally means "loading" a feeling into an action. Whenever you want to say that something was done not just mechanically but with real emotion behind it, name the feeling and add を込めて.
