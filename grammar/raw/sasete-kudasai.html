～させてください: please let me do it

The causative form ("make / let someone do") plus ください asks for permission to do something yourself.

In English it is "please let me...". It can be eager (volunteering for a task) or formal (asking permission).

1. Volunteering for something

The speaker asks to be allowed to take on a task.

私にやらせてください。

"Please let me do it."

  • 私（わたし）= me
  • に = (let) me
  • やらせて = let (me) do
  • ください = please

2. Asking permission politely

It also asks for permission in formal situations, such as thinking something over or leaving early.

少し考えさせてください。

"Please let me think about it a little."

  • 少し（すこし）= a little
  • 考えさせて（かんがえさせて）= let (me) think
  • ください = please

How to form it

Verb causative て-form + ください

  • 休む（やすむ）= to rest → 休ませてください（やすませてください）= please let me rest
  • 帰る（かえる）= to go home → 帰らせてください（かえらせてください）= please let me go home
  • 説明する（せつめいする）= to explain → 説明させてください（せつめいさせてください）= please let me explain

The causative: u-verbs end in ～せる (休ませる), ru-verbs in ～させる (考えさせる), する becomes させる and 来る becomes 来させる.

Key idea

～させてください asks for permission to act. The causative "let" points back at the speaker.
