～てやる: doing for someone lower / I'll show you

やる is a rough word for "to give" or "to do". After a て-form it has two uses: doing a favor for someone lower, such as a child or an animal, or announcing a strong, often defiant intention.

The second use feels like English "I'll show you" or "just watch me". It is very common in anime and dramas.

1. A determined or defiant intention

The speaker declares that they will do something, often against someone or something.

今度こそ勝ってやる！

"This time I'm going to win, just watch!"

  • 今度（こんど）= this time
  • こそ = for sure
  • 勝ってやる（かってやる）= I'll win (you'll see)

2. A favor for someone lower

Toward children, younger siblings, animals or plants, it is the rough version of ～てあげる.

弟の宿題を手伝ってやった。

"I helped my little brother with his homework."

  • 弟（おとうと）= younger brother
  • の = ('s)
  • 宿題（しゅくだい）= homework
  • を = object marker
  • 手伝ってやった（てつだってやった）= helped (him)

How to form it

Verb て-form + やる

  • 見る（みる）= to look → 見てやる（みてやる）= I'll take a look
  • 教える（おしえる）= to teach → 教えてやる（おしえてやる）= I'll teach you (rough)
  • 遊ぶ（あそぶ）= to play → 遊んでやる（あそんでやる）= play with (a child)

Threats often use it too: 殴ってやる (I'll punch you). Because it sounds rough, it is best kept for casual speech.

Key idea

～てやる is ～てあげる with an attitude: a favor done downward, or a promise made with force.
