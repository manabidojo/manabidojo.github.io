～かける: halfway done, or on the verge of happening

～かける attaches to the ます-stem of a verb and means the action started but was not finished, or was just about to happen: 食べかける "start eating (and stop)", 死にかける "nearly die".

It is the mirror image of ～切る (do completely): ～かける stops the action partway, or even right before it begins. The noun form ～かけ, as in 食べかけのパン "a half-eaten piece of bread", describes things left in that unfinished state.

1. On the verge of happening

With verbs like 死ぬ, ～かける means the event very nearly happened but did not.

彼はおぼれて死にかけた。

"He nearly drowned."

  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • は = topic marker
  • おぼれて = drowning (て-form)
  • 死にかけた（しにかけた）= nearly died

2. Started but left unfinished

With everyday actions, ～かける（or the noun form ～かけの + noun） means the action began and then stopped partway.

テーブルの上に食べかけのパンが置いてある。

"There is a half-eaten piece of bread on the table."

  • テーブル = table
  • の上に（のうえに）= on top of
  • 食べかけ（たべかけ）= half-eaten
  • の = noun-linking particle
  • パン = bread
  • が = subject marker
  • 置いてある（おいてある）= has been left there

How to form it

Verb ます-stem + かける（noun form: ます-stem + かけ）

  • 食べる（たべる）= to eat → 食べかける（たべかける）= start eating and stop
  • 死ぬ（しぬ）= to die → 死にかける（しにかける）= nearly die
  • 読む（よむ）= to read → 読みかけの本（よみかけのほん）= a book one has started but not finished

Key idea

～かける freezes an action partway: it got going, or was a hair's breadth from happening, but never reached the end.
