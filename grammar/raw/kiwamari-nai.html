～極まりない／～極まる: extremely (to the limit)

極まる means "to reach the limit". After a な-adjective, ～極まりない and ～極まる both mean "extremely", usually about something negative.

In English it is "extremely", "utterly" or "in the extreme". It is formal and strong.

1. An extreme negative quality

It often criticizes behavior or situations.

彼の態度は失礼極まりない。

"His attitude is extremely rude."

  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • の = ('s)
  • 態度（たいど）= attitude
  • は = topic marker
  • 失礼（しつれい）= rude
  • 極まりない（きわまりない）= extremely

2. In formal writing

極まる has the same meaning and appears in set phrases.

その運転は危険極まる。

"That driving is extremely dangerous."

  • その = that
  • 運転（うんてん）= driving
  • は = topic marker
  • 危険（きけん）= dangerous
  • 極まる（きわまる）= to the extreme

How to form it

な-adjective (without な) + 極まりない／極まる

  • 不便（ふべん）= inconvenient → 不便極まりない（ふべんきわまりない）= extremely inconvenient
  • 無礼（ぶれい）= rude → 無礼極まりない（ぶれいきわまりない）= utterly rude
  • 退屈（たいくつ）= boring → 退屈極まりない（たいくつきわまりない）= extremely boring

Although 極まりない looks like a negative, it means the same as 極まる: "to the extreme".

Key idea

～極まりない pushes a quality to its limit, usually to criticize it.
