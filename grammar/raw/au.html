～合う: doing something to each other

合う means "to fit, to match". After a verb's ます-stem, ～合う says that people do the action to each other.

In English it is "each other" or "mutually": 助け合う = help each other.

Two-way actions

Two or more people do the same action toward one another, which makes it common when talking about cooperation and relationships.

二人は顔を見合って笑った。

"The two looked at each other and laughed."

  • 二人（ふたり）= the two (people)
  • は = topic marker
  • 顔（かお）= face
  • を = object marker
  • 見合って（みあって）= looking at each other
  • 笑った（わらった）= laughed

困った時は、みんなで助け合おう。

"When times are hard, let's all help each other."

  • 困った時（こまったとき）= when in trouble
  • みんなで = everyone together
  • 助け合おう（たすけあおう）= let's help each other

How to form it

Verb ます-stem + 合う

  • 話す（はなす）= to talk → 話し合う（はなしあう）= discuss together
  • 愛する（あいする）= to love → 愛し合う（あいしあう）= love each other
  • 教える（おしえる）= to teach → 教え合う（おしえあう）= teach each other

Key idea

～合う turns an action into a two-way one: both sides do it to each other.
