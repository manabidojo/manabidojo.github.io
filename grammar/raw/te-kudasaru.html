～てくださる: someone respected does something for me

くださる is the honorific form of くれる. After a て-form it says that a superior kindly did something for the speaker or the speaker's side.

In English it is close to "kindly did (for me)". The favor comes from above, and the verb shows respect for the giver.

1. Thanking someone above you

Typical givers are teachers, bosses, customers or older people.

先生が丁寧に説明してくださった。

"The teacher kindly explained it carefully."

  • 先生（せんせい）= teacher
  • が = subject marker
  • 丁寧に（ていねいに）= carefully
  • 説明して（せつめいして）= explain
  • くださった = kindly did (for me)

2. Very polite requests

The request form ～てください comes from this verb. As a question, ～てくださいませんか is a very polite request.

もう一度言ってくださいませんか。

"Could you please say it once more?"

  • もう一度（もういちど）= once more
  • 言って（いって）= say
  • くださいませんか = could you kindly

How to form it

Verb て-form + くださる

  • 来る（くる）= to come → 来てくださる（きてくださる）= kindly come
  • 待つ（まつ）= to wait → 待ってくださった（まってくださった）= kindly waited
  • 手伝う（てつだう）= to help → 手伝ってくださる（てつだってくださる）= kindly help

The ます-form is irregular: くださいます, not くださります.

Key idea

～てくださる is ～てくれる with respect. The favor comes from someone above toward the speaker.
