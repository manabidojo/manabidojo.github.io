～に基づいて: based on

～に基づいて（にもとづいて）comes from the verb 基づく (もとづく, to be based on) and attaches to a noun to give the basis or foundation for an action or judgment. It is formed as Noun + に基づいて.

It corresponds to English "based on" or "in accordance with".

1. Acting or judging on a basis

Use ～に基づいて before a verb to say that the action follows from facts, data, rules, or experience.

事実に基づいて判断します。

"Make a judgment based on the facts."

  • 事実（じじつ）= fact(s)
  • に基づいて（にもとづいて）= based on
  • 判断します（はんだんします）= judge, make a judgment

2. ～に基づいた + noun

To describe a noun, use ～に基づいた + noun (or に基づく + noun), meaning "a ... based on ...".

経験に基づいたアドバイスをもらいました。

"I received advice based on experience."

  • 経験（けいけん）= experience
  • に基づいた（にもとづいた）= based on
  • アドバイス = advice
  • を = object marker
  • もらいました = received

How to form it

Noun + に基づいて; before a noun, use Noun + に基づいた + noun

  • 事実（じじつ）= fact → 事実に基づいて（じじつにもとづいて）= based on the facts
  • 計画（けいかく）= plan → 計画に基づいて（けいかくにもとづいて）= based on the plan
  • データ = data → データに基づいた判断（データにもとづいたはんだん）= a judgment based on data

Key idea

～に基づいて names the foundation: the action or judgment that follows is built on top of the facts, rules, or experience that come before it.
