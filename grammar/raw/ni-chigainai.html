～に違いない: it must be / there's no doubt

～に違いない（～にちがいない）expresses strong certainty: the speaker has not confirmed the fact directly, but the evidence points clearly to one conclusion. It attaches to the plain form of a verb or い-adjective, and directly to a noun or な-adjective stem (no だ in between).

It maps closely to English "must be" or "no doubt". Literally, 違いない means "there is no mistake (that ...)".

Confident conclusions from evidence

Use ～に違いない when the situation lets you draw only one reasonable conclusion.

彼は犯人に違いない。

"He must be the culprit."

  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • は = topic marker
  • 犯人（はんにん）= culprit
  • に違いない（にちがいない）= must be, no doubt

電気がついているから、誰か家にいるに違いない。

"The lights are on, so someone must be home."

  • 電気（でんき）= (electric) light
  • が = subject marker
  • ついている = is on
  • から = because, so
  • 誰か（だれか）= someone
  • 家（いえ）= home
  • に = location marker
  • いる = to be (somewhere)
  • に違いない（にちがいない）= must be

How to form it

Plain form + に違いない — nouns and な-adjectives attach directly, without だ.

  • 犯人（はんにん）= culprit → 犯人に違いない（はんにんにちがいない）= must be the culprit
  • 行く（いく）= to go → 行くに違いない（いくにちがいない）= must be going
  • 高い（たかい）= expensive → 高いに違いない（たかいにちがいない）= must be expensive

Key idea

～に違いない is a confident guess: you have not seen the fact with your own eyes, but the evidence leaves no room for doubt in your mind.
