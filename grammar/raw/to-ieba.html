～といえば／～というと: speaking of / when it comes to

～といえば and ～というと take a topic and bring up what comes to mind first, or pick up a topic someone just mentioned.

In English it is "speaking of...", "when it comes to..." or "when you think of...".

1. The first thing that comes to mind

The speaker names what is most typical of the topic.

日本といえば、富士山だ。

"When you think of Japan, you think of Mt. Fuji."

  • 日本（にほん）= Japan
  • といえば = when you think of
  • 富士山（ふじさん）= Mt. Fuji
  • だ = is

2. Picking up a topic

In conversation, it follows up on something just said.

旅行というと、来月京都に行くんだ。

"Speaking of trips, I'm going to Kyoto next month."

  • 旅行（りょこう）= trip
  • というと = speaking of
  • 来月（らいげつ）= next month
  • 京都（きょうと）= Kyoto
  • に = to
  • 行くんだ（いくんだ）= I'm going

How to form it

Noun + といえば／というと／といったら

  • 夏（なつ）= summer → 夏といえば（なつといえば）= when it comes to summer
  • 彼（かれ）= him → 彼というと（かれというと）= speaking of him
  • 北海道（ほっかいどう）= Hokkaido → 北海道といったら（ほっかいどうといったら）= when you think of Hokkaido

そういえば ("come to think of it") is a related set phrase that brings up something new.

Key idea

～といえば opens a topic and goes straight to what it calls to mind.
