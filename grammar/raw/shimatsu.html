～始末だ: ending up in a sorry state

始末 (しまつ) means "the way things turned out". ～始末だ describes a bad final result after a series of problems, often with criticism or exasperation.

In English it is "and in the end...", "and then, to top it off..." or "it got to the point where...".

A list of troubles with a bad ending

The speaker lists problems and ends with the worst one, often with criticism or exasperation. It can also stand alone as この始末だ, "look at this mess".

遅刻した上に、宿題まで忘れる始末だ。

"He was late, and then even forgot his homework."

  • 遅刻した（ちこくした）= was late
  • 上に（うえに）= on top of
  • 宿題（しゅくだい）= homework
  • まで = even
  • 忘れる（わすれる）= to forget
  • 始末だ（しまつだ）= it ended up like this

彼は酒を飲みすぎて、ついには倒れる始末だった。

"He drank too much, and in the end he collapsed."

  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • は = topic marker
  • 酒（さけ）= alcohol
  • 飲みすぎて（のみすぎて）= drank too much
  • ついには = in the end
  • 倒れる（たおれる）= to collapse
  • 始末だった（しまつだった）= it ended up like this

How to form it

Verb dictionary form + 始末だ, この／あの + 始末だ

  • 泣き出す（なきだす）= to burst into tears → 泣き出す始末だ（なきだすしまつだ）= it ended with (him) in tears
  • 借金する（しゃっきんする）= to borrow money → 借金する始末だ（しゃっきんするしまつだ）= it got to the point of borrowing money
  • この = this → この始末だ（このしまつだ）= look at this mess

Key idea

～始末だ ends a list of troubles with the worst outcome: look where it all led.
