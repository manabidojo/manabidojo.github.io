～に限って: of all times / only (when)

限る means "to limit". ～に限って focuses on one case and has two common uses: something bad happens exactly when it shouldn't, and a trusted person surely wouldn't do something.

In English it is "of all times", "just when" or "not him, of all people".

1. Bad luck at the worst moment

The speaker complains that trouble always comes at exactly that time.

急いでいる時に限って、電車が遅れる。

"The train is always late exactly when I'm in a hurry."

  • 急いでいる（いそいでいる）= am in a hurry
  • 時（とき）= when
  • に限って（にかぎって）= of all times
  • 電車（でんしゃ）= train
  • が = subject marker
  • 遅れる（おくれる）= to be late

2. Trust in someone

With a person, it says that they of all people wouldn't do it.

うちの子に限って、そんなことはしない。

"My child, of all people, would never do that."

  • うちの子（うちのこ）= my child
  • に限って（にかぎって）= of all people
  • そんなこと = such a thing
  • は = topic marker
  • しない = wouldn't do

How to form it

Noun + に限って

  • 雨の日（あめのひ）= rainy day → 雨の日に限って（あめのひにかぎって）= on rainy days of all days
  • 大事な時（だいじなとき）= important moment → 大事な時に限って（だいじなときにかぎって）= just when it matters
  • 彼（かれ）= him → 彼に限って（かれにかぎって）= him of all people

As a notice, 本日に限り means "today only".

Key idea

～に限って singles out one case: either the worst possible timing, or the person you'd least expect.
