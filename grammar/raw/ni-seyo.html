～にせよ／～にしろ: whether or not / even if

～にせよ and ～にしろ say that a conclusion holds in either case. Repeated, AにせよBにせよ means "whether A or B".

In English it is "whether... or...", "even if" or "either way".

1. Whatever the choice

With two options, it means the second clause applies to both.

行くにせよ行かないにせよ、連絡してください。

"Whether you go or not, let me know."

  • 行く（いく）= to go
  • にせよ = whether
  • 行かない（いかない）= not go
  • にせよ = or
  • 連絡して（れんらくして）= contact
  • ください = please

2. Even if

Used once, it concedes a point and then adds a contrasting statement.

冗談にしろ、言っていいことと悪いことがある。

"Even as a joke, some things just shouldn't be said."

  • 冗談（じょうだん）= joke
  • にしろ = even if
  • 言っていい（いっていい）= okay to say
  • ことと悪いこと（こととわるいこと）= things and bad things
  • がある = there are

How to form it

Plain form + にせよ／にしろ (nouns and な-adjectives drop だ or take である)

  • 忙しい（いそがしい）= busy → 忙しいにせよ（いそがしいにせよ）= even if (you're) busy
  • 賛成（さんせい）= for → 賛成にしろ反対にしろ（さんせいにしろはんたいにしろ）= whether for or against
  • 誰（だれ）= who → 誰がやるにせよ（だれがやるにせよ）= whoever does it

～にしても has the same meaning and is a bit more conversational.

Key idea

～にせよ says the outcome doesn't change: whichever case it is, this still holds.
