な-Adj+に: na-adjective adverbial

An na-adjective plus に turns the adjective into an adverb. Instead of describing a thing, it describes how an action is done. 静かだ (is quiet) becomes 静かに (quietly), and きれいだ (is clean) becomes きれいに (cleanly, neatly).

In English this usually matches the "-ly" ending: "quietly", "skillfully", "pessimistically".

1. Describing how an action is done

Put the na-adjective + に right before the verb it modifies.

図書館では静かに話してください。

"Please speak quietly in the library."

  • 図書館（としょかん）= library
  • で = at (place of action)
  • は = topic marker
  • 静かに（しずかに）= quietly
  • 話してください（はなしてください）= please speak

2. ～になる: "become ..."

The same に shape appears before なる (to become) to describe a change of state: 元気になる = "get well", "become healthy".

妹は元気になりました。

"My little sister got better."

  • 妹（いもうと）= younger sister
  • は = topic marker
  • 元気に（げんきに）= healthy, well
  • なりました = became

な-adjectives take に, い-adjectives take く

i-adjectives form their adverb differently: drop the final い and add く, as in 早い → 早く起きる (get up early).

How to form it

na-adjective + に

  • 静か（しずか）= quiet → 静かに（しずかに）= quietly
  • きれい = clean, pretty → きれいに = cleanly, neatly
  • 上手（じょうず）= skillful → 上手に（じょうずに）= skillfully

Key idea

に aims the quality at the verb instead of at a noun. If you would add "-ly" in English, an na-adjective takes に. Before なる it marks the state something turns into.
