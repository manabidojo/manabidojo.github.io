～を余儀なくされる: being forced to

余儀ない means "unavoidable". ～を余儀なくされる says that circumstances leave no choice but to do something, usually something undesirable.

In English it is "be forced to" or "have no choice but to". It is formal and common in news.

Forced by circumstances

The cause is usually an outside event, such as the weather, an injury or money problems, that leaves no other option.

大雨のため、試合は中止を余儀なくされた。

"Because of the heavy rain, the game had to be canceled."

  • 大雨（おおあめ）= heavy rain
  • のため = because of
  • 試合（しあい）= game
  • は = topic marker
  • 中止（ちゅうし）= cancellation
  • を余儀なくされた（をよぎなくされた）= was forced into

怪我で、彼は引退を余儀なくされた。

"Because of an injury, he was forced to retire."

  • 怪我（けが）= injury
  • で = because of
  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • は = topic marker
  • 引退（いんたい）= retirement
  • を余儀なくされた（をよぎなくされた）= was forced into

How to form it

Noun (an action) + を余儀なくされる

  • 延期（えんき）= postponement → 延期を余儀なくされる（えんきをよぎなくされる）= be forced to postpone
  • 変更（へんこう）= change → 変更を余儀なくされる（へんこうをよぎなくされる）= be forced to change
  • 入院（にゅういん）= hospitalization → 入院を余儀なくされる（にゅういんをよぎなくされる）= be forced to go into hospital

The active form ～を余儀なくさせる means "to force (someone) to".

Key idea

～を余儀なくされる says that the choice was taken away: circumstances decided.
