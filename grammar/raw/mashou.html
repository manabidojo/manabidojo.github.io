～ましょう: let's (polite suggestion)

～ましょう replaces the ます of a polite verb and turns it into a suggestion to do something together. It is the polite form of the volitional (行こう).

In English it is "let's". Compared with ～ませんか, it is more decided: the speaker assumes the other person will agree.

1. Suggesting something together

Use ～ましょう for plans that are already agreed on, or for the obvious next step.

そろそろ帰りましょう。

"Let's head home soon."

  • そろそろ = soon, it's about time
  • 帰りましょう（かえりましょう）= let's go home

2. Accepting an invitation

When someone invites you with ～ませんか, ～ましょう is a friendly way to say yes.

いいですね。行きましょう。

"Sounds good. Let's go."

  • いいですね = sounds good
  • 行きましょう（いきましょう）= let's go

How to form it

Verb ます-stem + ましょう

  • 始める（はじめる）= to begin → 始めましょう（はじめましょう）= let's begin
  • 待つ（まつ）= to wait → 待ちましょう（まちましょう）= let's wait
  • 勉強する（べんきょうする）= to study → 勉強しましょう（べんきょうしましょう）= let's study

Key idea

～ましょう is a polite "let's". It proposes a joint action and expects a yes; for a softer invitation, ask with ～ませんか instead.
