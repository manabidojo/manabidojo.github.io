～に比べて: compared to

比べる means "to compare". ～に比べて marks the thing that something else is compared against.

In English it is "compared to" or "compared with". It often describes a change or a difference.

1. Comparing two things

The standard of comparison goes before に比べて.

去年に比べて、今年は暑い。

"This year is hotter than last year."

  • 去年（きょねん）= last year
  • に比べて（にくらべて）= compared to
  • 今年（ことし）= this year
  • は = topic marker
  • 暑い（あつい）= hot

2. Describing a difference

It also states how big a difference is.

都会に比べて、田舎は家賃が安い。

"Compared to the city, rent in the countryside is cheap."

  • 都会（とかい）= city
  • に比べて（にくらべて）= compared to
  • 田舎（いなか）= countryside
  • 家賃（やちん）= rent
  • 安い（やすい）= cheap

How to form it

Noun + に比べて／に比べると

  • 昔（むかし）= the past → 昔に比べて（むかしにくらべて）= compared to the old days
  • 兄（あに）= older brother → 兄に比べて（あににくらべて）= compared to my brother
  • 先月（せんげつ）= last month → 先月に比べると（せんげつにくらべると）= compared to last month

Key idea

～に比べて names what you measure against and then states the difference.
