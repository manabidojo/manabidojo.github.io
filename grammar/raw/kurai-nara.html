～くらいなら: rather than (I'd prefer)

～くらいなら says that rather than doing something unpleasant, the speaker would prefer another option, even an extreme one.

In English it is "rather than...", "if it comes to..., I'd rather...". The second clause often contains ほうがいい or ～ない.

Anything but that

The first option is so bad that anything else seems better. The second clause often contains ほうがいい or a command.

謝るくらいなら、最初からするな。

"If you're going to apologize, don't do it in the first place."

  • 謝る（あやまる）= to apologize
  • くらいなら = if (you're going) to
  • 最初から（さいしょから）= from the start
  • するな = don't do it

あの人に頼むくらいなら、自分でやったほうがいい。

"Rather than ask that guy, I'd be better off doing it myself."

  • あの人（あのひと）= that person
  • に = (ask) of
  • 頼む（たのむ）= to ask
  • くらいなら = rather than
  • 自分で（じぶんで）= by myself
  • やったほうがいい = better do it

How to form it

Verb dictionary form + くらいなら／ぐらいなら

  • 負ける（まける）= to lose → 負けるくらいなら（まけるくらいなら）= rather than lose
  • 待つ（まつ）= to wait → 待つくらいなら（まつくらいなら）= rather than wait
  • 後悔する（こうかいする）= to regret → 後悔するくらいなら（こうかいするくらいなら）= rather than regret it

Key idea

～くらいなら names the option the speaker dislikes most and reaches for anything else instead.
