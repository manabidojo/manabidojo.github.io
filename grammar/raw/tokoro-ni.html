～ところに／～ところへ: just as (at that moment)

ところ means "point, moment". With に or へ, ～ところに says that something happened at exactly the moment of another situation, often interrupting it.

In English it is "just as..." or "right when...".

1. An interruption

The first clause sets the scene, and something unexpected arrives at that moment.

出かけようとしたところに、電話がかかってきた。

"Just as I was about to leave, the phone rang."

  • 出かけよう（でかけよう）= (about) to go out
  • とした = tried to
  • ところに = just as
  • 電話（でんわ）= phone
  • がかかってきた = rang

2. Good timing

It can also describe someone arriving at a convenient moment.

ちょうどいいところへ来たね。手伝って。

"You came at just the right time. Give me a hand."

  • ちょうど = exactly
  • いい = good
  • ところへ = at the moment
  • 来た（きた）= came
  • ね = right?
  • 手伝って（てつだって）= help

How to form it

Verb (ている / た / volitional + とする) + ところに／ところへ

  • 寝ようとする（ねようとする）= about to sleep → 寝ようとしたところに（ねようとしたところに）= just as (I) was about to sleep
  • 食べている（たべている）= eating → 食べているところへ（たべているところへ）= right while (I) was eating
  • 帰った（かえった）= got home → 帰ったところに（かえったところに）= just as (I) got home

Key idea

～ところに freezes one moment and says what came in right then.
