～に反して: contrary to / against

反する means "to go against". ～に反して says that a result is the opposite of expectations, or that an action goes against rules or wishes.

In English it is "contrary to" or "against".

1. Against expectations

With 予想 or 期待, it introduces a surprising result.

予想に反して、試合に負けた。

"Contrary to expectations, we lost the game."

  • 予想（よそう）= expectations
  • に反して（にはんして）= contrary to
  • 試合（しあい）= game
  • に = (the game)
  • 負けた（まけた）= lost

2. Against someone's wishes or rules

It also describes acting against a rule or someone's will.

親の意思に反して、彼は大学をやめた。

"Against his parents' wishes, he dropped out of university."

  • 親（おや）= parents
  • の = ('s)
  • 意思（いし）= wishes, will
  • に反して（にはんして）= against
  • 大学（だいがく）= university
  • やめた = quit

How to form it

Noun + に反して (before a noun: に反する + noun)

  • 期待（きたい）= expectations → 期待に反して（きたいにはんして）= contrary to expectations
  • 法律（ほうりつ）= the law → 法律に反する（ほうりつにはんする）= against the law
  • 規則（きそく）= rules → 規則に反して（きそくにはんして）= against the rules

Key idea

～に反して sets the result against what was expected or allowed: it went the other way.
