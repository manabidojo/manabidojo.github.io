～にかけて: over a span of time or place

～にかけて marks the end of a span of time or space, usually together with ～から. It covers the whole range loosely, without exact borders.

In English it is "from... through..." or "across".

1. A span of time

It is common in weather forecasts and schedules.

夜から朝にかけて雨が降るでしょう。

"It will rain from tonight through the morning."

  • 夜（よる）= night
  • から = from
  • 朝（あさ）= morning
  • にかけて = through
  • 雨（あめ）= rain
  • が = subject marker
  • 降るでしょう（ふるでしょう）= will probably fall

2. An area

It also describes an area that stretches from one place to another.

関東から東北にかけて、大雪になった。

"Heavy snow fell across the region from Kanto to Tohoku."

  • 関東（かんとう）= Kanto region
  • から = from
  • 東北（とうほく）= Tohoku region
  • にかけて = through
  • 大雪（おおゆき）= heavy snow
  • になった = became

How to form it

Noun A + から + noun B + にかけて

  • 春（はる）= spring → 春から夏にかけて（はるからなつにかけて）= from spring into summer
  • 月曜（げつよう）= Monday → 月曜から水曜にかけて（げつようからすいようにかけて）= from Monday through Wednesday
  • 首（くび）= neck → 首から肩にかけて（くびからかたにかけて）= from the neck to the shoulders

Compared with から～まで, which sets exact limits, ～にかけて is looser: it means "roughly over that range".

Key idea

～にかけて stretches something loosely across a range of time or space.
