お／ご～する: humble form of an action

お～する is the humble partner of お～になる. The speaker lowers their own action toward someone, which shows respect for that person.

In English it is close to "allow me to..." or "I will (gladly)...". It belongs to 謙譲語, the humble language, and is only used for what you do for others.

1. Offering to do something

It is the standard way for staff and juniors to describe what they will do for someone.

駅までお送りします。

"I'll take you to the station."

  • 駅（えき）= station
  • まで = to, as far as
  • お送りします（おおくりします）= see (you) off (humble)

2. With Sino-Japanese verbs

する-verbs built from Chinese words take ご instead of お.

後でご連絡します。

"I'll contact you later."

  • 後で（あとで）= later
  • ご連絡します（ごれんらくします）= contact (you) (humble)

How to form it

お + verb ます-stem + する, ご + Sino-Japanese noun + する

  • 待つ（まつ）= to wait → お待ちします（おまちします）= I'll wait
  • 知らせる（しらせる）= to inform → お知らせします（おしらせします）= I'll let you know
  • 案内する（あんないする）= to guide → ご案内します（ごあんないします）= I'll show you the way

お～いたす is even humbler: お待ちいたします.

Key idea

お～する lowers the speaker's own action. It is only for what you do for others, never for what they do.
