～くらい: about this much, to this extent

～くらい (also pronounced ぐらい) marks a point on a scale. After a number it means "about, approximately"; after a clause it means "to the extent that ~", like English "so ~ that ~".

1. Approximate amount

After a quantity like time, money, distance, people, くらい softens it into a rough estimate.

駅まで十分くらいかかります。

"It takes about ten minutes to the station."

  • 駅（えき）= station
  • まで = to, as far as
  • 十分（じゅっぷん）= ten minutes
  • くらい = about, approximately
  • かかります = takes (time)

2. Extent or degree

After a clause, くらい gives a vivid measure of how strong something is: the feeling or situation reaches the level described in the clause.

泣きたいくらい嬉しかった。

"I was so happy I could have cried."

  • 泣きたい（なきたい）= want to cry (泣く plus ～たい)
  • くらい = to the extent that
  • 嬉しかった（うれしかった）= was happy

How to form it

Number / noun + くらい, or plain-form clause + くらい

  • 三十分（さんじゅっぷん）= thirty minutes → 三十分くらい = about thirty minutes
  • これ = this → これくらい = about this much
  • 泣きたい（なきたい）= want to cry → 泣きたいくらい = so much that one could cry

Key idea

くらい answers the question "how much?" with a rough landmark, either a round number "about ten minutes" or a concrete image of how far something goes "enough to make me cry".
