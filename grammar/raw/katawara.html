～かたわら: while also (doing on the side)

かたわら means "beside". ～かたわら says that someone does a main activity and, alongside it, another one, usually over a long period.

In English it is "while also..." or "alongside...". It describes people with a main job and a side activity.

A main activity and a side one

The first activity is the main one, and the second is kept up alongside it over a long period, like writing or studying next to a job.

彼は会社で働くかたわら、小説を書いている。

"While working at a company, he also writes novels."

  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • は = topic marker
  • 会社（かいしゃ）= company
  • で = at
  • 働く（はたらく）= to work
  • かたわら = while also
  • 小説（しょうせつ）= novels
  • を = object marker
  • 書いている（かいている）= writes

彼女は子育てのかたわら、大学で勉強している。

"Alongside raising her children, she studies at university."

  • 彼女（かのじょ）= she
  • は = topic marker
  • 子育て（こそだて）= raising children
  • のかたわら = alongside
  • 大学（だいがく）= university
  • で = at
  • 勉強している（べんきょうしている）= is studying

How to form it

Verb dictionary form + かたわら, Noun + のかたわら

  • 教える（おしえる）= to teach → 教えるかたわら（おしえるかたわら）= while teaching
  • 仕事（しごと）= work → 仕事のかたわら（しごとのかたわら）= alongside work
  • 研究する（けんきゅうする）= to research → 研究するかたわら（けんきゅうするかたわら）= while doing research

Unlike ～ながら, which describes two things at the same moment, ～かたわら describes two activities running in parallel over time.

Key idea

～かたわら describes a double life: a main activity, and another one kept up beside it.
