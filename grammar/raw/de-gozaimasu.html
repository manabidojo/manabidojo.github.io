～でございます: very polite form of です

でございます is the most polite way to say です. Shop staff, hotel staff and announcers use it, as do butlers and very formal characters in anime.

There is no English equivalent; it works like a very respectful "it is". ございます alone is the polite form of あります ("there is").

1. Customer service

Staff use it to describe things and places to customers.

こちらがお部屋でございます。

"This is your room."

  • こちら = this (polite)
  • が = subject marker
  • お部屋（おへや）= room (polite)
  • でございます = is (very polite)

2. Introducing yourself formally

In formal greetings and phone calls, it raises the whole sentence.

担当の田中でございます。

"I'm Tanaka, the person in charge."

  • 担当（たんとう）= person in charge
  • の = (who is)
  • 田中（たなか）= Tanaka
  • でございます = am (very polite)

How to form it

Noun / な-adjective + でございます

  • 本（ほん）= book → 本でございます（ほんでございます）= it is a book
  • 結構（けっこう）= fine → 結構でございます（けっこうでございます）= that is fine
  • あります = there is → ございます = there is (very polite)

お手洗いはあちらにございます (the restroom is over there) uses ございます in place of あります.

Key idea

でございます is です at its most polite. When you hear it, you are usually the customer.
