～と思いきや: just when I thought (but actually)

～と思いきや says that the speaker expected one thing, but the opposite happened. It comes from an old form of 思う.

In English it is "just when I thought..." or "I expected..., but...". It is common in writing, blogs and narration, often with a humorous tone.

An expectation turned upside down

The expectation is set up and then overturned, in either direction: an unpleasant surprise or a pleasant one.

晴れると思いきや、急に雨が降り出した。

"Just when I thought it'd be sunny, it suddenly started raining."

  • 晴れる（はれる）= to be sunny
  • と思いきや（とおもいきや）= just when (I) thought
  • 急に（きゅうに）= suddenly
  • 雨（あめ）= rain
  • が = subject marker
  • 降り出した（ふりだした）= started falling

難しいと思いきや、意外と簡単だった。

"I expected it to be hard, but it was surprisingly easy."

  • 難しい（むずかしい）= difficult
  • と思いきや（とおもいきや）= I expected, but
  • 意外と（いがいと）= surprisingly
  • 簡単（かんたん）= easy
  • だった = was

How to form it

Plain form + と思いきや (nouns and な-adjectives often drop だ)

  • 勝つ（かつ）= to win → 勝つと思いきや（かつとおもいきや）= I thought (they'd) win, but
  • 終わった（おわった）= finished → 終わったと思いきや（おわったとおもいきや）= just when I thought it was over
  • 静か（しずか）= quiet → 静かかと思いきや（しずかかとおもいきや）= I expected it to be quiet, but

It describes the speaker's own expectation; the result is a fact they observed.

Key idea

～と思いきや sets up an expectation and immediately knocks it over.
