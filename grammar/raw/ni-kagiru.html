～に限る: nothing beats / it's best to

～に限る at the end of a sentence says that something is the best option, in the speaker's opinion.

In English it is "nothing beats..." or "the best thing is to...". It expresses a personal, confident preference.

1. The best thing to do

After a verb, it says what the best action is in a situation.

疲れた時は寝るに限る。

"When you're tired, nothing beats sleep."

  • 疲れた時（つかれたとき）= when tired
  • は = topic marker
  • 寝る（ねる）= to sleep
  • に限る（にかぎる）= is the best

2. The best choice

After a noun, it names the best thing of its kind.

夏はやっぱり冷たいビールに限る。

"In summer, nothing beats a cold beer."

  • 夏（なつ）= summer
  • は = topic marker
  • やっぱり = after all
  • 冷たい（つめたい）= cold
  • ビール = beer
  • に限る（にかぎる）= is the best

How to form it

Verb dictionary form / ない-form + に限る, Noun + に限る

  • 休む（やすむ）= to rest → 休むに限る（やすむにかぎる）= resting is best
  • 行かない（いかない）= not go → 行かないに限る（いかないにかぎる）= best not to go
  • 温泉（おんせん）= hot spring → 温泉に限る（おんせんにかぎる）= nothing beats a hot spring

Key idea

～に限る is a confident recommendation: in this case, nothing is better.
