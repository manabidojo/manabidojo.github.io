～ばよかった: I should have

～ばよかった combines the conditional ～ば with よかった ("it was good"). Literally "it would have been good if...", it expresses regret about something that didn't happen.

In English it is "I should have...". With a negative verb it becomes "I shouldn't have...".

1. Regretting what you didn't do

The speaker looks back at a better choice they didn't make.

もっと早く来ればよかった。

"I should have come earlier."

  • もっと = more
  • 早く（はやく）= early
  • 来れば（くれば）= if (I) had come
  • よかった = it would have been good

2. Regretting what you did

With the ない-form, ～なければよかった regrets something that was done.

あんなこと、言わなければよかった。

"I shouldn't have said that."

  • あんなこと = that kind of thing
  • 言わなければ（いわなければ）= if (I) hadn't said
  • よかった = it would have been good

How to form it

Verb ば-form + よかった

  • 買う（かう）= to buy → 買えばよかった（かえばよかった）= I should have bought it
  • 勉強する（べんきょうする）= to study → 勉強すればよかった（べんきょうすればよかった）= I should have studied
  • 行く（いく）= to go → 行かなければよかった（いかなければよかった）= I shouldn't have gone

～たらよかった means the same. Adding のに makes the regret stronger: 来ればよかったのに.

Key idea

～ばよかった is hindsight: it names the better choice that is no longer possible.
