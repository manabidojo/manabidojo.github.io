～とおり（に）: just as / exactly as

とおり means "the way" or "as". ～とおり（に） says that something is done or happens exactly as something else says or shows.

In English it is "as" or "just like", as in "do it as I said". After a noun it often becomes どおり.

1. Following instructions exactly

With の after a noun, or directly after a verb, it describes doing things exactly that way.

説明書のとおりに組み立てた。

"I assembled it as the manual said."

  • 説明書（せつめいしょ）= manual
  • のとおりに = as, following
  • 組み立てた（くみたてた）= assembled

2. As expected

After a verb like 思った, it says that things turned out exactly as imagined.

思ったとおり、彼は来なかった。

"Just as I thought, he didn't come."

  • 思った（おもった）= thought
  • とおり = just as
  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • 来なかった（こなかった）= didn't come

How to form it

Verb (dictionary / た-form) + とおり（に）, Noun + のとおり（に） or Noun + どおり（に）

  • 言う（いう）= to say → 言ったとおりに（いったとおりに）= as I said
  • 予定（よてい）= plan → 予定どおり（よていどおり）= as planned
  • 見る（みる）= to see → 見たとおり（みたとおり）= as you can see

Key idea

～とおり matches action to a model: exactly as said, written, planned or imagined.
