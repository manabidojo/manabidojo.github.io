～まみれ: covered in (something messy)

～まみれ after a noun says that something is completely covered in something dirty, sticky or unpleasant.

In English it is "covered in" or "smeared with". Typical nouns are 泥 (mud), 汗 (sweat), 血 (blood) and 埃 (dust).

Coated in something messy

It describes being covered all over in mud, sweat, blood, dust and the like.

子供たちは泥まみれになって遊んだ。

"The kids played until they were covered in mud."

  • 子供たち（こどもたち）= children
  • は = topic marker
  • 泥まみれ（どろまみれ）= covered in mud
  • になって = becoming
  • 遊んだ（あそんだ）= played

一日中働いて、汗まみれになった。

"After working all day, I was drenched in sweat."

  • 一日中（いちにちじゅう）= all day
  • 働いて（はたらいて）= worked
  • 汗まみれ（あせまみれ）= covered in sweat
  • になった = became

How to form it

Noun + まみれ (works as a noun: まみれだ, まみれの + noun, まみれになる)

  • 血（ち）= blood → 血まみれ（ちまみれ）= covered in blood
  • 埃（ほこり）= dust → 埃まみれの本（ほこりまみれのほん）= a dust-covered book
  • 借金（しゃっきん）= debt → 借金まみれ（しゃっきんまみれ）= drowning in debt

Compared with ～だらけ ("full of"), ～まみれ is about something stuck all over the surface.

Key idea

～まみれ describes being coated in something messy from head to toe.
