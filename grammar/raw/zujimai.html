～ずじまい: ended up never doing

～ずじまい after a verb's ない-stem says that something the speaker intended or hoped to do never happened, and now the chance is gone.

In English it is "ended up never..." or "never got around to...". It carries a feeling of regret.

A chance that never came

Something the speaker intended or hoped to do never happened, and now the opportunity is gone.

結局、彼には会えずじまいだった。

"In the end, I never got to see him."

  • 結局（けっきょく）= in the end
  • 彼（かれ）= him
  • には = (as for)
  • 会えず（あえず）= without being able to meet
  • じまいだった = ended up

買った本を、読まずじまいで捨ててしまった。

"I threw away the book I'd bought without ever reading it."

  • 買った（かった）= bought
  • 本（ほん）= book
  • を = object marker
  • 読まず（よまず）= without reading
  • じまいで = ending up
  • 捨ててしまった（すててしまった）= threw away

How to form it

Verb ない-stem + ずじまい (する becomes せずじまい)

  • 言う（いう）= to say → 言わずじまい（いわずじまい）= ended up never saying
  • 聞く（きく）= to ask → 聞けずじまい（きけずじまい）= never got to ask
  • 行く（いく）= to go → 行かずじまい（いかずじまい）= never ended up going

Key idea

～ずじまい closes a door with regret: it was meant to happen, but it never did.
