～ごとに: every / each (repeating per unit)

ごとに after a noun or number says that something repeats with each unit: every hour, every person, every time.

In English it is "every" or "each". With changes, it can also mean "with each... it gets more".

1. Regular repetition

After a time or distance, it gives the interval of repetition.

バスは十分ごとに来る。

"The bus comes every ten minutes."

  • バス = bus
  • は = topic marker
  • 十分（じゅっぷん）= ten minutes
  • ごとに = every
  • 来る（くる）= to come

2. Something growing with each step

With a verb, it means that every time something happens, the result changes a little more.

会うごとに、彼女はきれいになる。

"Every time I see her, she gets prettier."

  • 会う（あう）= to meet
  • ごとに = every time
  • 彼女（かのじょ）= she
  • きれいに = pretty
  • なる = to become

～ごとに vs ～おきに

ごとに never skips a unit: 一日ごと is every day. おきに leaves a gap: 一日おき is every other day. With hours and minutes the two usually mean the same: 十分ごと and 十分おき are both every ten minutes.

How to form it

Noun / number + ごとに, Verb dictionary form + ごとに

  • 一時間（いちじかん）= one hour → 一時間ごとに（いちじかんごとに）= every hour
  • 国（くに）= country → 国ごとに（くにごとに）= for each country
  • 季節（きせつ）= season → 季節ごとに（きせつごとに）= every season

Key idea

ごとに attaches the same thing to every unit: every hour, every person, every time.
