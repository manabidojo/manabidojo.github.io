～直す: redoing something

直す means "to fix, to correct". After a verb's ます-stem, ～直す means doing an action again to fix it or do it better.

In English it is "redo" or "re-": 書き直す = rewrite, 考え直す = reconsider.

1. Doing it again to fix it

The first attempt wasn't good enough, so the action is repeated.

もう一度書き直してください。

"Please rewrite it once more."

  • もう一度（もういちど）= once more
  • 書き直して（かきなおして）= rewrite
  • ください = please

2. Reconsidering

With 考える, it means changing one's mind after thinking again.

その話、もう一回考え直してみるよ。

"I'll try rethinking that idea once more."

  • その = that
  • 話（はなし）= matter, idea
  • もう一回（もういっかい）= once more
  • 考え直して（かんがえなおして）= rethink
  • みる = try
  • よ = (you know)

How to form it

Verb ます-stem + 直す

  • 見る（みる）= to look → 見直す（みなおす）= look again, reassess
  • 読む（よむ）= to read → 読み直す（よみなおす）= reread
  • やる = to do → やり直す（やりなおす）= start over

見直す has a second meaning: to see someone in a new, better light: 彼を見直した (he went up in my estimation).

Key idea

～直す says "again, and better this time": it repeats an action to correct it.
