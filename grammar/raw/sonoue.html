そのうえ: on top of that / in addition

そのうえ ("on top of that") adds a further point to what was just said, usually one that pushes in the same direction.

In English it is "on top of that", "in addition" or "besides". It is a bit more formal than それに.

1. Adding a good point

It stacks another positive point onto the first.

彼は頭がいい。そのうえ、スポーツも得意だ。

"He's smart. On top of that, he's good at sports."

  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • は = topic marker
  • 頭がいい（あたまがいい）= smart
  • そのうえ = on top of that
  • スポーツ = sports
  • も = also
  • 得意（とくい）= good at

2. Piling up bad points

It also adds one problem on top of another.

道に迷った。そのうえ、雨まで降ってきた。

"I got lost. On top of that, it even started raining."

  • 道に迷った（みちにまよった）= got lost
  • そのうえ = on top of that
  • 雨（あめ）= rain
  • まで = even
  • 降ってきた（ふってきた）= started falling

How to form it

Sentence A. そのうえ、sentence B. そのうえ never changes form.

  • 安い（やすい）= cheap → そのうえ、おいしい = on top of that, tasty
  • 親切だ（しんせつだ）= is kind → そのうえ、話も面白い（そのうえ、はなしもおもしろい）= on top of that, fun to talk to
  • 寒い（さむい）= cold → そのうえ、風も強い（そのうえ、かぜもつよい）= on top of that, windy too

Key idea

そのうえ piles one more point on top, good or bad, in a slightly formal tone.
