～ながらも: even though / while being

～ながらも is a concessive connector: despite the state described in the first clause, the second clause still holds. It maps to English "even though" or "while (being) ... , still ...".

Be careful not to confuse it with plain ～ながら meaning "while doing two actions at once" (音楽を聞きながら勉強する). Adding も makes the contrastive, "even though" reading explicit, and ～ながらも attaches not only to verbs but also to adjectives and nouns.

Despite a state, the opposite holds

The first clause sets up an expectation; the clause after ～ながらも goes against it.

彼は若いながらも、経験が豊富だ。

"Even though he is young, he has plenty of experience."

  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • は = topic marker
  • 若い（わかい）= young
  • ながらも = even though
  • 経験（けいけん）= experience
  • が = subject marker
  • 豊富だ（ほうふだ）= is plentiful

彼女は本当のことを知っていながらも、何も言わなかった。

"Even though she knew the truth, she said nothing."

  • 彼女（かのじょ）= she
  • 本当のこと（ほんとうのこと）= the truth
  • を = object marker
  • 知っていながらも（しっていながらも）= even though (she) knew
  • 何も（なにも）= nothing (with negative)
  • 言わなかった（いわなかった）= did not say

How to form it

Verb ます-stem / い-adjective / noun + ながらも

  • 知る（しる）= to know → 知りながらも（しりながらも）= even while knowing
  • 若い（わかい）= young → 若いながらも（わかいながらも）= even though young
  • 狭い（せまい）= cramped → 狭いながらも（せまいながらも）= cramped though it is

Key idea

～ながらも means "even though" or "despite". The first part is true, but the second part goes against what you would normally expect.
