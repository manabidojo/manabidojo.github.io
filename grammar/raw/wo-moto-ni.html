～をもとに: based on

もと means "basis" or "source". ～をもとに says that something was made or decided using another thing as its basis.

In English it is "based on" or "using... as a basis".

1. A work based on something real

Movies, stories and products are often based on real events or data.

実際の事件をもとに映画が作られた。

"The movie was made based on a real incident."

  • 実際の（じっさいの）= actual, real
  • 事件（じけん）= incident
  • をもとに = based on
  • 映画（えいが）= movie
  • が = subject marker
  • 作られた（つくられた）= was made

2. A decision based on information

It also describes judgments made from data or experience.

アンケートの結果をもとに、新しい商品を考えた。

"We came up with a new product based on the survey results."

  • アンケート = survey
  • の = ('s)
  • 結果（けっか）= results
  • をもとに = based on
  • 新しい（あたらしい）= new
  • 商品（しょうひん）= product
  • 考えた（かんがえた）= thought up

～をもとに vs ～に基づいて

Both mean "based on". ～に基づいて means following the basis faithfully, like rules or data. ～をもとに means using it as raw material for something new, like a novel turned into a film.

How to form it

Noun + をもとに（して） (before a noun: をもとにした + noun)

  • 経験（けいけん）= experience → 経験をもとに（けいけんをもとに）= based on experience
  • データ = data → データをもとに = based on the data
  • 小説（しょうせつ）= novel → 小説をもとにしたドラマ（しょうせつをもとにしたドラマ）= a drama based on a novel

Key idea

～をもとに names the source material that something new was built from.
