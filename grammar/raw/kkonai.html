～っこない: there's no way (casual)

～っこない after a verb's ます-stem says that something absolutely can't or won't happen. It is casual and emotional.

In English it is "there's no way..." or "can't possibly". It is often used with potential verbs like できる or 分かる.

Ruling something out

The speaker is sure that something can't be done or won't happen. It is casual and emotional.

そんなこと、できっこない。

"There's no way I can do that."

  • そんなこと = that kind of thing
  • できっこない = there's no way (I) can do

今から行っても、間に合いっこないよ。

"Even if we go now, there's no way we'll make it."

  • 今から（いまから）= from now
  • 行っても（いっても）= even if (we) go
  • 間に合いっこない（まにあいっこない）= no way (we'll) make it
  • よ = (you know)

How to form it

Verb ます-stem + っこない

  • 分かる（わかる）= to understand → 分かりっこない（わかりっこない）= can't possibly understand
  • 勝つ（かつ）= to win → 勝てっこない（かてっこない）= no way to win
  • 言う（いう）= to tell → 言えっこない（いえっこない）= can't possibly say

It is only used in casual speech. In formal contexts, use はずがない or わけがない.

Key idea

～っこない rules something out completely, in a casual, emotional tone.
