～てやまない: never stop (hoping / loving)

やむ means "to stop". ～てやまない after verbs of feeling like 願う, 祈る or 愛する says that the feeling never stops, expressing it deeply and sincerely.

In English it is "sincerely", "never cease to" or "from the bottom of my heart". It is formal and common in speeches and letters.

A feeling that never stops

It expresses deep, lasting wishes, love or respect, often in formal speeches and letters.

皆様のご活躍を願ってやみません。

"I sincerely wish you all the best."

  • 皆様（みなさま）= everyone
  • の = ('s)
  • ご活躍（ごかつやく）= success, activity
  • を = object marker
  • 願って（ねがって）= wishing
  • やみません = never stop

彼は故郷を愛してやまなかった。

"He never stopped loving his hometown."

  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • は = topic marker
  • 故郷（こきょう）= hometown
  • を = object marker
  • 愛して（あいして）= loving
  • やまなかった = never stopped

How to form it

Verb て-form + やまない

  • 祈る（いのる）= to pray → 祈ってやまない（いのってやまない）= sincerely pray
  • 期待する（きたいする）= to hope → 期待してやまない（きたいしてやまない）= have high hopes
  • 尊敬する（そんけいする）= to respect → 尊敬してやまない（そんけいしてやまない）= deeply respect

It is used for the speaker's own feelings, or in narration about others.

Key idea

～てやまない describes a feeling that keeps going without end, in a formal, heartfelt tone.
