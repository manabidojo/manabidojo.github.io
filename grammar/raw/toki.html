～とき: when / at the time of

～とき tells when something happens. The word とき (時) is simply the noun "time", and the words before it describe which time you mean, "the time when I was a child", "the time when I eat".

For English speakers, it works like "when ..." at the start of a sentence.

1. With a noun: Noun + のとき

To say "when I was a child" or "when it's summer", connect the noun to とき with the particle の.

子供のとき、よく泳ぎました。

"When I was a child, I often swam."

  • 子供（こども）= child
  • の = connects the noun to とき (like "of")
  • とき = when / time
  • よく = often
  • 泳ぎました（およぎました）= swam

2. With a verb: plain form + とき

A verb in the plain form goes directly before とき, with no particle in between.

ご飯を食べるとき、「いただきます」と言います。

"When we eat, we say 'itadakimasu'."

  • ご飯（ごはん）= meal / rice
  • を = object marker
  • 食べる（たべる）= to eat
  • とき = when
  • いただきます = set phrase said before eating
  • と = quotation marker
  • 言います（いいます）= say

A comma usually follows the とき clause, just like the comma after "When ..." in English.

How to form it

Noun + の + とき ／ plain form + とき

  • 子供（こども）= child → 子供のとき（こどものとき）= when (I) was a child
  • 寒い（さむい）= cold → 寒いとき（さむいとき）= when it is cold
  • 暇な（ひまな）= free (na-adjective) → 暇なとき（ひまなとき）= when (I) am free

Key idea

とき is just the noun "time" with a description in front of it: "the time when ...". Whatever comes before とき tells you which time the rest of the sentence happens at.
