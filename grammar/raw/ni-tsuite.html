～について: talking about a topic

～について attaches to a noun and marks it as the topic of talking, thinking, writing, or asking. It is formed simply as Noun + について.

For English speakers, it maps closely to "about" or "regarding", as in "talk about history" or "ask about the plan".

1. Topic of talking or thinking

Use ～について before verbs like 話す (to talk), 考える (to think), or 聞く (to ask) to say what the action is about.

日本の歴史について話します。

"I will talk about Japanese history."

  • 日本（にほん）= Japan
  • の = possessive marker (Japan's)
  • 歴史（れきし）= history
  • について = about, regarding
  • 話します（はなします）= will talk

2. ～についての + noun

To connect the phrase directly to a noun, add の: ～についての + noun means "a ... about ...".

日本文化についての本を読みました。

"I read a book about Japanese culture."

  • 日本文化（にほんぶんか）= Japanese culture
  • についての = about (linking to a noun)
  • 本（ほん）= book
  • を = object marker
  • 読みました（よみました）= read (past)

A close relative is ～に関して, which means the same thing but sounds more formal and written. In everyday conversation, ～について is the natural choice.

How to form it

Noun + について (add の before another noun)

  • 歴史（れきし）= history → 歴史について（れきしについて）= about history
  • 仕事（しごと）= work → 仕事について（しごとについて）= about work
  • 映画（えいが）= movie → 映画についての話（えいがについてのはなし）= a conversation about the movie

Key idea

～について points at the content of an action: whatever comes before it is what you talk, think, write, or ask about.
