～ように言う: telling someone to do something

ように plus a verb like 言う ("to say"), 頼む ("to ask") or 注意する ("to warn") reports an instruction or request indirectly.

In English it is "tell someone to..." or "ask someone to...". The instruction goes before ように in the plain form.

1. Reporting an instruction

With the passive 言われた, the speaker is the one who was told.

母に早く帰るように言われた。

"My mom told me to come home early."

  • 母（はは）= mother
  • に = by
  • 早く（はやく）= early
  • 帰る（かえる）= to go home
  • ように = (to do)
  • 言われた（いわれた）= was told

2. A negative instruction

With the ない-form, it reports what someone was told not to do.

遅れないように伝えてください。

"Please tell them not to be late."

  • 遅れない（おくれない）= not be late
  • ように = (to do)
  • 伝えて（つたえて）= convey, tell
  • ください = please

How to form it

Verb dictionary form / ない-form + ように + 言う／頼む／注意する

  • 静かにする（しずかにする）= to be quiet → 静かにするように言う（しずかにするようにいう）= tell (someone) to be quiet
  • 忘れる（わすれる）= to forget → 忘れないように注意する（わすれないようにちゅういする）= warn not to forget
  • 手伝う（てつだう）= to help → 手伝うように頼む（てつだうようにたのむ）= ask (someone) to help

Key idea

～ように言う turns a direct order into reported speech: it tells what someone was told to do.
