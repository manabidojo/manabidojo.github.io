～間に: something happens during a span of time

～間に（あいだに）uses the noun 間 ("interval") to mean "during / while": at some point within the span of X, the main event happens. It typically follows a Verb in ている-form (寝ている間に) or a Noun + の (夏休みの間に).

The particle に matters: ～間に means the main action happens at a point within the span, while ～間 without に means it continues throughout the whole span. And unlike ～うちに, which urges acting before the condition changes, ～間に is a neutral statement about timing.

1. While someone is doing something

With ている + 間に, one action provides the time frame, and another action takes place inside it.

赤ちゃんが寝ている間に家事をする。

"I do the housework while the baby is sleeping."

  • 赤ちゃん（あかちゃん）= baby
  • が = subject marker
  • 寝ている（ねている）= is sleeping
  • 間に（あいだに）= during, while
  • 家事（かじ）= housework
  • を = object marker
  • する = do

2. During a period (noun + の間に)

With a noun describing a period, の間に means something happened at some point within that period.

夏休みの間に、本を三冊読んだ。

"During the summer vacation, I read three books."

  • 夏休み（なつやすみ）= summer vacation
  • の = links the noun to 間に
  • 間に（あいだに）= during
  • 本（ほん）= book
  • を = object marker
  • 三冊（さんさつ）= three volumes
  • 読んだ（よんだ）= read

How to form it

Verb ている + 間に / Noun + の + 間に

  • 寝る（ねる）= to sleep → 寝ている間に（ねているあいだに）= while (someone) is sleeping
  • 待つ（まつ）= to wait → 待っている間に（まっているあいだに）= while waiting
  • 夏休み（なつやすみ）= summer vacation → 夏休みの間に（なつやすみのあいだに）= during summer vacation

Key idea

～間に draws a time bracket and drops one event inside it. Keep the に: with it, the event happens at a point within the span; without it, the second action lasts the entire span.
