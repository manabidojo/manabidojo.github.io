～ところを: in the act of / scene of

ところ literally means "place / point". After a verb, it refers to a specific point in an action. With ～ところを, that moment becomes part of a larger sentence: someone sees it, interrupts it, or otherwise acts on it. It often corresponds to English expressions like "see someone doing" or "while someone is in the middle of...".

Unlike ～ところだ, which simply reports what stage an action is at (about to do, doing, just did), ～ところを embeds that moment inside a larger sentence.

1. Witnessing or catching someone in the act

The verb after ところを is typically 見る, 見られる, 捕まえる, or 発見する. The passive version often carries a nuance of being caught at an awkward moment.

子供たちがサッカーをするところを見た。

"I saw the children playing soccer."

  • 子供たち（こどもたち）= children
  • が = subject marker
  • サッカー = soccer
  • を = object marker
  • する = to do / to play
  • ところを = the scene of (marked as object)
  • 見た（みた）= saw

先生に居眠りしているところを見られた。

"I was caught dozing off by the teacher."

  • 先生（せんせい）= teacher
  • に = agent marker (by)
  • 居眠りしている（いねむりしている）= dozing off
  • ところを = in the act of
  • 見られた（みられた）= was seen

2. Polite apologies for interrupting

In set phrases, ところを acknowledges the situation someone is in while you impose on them, "at a time when you are busy".

お忙しいところをすみません。

"I am sorry to bother you when you are busy."

  • お忙しい（おいそがしい）= busy (polite)
  • ところを = at a time when
  • すみません = I am sorry / excuse me

How to form it

Verb plain form (dictionary / ている / た) + ところを + another verb

  • する = to do → するところを見た（するところをみた）= saw (someone) doing it
  • 寝ている（ねている）= is sleeping → 寝ているところを見られた（ねているところをみられた）= was seen sleeping
  • 逃げる（にげる）= to flee → 逃げるところを捕まえた（にげるところをつかまえた）= caught (them) as they fled

Key idea

～ところを freezes an action at its critical moment and embeds that snapshot in a larger sentence: someone sees, catches, photographs, or otherwise reacts to the very scene of it happening.
