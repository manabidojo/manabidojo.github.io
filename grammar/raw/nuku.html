～抜く: doing to the very end

抜く means "to pull out" or "to get through". After a verb's ます-stem, ～抜く says that an action is carried out completely, to the very end, usually despite difficulty.

In English it is "all the way through", "to the end" or "thoroughly".

Carrying something through

It shows determination and endurance: the action is completed despite difficulty, or done as thoroughly as possible.

最後まで走り抜いた。

"I ran all the way to the end."

  • 最後（さいご）= the end
  • まで = until
  • 走り抜いた（はしりぬいた）= ran all the way through

悩み抜いた末に、会社を辞めることにした。

"After agonizing over it thoroughly, I decided to quit the company."

  • 悩み抜いた（なやみぬいた）= agonized thoroughly
  • 末に（すえに）= after
  • 会社（かいしゃ）= company
  • を = object marker
  • 辞める（やめる）= to quit
  • ことにした = decided to

How to form it

Verb ます-stem + 抜く

  • 考える（かんがえる）= to think → 考え抜く（かんがえぬく）= think through thoroughly
  • 戦う（たたかう）= to fight → 戦い抜く（たたかいぬく）= fight to the end
  • 守る（まもる）= to protect → 守り抜く（まもりぬく）= protect to the end

Key idea

～抜く pushes an action all the way through, no matter how hard it gets.
