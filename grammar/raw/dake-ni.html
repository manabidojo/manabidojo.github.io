～だけに: precisely because

～だけに says that a result is all the stronger because of a certain fact. It can lead to a positive or a negative result.

In English it is "precisely because..." or "all the more because...".

1. Disappointment that matches the hopes

The bigger the expectation, the bigger the disappointment.

期待していただけに、がっかりした。

"Precisely because I had high hopes, I was disappointed."

  • 期待していた（きたいしていた）= had high hopes
  • だけに = precisely because
  • がっかりした = was disappointed

2. A reaction that fits the situation

It also says that something is all the more true given the circumstances.

初めての優勝だけに、喜びも大きかった。

"Since it was our first championship, the joy was all the greater."

  • 初めての（はじめての）= first
  • 優勝（ゆうしょう）= championship
  • だけに = precisely because
  • 喜び（よろこび）= joy
  • も = also
  • 大きかった（おおきかった）= was big

～だけに vs ～だけあって

～だけあって mostly praises: the result lives up to the reputation. ～だけに can go either way and stresses that the result is all the stronger because of the circumstances.

How to form it

Plain form + だけに (nouns take noun + だけに, な-adjectives take な)

  • 若い（わかい）= young → 若いだけに（わかいだけに）= being young
  • 努力した（どりょくした）= made an effort → 努力しただけに（どりょくしただけに）= precisely because (they) tried so hard
  • 大事な試合（だいじなしあい）= important match → 大事な試合だけに（だいじなしあいだけに）= being such an important match

Key idea

～だけに says "because of this, all the more": the situation amplifies the result.
