～にかかわらず: regardless of

～にかかわらず says that something doesn't depend on a certain factor: whatever it is, the result is the same.

In English it is "regardless of" or "whether or not". Be careful not to confuse it with ～にもかかわらず, which means "despite".

1. Regardless of a factor

Nouns that contain a range or a contrast (age, weather, gender) are common.

年齢にかかわらず、誰でも参加できる。

"Anyone can join, regardless of age."

  • 年齢（ねんれい）= age
  • にかかわらず = regardless of
  • 誰でも（だれでも）= anyone
  • 参加できる（さんかできる）= can take part

2. With opposite pairs

It often follows a pair of opposites.

天気の良し悪しにかかわらず、試合は行われる。

"The game will be held whether the weather is good or bad."

  • 天気（てんき）= weather
  • の = ('s)
  • 良し悪し（よしあし）= good or bad
  • にかかわらず = regardless of
  • 試合（しあい）= game
  • 行われる（おこなわれる）= will be held

～にかかわらず vs ～にもかかわらず

にかかわらず means "regardless of": the factor doesn't matter. にもかかわらず means "despite": something happened even though the factor was against it. Compare 天気にかかわらず出かける (I go out whatever the weather) with 雨にもかかわらず出かけた (I went out despite the rain).

How to form it

Noun + にかかわらず, Verb dictionary form + ない-form + にかかわらず

  • 性別（せいべつ）= gender → 性別にかかわらず（せいべつにかかわらず）= regardless of gender
  • 経験（けいけん）= experience → 経験の有無にかかわらず（けいけんのうむにかかわらず）= with or without experience
  • 行く（いく）= to go → 行く行かないにかかわらず（いくいかないにかかわらず）= whether (you) go or not

Key idea

～にかかわらず removes a factor from the equation: whatever it is, the result stays the same.
