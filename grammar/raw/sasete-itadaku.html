～させていただく: humbly doing (with permission)

～させていただく combines the causative ("let someone do") with いただく (humble "receive"). Literally "I humbly receive your letting me do it", it is a very humble way to say that the speaker will do something.

In English it is close to "allow me to..." or "we will..." in formal announcements. It is common in business and customer service.

1. Formal announcements

Businesses use it to announce their own actions politely.

本日はお休みさせていただきます。

"We are closed today."

  • 本日（ほんじつ）= today (formal)
  • は = topic marker
  • お休み（おやすみ）= day off
  • させていただきます = (humbly) will take

2. Introducing your own action

In meetings and speeches, it presents the speaker's action humbly.

それでは、始めさせていただきます。

"Well then, allow me to begin."

  • それでは = well then
  • 始めさせて（はじめさせて）= let (me) begin
  • いただきます = (humbly) receive

How to form it

Verb causative て-form + いただく

  • 説明する（せつめいする）= to explain → 説明させていただきます（せつめいさせていただきます）= allow me to explain
  • 帰る（かえる）= to go home → 帰らせていただきます（かえらせていただきます）= I will (humbly) leave
  • 使う（つかう）= to use → 使わせていただく（つかわせていただく）= humbly use

People sometimes overuse it in situations where no permission is involved, and some listeners find that excessive. Plain humble forms like いたします are often enough.

Key idea

～させていただく wraps the speaker's own action in humility, as if acting only with the listener's permission.
