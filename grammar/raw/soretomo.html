それとも: or (in questions)

それとも offers an alternative inside a question. It connects two options and asks the listener to choose.

In English it is "or", as in "coffee or tea?". It is only used in questions; in statements, "or" is か or または.

1. Offering a choice

Each option is often its own short question.

コーヒーにする？それとも紅茶？

"Coffee? Or tea?"

  • コーヒー = coffee
  • にする = go with
  • それとも = or
  • 紅茶（こうちゃ）= black tea

2. Weighing two possibilities

In longer sentences, それとも links two complete questions.

電車で行きますか、それともバスで行きますか。

"Will you go by train, or by bus?"

  • 電車（でんしゃ）= train
  • で = by
  • 行きますか（いきますか）= will (you) go?
  • それとも = or
  • バス = bus

How to form it

Question A？ それとも、question B？ それとも never changes form.

  • 今（いま）= now → 今？それとも後で？（いま？それともあとで？）= now? Or later?
  • 和食（わしょく）= Japanese food → 和食？それとも洋食？（わしょく？それともようしょく？）= Japanese food? Or Western?
  • 本当（ほんとう）= true → 本当？それとも冗談？（ほんとう？それともじょうだん？）= for real? Or a joke?

Key idea

それとも is the "or" of questions: it puts two options side by side and asks the listener to pick one.
