～をよそに: ignoring / in spite of

よそ means "somewhere else". ～をよそに says that someone acts without paying attention to other people's feelings, worries or circumstances.

In English it is "ignoring...", "in spite of..." or "heedless of...".

Carrying on regardless

The noun is usually someone else's worry or opinion, or a situation pulling the other way, and it is simply ignored.

親の心配をよそに、彼は一人で旅に出た。

"Ignoring his parents' worries, he set off on a trip alone."

  • 親（おや）= parents
  • の = ('s)
  • 心配（しんぱい）= worry
  • をよそに = ignoring
  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • は = topic marker
  • 一人で（ひとりで）= alone
  • 旅に出た（たびにでた）= set off on a trip

周りの反対をよそに、計画は進められた。

"The plan went ahead in spite of opposition from those around them."

  • 周り（まわり）= people around
  • の = ('s)
  • 反対（はんたい）= opposition
  • をよそに = in spite of
  • 計画（けいかく）= plan
  • は = topic marker
  • 進められた（すすめられた）= was pushed forward

How to form it

Noun + をよそに

  • 期待（きたい）= expectations → 期待をよそに（きたいをよそに）= in spite of the expectations
  • 批判（ひはん）= criticism → 批判をよそに（ひはんをよそに）= ignoring the criticism
  • 騒ぎ（さわぎ）= commotion → 騒ぎをよそに（さわぎをよそに）= heedless of the commotion

Key idea

～をよそに shows someone looking the other way: whatever others feel, they carry on.
