～ごとく／～ごとき: like / as if (literary)

ごとく and ごとき are literary forms meaning "like" or "as". ごとく works like ように (before verbs and adjectives) and ごとき works like ような (before nouns).

In English it is "like" or "as if". It appears in literature, speeches, and in anime with dramatic or old-fashioned characters.

1. A literary comparison

It compares an action with something vivid.

彼は風のごとく走り去った。

"He ran off like the wind."

  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • は = topic marker
  • 風（かぜ）= wind
  • のごとく = like
  • 走り去った（はしりさった）= ran off

2. Looking down on someone

ごとき after a person can sound contemptuous, like "the likes of".

お前ごときに負けるわけがない。

"There's no way I'd lose to the likes of you."

  • お前（おまえ）= you (rough)
  • ごとき = the likes of
  • に = to
  • 負ける（まける）= to lose
  • わけがない = there's no way

How to form it

Noun + のごとく／のごとき, Plain form + (が) ごとく

  • 夢（ゆめ）= dream → 夢のごとく（ゆめのごとく）= like a dream
  • 前述（ぜんじゅつ）= the above → 前述のごとく（ぜんじゅつのごとく）= as mentioned above
  • 私（わたし）= me → 私ごとき（わたしごとき）= someone like me (humble)

私ごとき used about oneself is humble: "someone as lowly as me".

Key idea

ごとく／ごとき is ように／ような in a literary costume, sometimes with a touch of drama or contempt.
