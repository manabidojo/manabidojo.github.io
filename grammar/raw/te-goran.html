～てごらん: try it and see

ごらん comes from the honorific 御覧 ("look"). After a て-form, ～てごらん is a gentle invitation to try something and see.

In English it is "try..." or "go ahead and...". It is said by parents, teachers or older people to someone younger.

1. Encouraging someone to try

The speaker gently urges the listener to have a go.

ちょっと食べてごらん。

"Here, try a bite."

  • ちょっと = a little
  • 食べて（たべて）= eat
  • ごらん = try and see

2. Look and see

With 見る, 見てごらん means "take a look".

空を見てごらん。きれいな虹だよ。

"Look at the sky. What a beautiful rainbow."

  • 空（そら）= sky
  • を = object marker
  • 見てごらん（みてごらん）= take a look
  • きれいな = beautiful
  • 虹（にじ）= rainbow
  • だよ = it is

How to form it

Verb て-form + ごらん

  • やる = to do → やってごらん = try doing it
  • 言う（いう）= to say → 言ってごらん（いってごらん）= go ahead, say it
  • 考える（かんがえる）= to think → 考えてごらん（かんがえてごらん）= think about it

It is the gentle, from-above version of ～てみて. Don't use it toward superiors.

Key idea

～てごらん is a kind push from someone older: "go on, give it a try".
