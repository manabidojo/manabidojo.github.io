～ぶり: for the first time in / way of doing

ぶり has two uses. After a time span, it means "for the first time in" that long. After a noun or a verb's ます-stem, it describes someone's manner of doing something.

In English the first is "for the first time in (three years)", the second "the way (someone) does...".

1. After a long gap

The time span says how long it has been since the last time.

三年ぶりに故郷に帰った。

"I went back to my hometown for the first time in three years."

  • 三年（さんねん）= three years
  • ぶりに = for the first time in
  • 故郷（こきょう）= hometown
  • に = to
  • 帰った（かえった）= went back

2. A way of doing something

After a noun or ます-stem, it describes how someone does something, often to praise or criticize it.

彼の仕事ぶりは、みんなに評価されている。

"His way of working is highly regarded by everyone."

  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • の = ('s)
  • 仕事ぶり（しごとぶり）= way of working
  • は = topic marker
  • みんな = everyone
  • に = by
  • 評価されている（ひょうかされている）= is valued

How to form it

Time span + ぶり（に）, Noun / verb ます-stem + ぶり

  • 久しぶり（ひさしぶり）= after a long time → 久しぶりに（ひさしぶりに）= for the first time in ages
  • 十年（じゅうねん）= ten years → 十年ぶりの再会（じゅうねんぶりのさいかい）= a reunion after ten years
  • 食べる（たべる）= to eat → 食べっぷり（たべっぷり）= way of eating

久しぶり ("long time no see") is the most common word with this ぶり. In the "manner" sense, ぶり often becomes っぷり in casual speech.

Key idea

～ぶり measures a gap of time, or describes someone's style of doing things.
