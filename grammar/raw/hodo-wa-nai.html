～ほど～はない: nothing compares to

ほど marks a degree, and with the negative ない the pattern AほどBはない says that nothing is as B as A.

In English it is "nothing is as... as" or "there's nothing like". It is a strong way to say that something is the best or most extreme.

Putting something at the very top

The thing before ほど is presented as the most extreme of its kind. It works for general truths and for strong personal feelings alike.

家族ほど大切なものはない。

"Nothing is as important as family."

  • 家族（かぞく）= family
  • ほど = as much as
  • 大切な（たいせつな）= important
  • もの = thing
  • はない = there is none

仕事の後のビールほどおいしいものはない。

"Nothing tastes as good as a beer after work."

  • 仕事（しごと）= work
  • の後（のあと）= after
  • ビール = beer
  • ほど = as much as
  • おいしい = tasty
  • ものはない = there is nothing

How to form it

Noun + ほど + adjective + noun (もの／人) + はない

  • 富士山（ふじさん）= Mt. Fuji → 富士山ほど高い山はない（ふじさんほどたかいやまはない）= no mountain is as high as Fuji
  • 母（はは）= mother → 母ほど優しい人はいない（ははほどやさしいひとはいない）= nobody is as kind as my mother
  • 健康（けんこう）= health → 健康ほど大事なものはない（けんこうほどだいじなものはない）= nothing matters as much as health

For people and animals, use いない instead of ない.

Key idea

AほどBはない puts A at the very top: there is nothing more B than it.
