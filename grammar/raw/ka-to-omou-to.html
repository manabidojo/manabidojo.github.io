～かと思うと／～かと思ったら: no sooner than / just when

～かと思うと and ～かと思ったら say that right after one thing happened, something else happened, usually surprisingly or in contrast.

In English it is "no sooner had... than..." or "just when (you thought)...". It describes something the speaker observed, not their own actions.

A sudden switch

Two contrasting things happen one right after the other. It describes something the speaker observed, often restless or unpredictable behavior.

泣いたかと思ったら、もう笑っている。

"No sooner had she cried than she was laughing again."

  • 泣いた（ないた）= cried
  • かと思ったら（かとおもったら）= no sooner than
  • もう = already
  • 笑っている（わらっている）= is laughing

弟は帰ってきたかと思うと、すぐまた出かけた。

"My brother had barely come home when he went out again."

  • 弟（おとうと）= younger brother
  • は = topic marker
  • 帰ってきた（かえってきた）= came home
  • かと思うと（かとおもうと）= no sooner than
  • すぐ = right away
  • また = again
  • 出かけた（でかけた）= went out

How to form it

Verb た-form + かと思うと／かと思ったら

  • 晴れる（はれる）= to clear up → 晴れたかと思ったら（はれたかとおもったら）= no sooner had it cleared up
  • 座る（すわる）= to sit → 座ったかと思うと（すわったかとおもうと）= no sooner had (he) sat down
  • 来る（くる）= to come → 来たかと思ったら（きたかとおもったら）= just when (they) came

Key idea

～かと思うと captures a sudden switch: one thing happens, and almost immediately something else does.
