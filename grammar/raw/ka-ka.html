～か～か: A or B / whether or not

The particle か between options means "or". A verb followed by its own negative, ～か～ないか, means "whether or not".

In English it is "A or B" or "whether or not".

1. Whether or not

A verb and its negative with か give the two sides of a decision.

行くか行かないか、早く決めて。

"Decide quickly whether you're going or not."

  • 行くか（いくか）= go or
  • 行かないか（いかないか）= not go
  • 早く（はやく）= quickly
  • 決めて（きめて）= decide

2. Choosing between options

With nouns, か offers alternatives.

月曜日か火曜日に会いましょう。

"Let's meet on Monday or Tuesday."

  • 月曜日（げつようび）= Monday
  • か = or
  • 火曜日（かようび）= Tuesday
  • に = on
  • 会いましょう（あいましょう）= let's meet

How to form it

A + か + B (+ か), Verb plain form + か + ない-form + か

  • 肉（にく）= meat → 肉か魚か（にくかさかなか）= meat or fish
  • 来る（くる）= to come → 来るか来ないか（くるかこないか）= whether (they) come or not
  • 本当（ほんとう）= true → 本当か嘘か（ほんとうかうそか）= whether it's true or a lie

Key idea

か splits things into options. List nouns with it, or pair a verb with its negative for "whether or not".
