～ばかりか: not only (but even)

～ばかりか says "not only A, but even B", where B is more surprising or extreme than A. The second clause often contains も or まで.

In English it is "not only... but (even)...". It is similar to ～だけでなく, but more emphatic.

1. Adding something worse

The second point goes even further than the first.

彼は謝らないばかりか、文句まで言った。

"Not only did he not apologize, he even complained."

  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • は = topic marker
  • 謝らない（あやまらない）= doesn't apologize
  • ばかりか = not only
  • 文句（もんく）= complaints
  • まで = even
  • 言った（いった）= said

2. Adding something better

It also works for positive surprises.

彼女は英語ばかりか、中国語も話せる。

"She speaks not only English but also Chinese."

  • 彼女（かのじょ）= she
  • は = topic marker
  • 英語（えいご）= English
  • ばかりか = not only
  • 中国語（ちゅうごくご）= Chinese
  • も = also
  • 話せる（はなせる）= can speak

How to form it

Plain form + ばかりか (nouns take noun + ばかりか, な-adjectives take な)

  • 遅れる（おくれる）= to be late → 遅れたばかりか（おくれたばかりか）= not only was (he) late
  • 子供（こども）= children → 子供ばかりか（こどもばかりか）= not only children
  • 便利（べんり）= convenient → 便利なばかりか（べんりなばかりか）= not only convenient

Key idea

～ばかりか escalates: A is already notable, and B goes even further.
