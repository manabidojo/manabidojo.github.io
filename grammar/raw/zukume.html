～ずくめ: nothing but / full of

～ずくめ attaches to a noun and says that a situation, period, or appearance consists entirely of that one thing, in English "nothing but ..." or "all ... from head to toe". It combines with a fairly small set of conventional nouns: いいこと, ごちそう, 黒, 規則, 記録, and the like.

Unlike ～だらけ, which is negative and means "covered in / riddled with" (泥だらけ, 間違いだらけ), ～ずくめ is neutral to positive and often describes a happy streak or a uniform look.

1. A run of events that are all one thing

Applied to a stretch of time or experience, ずくめ says the whole period was filled with that one kind of thing.

今年は贅沢ずくめの一年だった。

"This year was a year of nothing but luxury."

  • 今年（ことし）= this year
  • は = topic marker
  • 贅沢（ぜいたく）= luxury
  • ずくめ = nothing but / full of
  • の = linking particle
  • 一年（いちねん）= one year
  • だった = was

2. An appearance made of one thing

With colors, ずくめ almost always describes someone dressed entirely in that color.

黒ずくめの男が立っていた。

"A man dressed all in black was standing there."

  • 黒（くろ）= black
  • ずくめ = entirely in / nothing but
  • の = linking particle
  • 男（おとこ）= man
  • が = subject marker
  • 立っていた（たっていた）= was standing

How to form it

Noun + ずくめ (often followed by の + noun)

  • いいこと = good things → いいことずくめ = nothing but good things
  • 黒（くろ）= black → 黒ずくめ（くろずくめ）= dressed all in black
  • ごちそう = feast / delicacies → ごちそうずくめ = a spread of nothing but delicacies

Key idea

～ずくめ paints a scene in a single color: whatever noun it follows, the whole period, situation, or outfit is made of that and nothing else.
