疑問詞＋か: question inside a sentence

A question word like 何, どこ, 誰 or いつ followed by か turns a question into a part of a larger sentence.

In English this is the "where he lives" in "I don't know where he lives". The embedded question is in the plain form and is followed by verbs like 知る, 分かる or 教える.

1. Questions inside sentences

The question part ends with か and becomes the object of the main verb.

彼がどこに住んでいるか知らない。

"I don't know where he lives."

  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • が = subject marker
  • どこに = where
  • 住んでいる（すんでいる）= lives
  • か = (question)
  • 知らない（しらない）= don't know

2. Asking someone to tell you

With 教えてください it becomes a polite request for information.

何時に始まるか教えてください。

"Please tell me what time it starts."

  • 何時（なんじ）= what time
  • に = at
  • 始まる（はじまる）= to start
  • か = (question)
  • 教えて（おしえて）= tell
  • ください = please

Question word + か vs かどうか

With a question word, the embedded question asks about content: どこに住んでいるか (where he lives). Without one, use かどうか for a yes or no question: 来るかどうか分からない (I don't know whether he'll come).

How to form it

Question word + plain form + か (nouns and な-adjectives drop だ)

  • 誰（だれ）= who → 誰が来るか（だれがくるか）= who will come
  • いつ = when → いつ帰るか（いつかえるか）= when (they'll) come back
  • 何（なに）= what → 何が好きか（なにがすきか）= what (they) like

Key idea

Question word + か lets a question sit inside another sentence, just like "where he lives" in English.
