～に際して: on the occasion of / when

際 (さい) means "occasion". ～に際して marks a special or important moment, often a beginning, at which something is done.

In English it is "on the occasion of", "when" or "upon". It is formal and common in announcements.

Marking a special occasion

It frames something done at an important moment, often the start of something like a departure, a contract or a new school year.

出発に際して、注意事項を説明します。

"Before departure, I will explain the precautions."

  • 出発（しゅっぱつ）= departure
  • に際して（にさいして）= on the occasion of
  • 注意事項（ちゅういじこう）= precautions
  • を = object marker
  • 説明します（せつめいします）= will explain

入学に際して、祖父が時計をくれた。

"When I started school, my grandfather gave me a watch."

  • 入学（にゅうがく）= entering school
  • に際して（にさいして）= on the occasion of
  • 祖父（そふ）= grandfather
  • が = subject marker
  • 時計（とけい）= watch
  • くれた = gave (me)

How to form it

Noun / verb dictionary form + に際して

  • 契約（けいやく）= contract → 契約に際して（けいやくにさいして）= when signing the contract
  • 開店（かいてん）= opening a shop → 開店に際して（かいてんにさいして）= on the opening of the shop
  • 引っ越す（ひっこす）= to move → 引っ越すに際して（ひっこすにさいして）= when moving

It is close to ～際に, but sounds more formal and is mostly used for important, one-time occasions.

Key idea

～に際して marks a special moment and what is done at it.
