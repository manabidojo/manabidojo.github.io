～に応じて: according to / in response to

応じる means "to respond, to meet". ～に応じて says that something changes to match a situation, need or request.

In English it is "according to", "depending on" or "in response to".

1. Changing to match something

The result is adjusted to fit the noun before に応じて.

収入に応じて税金が決まる。

"Taxes are set according to income."

  • 収入（しゅうにゅう）= income
  • に応じて（におうじて）= according to
  • 税金（ぜいきん）= taxes
  • が = subject marker
  • 決まる（きまる）= to be decided

2. Meeting a request

It also means responding to a request or demand.

お客様の希望に応じて、料理を作ります。

"We prepare dishes according to the customer's wishes."

  • お客様（おきゃくさま）= customer
  • の = ('s)
  • 希望（きぼう）= wishes
  • に応じて（におうじて）= according to
  • 料理（りょうり）= dishes
  • 作ります（つくります）= make

How to form it

Noun + に応じて (before a noun: に応じた + noun)

  • 年齢（ねんれい）= age → 年齢に応じて（ねんれいにおうじて）= according to age
  • 必要（ひつよう）= need → 必要に応じて（ひつようにおうじて）= as needed
  • 能力（のうりょく）= ability → 能力に応じた仕事（のうりょくにおうじたしごと）= work suited to one's ability

Key idea

～に応じて adjusts the result to fit: the more or different the input, the more or different the outcome.
