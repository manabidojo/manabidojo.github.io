～やすい: easy to do

～やすい attaches to the ます-stem of a verb (the form before ます) and says that the action is easy to do. The result behaves like a normal い-adjective, so it can become past (読みやすかった), negative (読みやすくない), and so on.

For English speakers it maps to "easy to ...". Its opposite is ～にくい ("hard to ..."), which attaches in exactly the same way.

1. An action that is easy to perform

Use ～やすい to describe something that people can do without difficulty.

この本は読みやすい。

"This book is easy to read."

  • この = this
  • 本（ほん）= book
  • は = topic marker
  • 読みやすい（よみやすい）= easy to read

2. Something that happens easily

～やすい can also mean that something tends to happen, even when nobody is doing it on purpose.

冬は風邪をひきやすい。

"In winter, you catch colds easily."

  • 冬（ふゆ）= winter
  • は = topic marker
  • 風邪（かぜ）= a cold
  • を = object marker
  • ひきやすい = easy to catch, tend to catch

How to form it

Verb ます-stem + やすい

  • 読む（よむ）= to read → 読みやすい（よみやすい）= easy to read
  • 食べる（たべる）= to eat → 食べやすい（たべやすい）= easy to eat
  • 分かる（わかる）= to understand → 分かりやすい（わかりやすい）= easy to understand

Key idea

～やすい turns a verb into an adjective that rates the action: low effort, or likely to happen. If it is hard instead of easy, swap in ～にくい.
