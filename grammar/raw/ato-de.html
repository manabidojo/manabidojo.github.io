～後で: "after" doing something

～後で（あとで）marks the time after an action or event. It follows a verb in the た-form (食べた後で, "after eating") or a noun + の (仕事の後で, "after work").

It maps directly to English "after doing ...".

～後で is the mirror image of ～前に ("before").

1. After an action

The verb before 後で is always in the た-form, regardless of the tense of the whole sentence => by the time of the main clause, that action is already completed.

食べた後で散歩した。

"I took a walk after eating."

  • 食べた（たべた）= ate (past of 食べる, to eat)
  • 後で（あとで）= after
  • 散歩した（さんぽした）= took a walk (past of 散歩する, to take a walk)

2. After a noun event

With a noun, insert の before 後で.

授業の後で友達と話した。

"I talked with a friend after class."

  • 授業（じゅぎょう）= class, lesson
  • の = links the noun to 後で
  • 後で（あとで）= after
  • 友達（ともだち）= friend
  • と = with
  • 話した（はなした）= talked (past of 話す, to talk)

How to form it

Verb た-form + 後で / Noun + の後で

  • 食べる（たべる）= to eat → 食べた後で（たべたあとで）= after eating
  • 終わる（おわる）= to end → 終わった後で（おわったあとで）= after it ends
  • 仕事（しごと）= work → 仕事の後で（しごとのあとで）= after work

Key idea

Whatever stands before 後で happens first, and it must look completed — that is why the verb is always in the た-form, even in a sentence about the future.
