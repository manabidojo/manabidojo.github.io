～に伴って: along with / as (one change brings another)

伴う means "to accompany". ～に伴って says that one change brings another with it, usually on a large scale.

In English it is "along with", "as" or "accompanying". It is formal and common in news and reports.

1. Changes that go together

As the first thing changes, the second changes along with it.

人口の増加に伴って、問題も増えた。

"As the population grew, so did the problems."

  • 人口（じんこう）= population
  • の = ('s)
  • 増加（ぞうか）= increase
  • に伴って（にともなって）= along with
  • 問題（もんだい）= problems
  • も = also
  • 増えた（ふえた）= increased

2. A consequence

It also introduces something that follows from an event.

工事に伴って、この道は通れなくなります。

"Due to construction work, this road will be closed."

  • 工事（こうじ）= construction
  • に伴って（にともなって）= due to
  • この = this
  • 道（みち）= road
  • は = topic marker
  • 通れなくなります（とおれなくなります）= will become impassable

How to form it

Noun / verb dictionary form + に伴って (before a noun: に伴う + noun)

  • 経済の成長（けいざいのせいちょう）= economic growth → 経済の成長に伴って（けいざいのせいちょうにともなって）= along with economic growth
  • 引っ越し（ひっこし）= moving → 引っ越しに伴う手続き（ひっこしにともなうてつづき）= procedures that come with moving
  • 年を取る（としをとる）= to age → 年を取るに伴って（としをとるにともなって）= as (one) ages

Key idea

～に伴って links two changes: when one moves, the other comes along with it.
