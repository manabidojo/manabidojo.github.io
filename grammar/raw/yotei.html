～予定だ: it's planned / scheduled

予定 (よてい) means "plan" or "schedule". After a verb or a noun with の, ～予定だ says that something has been arranged in advance.

In English it is "be scheduled to" or "plan to". It sounds more official than つもり, like something already on the calendar.

1. Talking about a fixed plan

The plan is settled, not just an idea.

来週、東京に行く予定だ。

"I'm scheduled to go to Tokyo next week."

  • 来週（らいしゅう）= next week
  • 東京（とうきょう）= Tokyo
  • に = to
  • 行く（いく）= to go
  • 予定だ（よていだ）= it's planned

2. Events that are planned

It also describes when events are expected to happen, not only people's actions.

会議は三時に終わる予定です。

"The meeting is scheduled to end at three."

  • 会議（かいぎ）= meeting
  • は = topic marker
  • 三時（さんじ）= three o'clock
  • 終わる（おわる）= to end
  • 予定です（よていです）= is scheduled

How to form it

Verb dictionary form + 予定だ, Noun + の + 予定だ

  • 帰る（かえる）= to go home → 帰る予定だ（かえるよていだ）= planning to go home
  • 出張（しゅっちょう）= business trip → 出張の予定だ（しゅっちょうのよていだ）= a business trip is planned
  • 結婚する（けっこんする）= to marry → 結婚する予定だった（けっこんするよていだった）= was planning to marry

Key idea

予定 is a plan that is already on the schedule. For a personal intention that may change, つもり fits better.
