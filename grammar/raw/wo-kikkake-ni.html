～をきっかけに: triggered by / taking the opportunity of

きっかけ means "trigger" or "occasion". ～をきっかけに names an event that started a change, decision or new activity.

In English it is "triggered by", "prompted by" or "taking... as an opportunity".

1. The start of an interest

An event led to a new interest or habit.

留学をきっかけに、日本に興味を持った。

"Studying abroad got me interested in Japan."

  • 留学（りゅうがく）= studying abroad
  • をきっかけに = triggered by
  • 日本（にほん）= Japan
  • に = in
  • 興味を持った（きょうみをもった）= became interested

2. A turning point

It also marks events that changed someone's life or habits.

病気をきっかけに、たばこをやめた。

"Getting sick made me quit smoking."

  • 病気（びょうき）= illness
  • をきっかけに = triggered by
  • たばこ = cigarettes
  • を = object marker
  • やめた = quit

How to form it

Noun + をきっかけに（して）, Verb plain form + のをきっかけに

  • 結婚（けっこん）= marriage → 結婚をきっかけに（けっこんをきっかけに）= with getting married as the trigger
  • 引っ越し（ひっこし）= moving house → 引っ越しをきっかけに（ひっこしをきっかけに）= prompted by moving
  • 映画を見た（えいがをみた）= watched a movie → 映画を見たのをきっかけに（えいがをみたのをきっかけに）= after watching a movie (it started)

Key idea

～をきっかけに points to the spark: the event that set a change in motion.
