～向け／～向き: aimed at / suitable for

向け (むけ) after a noun means made for a certain group. 向き (むき) means suited to a group, whether intended or not.

In English 向け is "for" or "aimed at", and 向き is "suitable for" or "fit for".

1. Made for a target group

向け describes products, books or services designed for someone.

これは子供向けの本だ。

"This is a book for children."

  • これ = this
  • は = topic marker
  • 子供向け（こどもむけ）= aimed at children
  • の = (of)
  • 本（ほん）= book
  • だ = is

2. Suited to someone

向き says that something happens to fit a person or purpose well.

このコースは初心者向きだ。

"This course is good for beginners."

  • この = this
  • コース = course
  • は = topic marker
  • 初心者向き（しょしんしゃむき）= suitable for beginners
  • だ = is

How to form it

Noun + 向け／向き (before a noun: 向けの／向きの + noun)

  • 外国人（がいこくじん）= foreigners → 外国人向け（がいこくじんむけ）= for foreigners
  • 大人（おとな）= adults → 大人向けのアニメ（おとなむけのアニメ）= anime for adults
  • 夏（なつ）= summer → 夏向きの服（なつむきのふく）= clothes suited to summer

Key idea

向け is about who it was made for; 向き is about who it fits.
