～の中で～が一番: the most within a group

一番 (いちばん) means "number one". With ～の中で ("among") it picks the top item out of a group.

In English it works like "the most" or the "-est" ending: 一番高い = the highest.

1. Picking the top item

First the group with の中で, then the winner with が, then 一番 and the adjective.

果物の中でりんごが一番好きだ。

"Of all fruits, I like apples the most."

  • 果物（くだもの）= fruit
  • の中で（のなかで）= among
  • りんご = apple
  • が = marks the winner
  • 一番（いちばん）= the most
  • 好き（すき）= liked

2. Asking for the top item

A question word like 何, どこ or 誰 asks which one is number one.

日本料理の中で何が一番おいしいですか。

"What is the tastiest Japanese dish?"

  • 日本料理（にほんりょうり）= Japanese food
  • の中で（のなかで）= among
  • 何（なに）= what
  • 一番（いちばん）= the most
  • おいしい = tasty

How to form it

Group + の中で + item + が + 一番 + adjective

  • 家族（かぞく）= family → 家族の中で（かぞくのなかで）= in the family
  • 世界（せかい）= world → 世界で一番（せかいでいちばん）= the most in the world
  • 一年（いちねん）= one year → 一年で一番（いちねんでいちばん）= the most in the year

With places and time spans, で alone is enough: 世界で一番高い山 (the highest mountain in the world).

Key idea

一番 turns any adjective into "the most". The group it is compared within goes first, with の中で or で.
