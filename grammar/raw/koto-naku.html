～ことなく: without (ever) doing

～ことなく after a verb in the dictionary form says that an action continues without something happening, usually something you'd expect.

In English it is "without (ever)...". It is more formal than ～ないで.

Without the expected interruption

It stresses endurance or consistency: something went on without a single break, or without giving in.

彼は休むことなく働き続けた。

"He kept working without a single break."

  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • は = topic marker
  • 休む（やすむ）= to rest
  • ことなく = without
  • 働き続けた（はたらきつづけた）= kept working

彼女は諦めることなく、夢を追い続けた。

"She never gave up and kept chasing her dream."

  • 彼女（かのじょ）= she
  • は = topic marker
  • 諦める（あきらめる）= to give up
  • ことなく = without
  • 夢（ゆめ）= dream
  • を = object marker
  • 追い続けた（おいつづけた）= kept chasing

How to form it

Verb dictionary form + ことなく

  • 迷う（まよう）= to hesitate → 迷うことなく（まようことなく）= without hesitating
  • 変わる（かわる）= to change → 変わることなく（かわることなく）= without changing
  • 失敗する（しっぱいする）= to fail → 失敗することなく（しっぱいすることなく）= without failing

The N1 pattern ～ことなしに is similar but stresses a condition: "unless (you do)".

Key idea

～ことなく describes something that went on without the expected interruption.
