～か～ないかのうちに: the moment / just as

～か～ないかのうちに repeats a verb in its positive and negative forms: "while it's unclear whether it happened or not". It says that the next action started at almost the same instant.

In English it is "the moment..." or "before... had even finished".

Almost at the same instant

The second action starts before the first has clearly finished, which often shows eagerness or impatience.

ベルが鳴るか鳴らないかのうちに、彼は教室を飛び出した。

"The moment the bell rang, he dashed out of the classroom."

  • ベル = bell
  • が = subject marker
  • 鳴るか鳴らないか（なるかならないか）= whether (it) rang or not
  • のうちに = in the moment
  • 教室（きょうしつ）= classroom
  • 飛び出した（とびだした）= dashed out

席に着いたか着かないかのうちに、子供たちは食べ始めた。

"The kids started eating before they'd even sat down."

  • 席に着いた（せきについた）= sat down
  • 着かない（つかない）= didn't sit
  • のうちに = in the moment
  • 子供たち（こどもたち）= children
  • 食べ始めた（たべはじめた）= started eating

How to form it

Verb dictionary form / た-form + か + ない-form + かのうちに

  • 終わる（おわる）= to end → 終わるか終わらないかのうちに（おわるかおわらないかのうちに）= the moment (it) ended
  • 言う（いう）= to say → 言うか言わないかのうちに（いうかいわないかのうちに）= before (he) had even finished saying
  • 寝る（ねる）= to sleep → 寝たか寝ないかのうちに（ねたかねないかのうちに）= just as (I) fell asleep

Key idea

～か～ないかのうちに blurs two moments together: the second thing started before the first was even clearly done.
