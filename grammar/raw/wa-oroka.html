～はおろか: let alone / not to mention

～はおろか says that not even the easier thing B is possible, let alone A. The second clause usually contains も or さえ and is often negative.

In English it is "let alone..." or "not to mention...".

Not even the easier thing

The first item is the harder one, and not even the second, easier one is possible. The sentence is usually negative.

漢字はおろか、ひらがなも読めない。

"He can't read hiragana, let alone kanji."

  • 漢字（かんじ）= kanji
  • はおろか = let alone
  • ひらがな = hiragana
  • も = even
  • 読めない（よめない）= can't read

忙しくて、旅行はおろか、週末も休めない。

"I'm so busy I can't even rest on weekends, let alone travel."

  • 忙しくて（いそがしくて）= busy (and)
  • 旅行（りょこう）= travel
  • はおろか = let alone
  • 週末（しゅうまつ）= weekends
  • も = even
  • 休めない（やすめない）= can't rest

How to form it

Noun + はおろか + noun + も／さえ／まで

  • 結婚（けっこん）= marriage → 結婚はおろか（けっこんはおろか）= let alone marriage
  • 車（くるま）= car → 車はおろか自転車もない（くるまはおろかじてんしゃもない）= no bicycle, let alone a car
  • 英語（えいご）= English → 英語はおろか（えいごはおろか）= let alone English

Key idea

～はおろか dismisses the bigger thing and points out that even the smaller one is out of reach.
