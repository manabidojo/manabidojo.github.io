～を通じて／～を通して: through / via / throughout

通じる and 通す both mean "to pass through". ～を通じて and ～を通して mark the means or channel through which something happens, and also a period throughout which something continues.

In English it is "through", "via" or "throughout".

1. Through a person or medium

Something happens by way of a person, an activity or a medium.

友達を通じて彼と知り合った。

"I got to know him through a friend."

  • 友達（ともだち）= friend
  • を通じて（をつうじて）= through
  • 彼（かれ）= him
  • と = with
  • 知り合った（しりあった）= got to know

2. Throughout a period

With time expressions, it means "all through".

この地域は一年を通して暖かい。

"This area is warm all year round."

  • この = this
  • 地域（ちいき）= area
  • は = topic marker
  • 一年（いちねん）= one year
  • を通して（をとおして）= throughout
  • 暖かい（あたたかい）= warm

How to form it

Noun + を通じて／を通して

  • インターネット = internet → インターネットを通じて = via the internet
  • 経験（けいけん）= experience → 経験を通して（けいけんをとおして）= through experience
  • 四季（しき）= four seasons → 四季を通じて（しきをつうじて）= throughout the seasons

を通して often stresses that someone actively used the means, while を通じて sounds more neutral.

Key idea

～を通じて is a channel: through a person, a medium, or a whole period of time.
