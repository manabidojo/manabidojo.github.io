～もさることながら: not only (but even more so)

～もさることながら acknowledges one point as obviously important and then stresses another point that matters even more.

In English it is "not only..., but (even more so)..." or "while... is great, ... is even better". It is formal.

Raising a second point higher

The first point is acknowledged as good or important, and the second one is presented as even more remarkable.

味もさることながら、店の雰囲気もいい。

"The food is great, and the atmosphere even more so."

  • 味（あじ）= taste
  • もさることながら = not only
  • 店（みせ）= restaurant
  • の = ('s)
  • 雰囲気（ふんいき）= atmosphere
  • も = also
  • いい = good

才能もさることながら、努力が大切だ。

"Talent matters, but effort matters even more."

  • 才能（さいのう）= talent
  • もさることながら = not only
  • 努力（どりょく）= effort
  • が = subject marker
  • 大切（たいせつ）= important

How to form it

Noun + もさることながら

  • 内容（ないよう）= content → 内容もさることながら（ないようもさることながら）= not only the content
  • 値段（ねだん）= price → 値段もさることながら（ねだんもさることながら）= not only the price
  • 見た目（みため）= looks → 見た目もさることながら（みためもさることながら）= not only the looks

Key idea

～もさることながら grants that the first point is good and then raises the second one even higher.
