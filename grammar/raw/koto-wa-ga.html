～ことは～が: it's true that, but

～ことは～が repeats the same word around ことは, as in 読んだことは読んだが. It admits something is true, but adds a reservation.

In English it is "I did..., but..." or "it's true that..., but...". The first part concedes, the second part limits it.

1. Admitting with a limitation

The speaker did it, but not fully or not with the expected result.

読んだことは読んだが、よく分からなかった。

"I did read it, but I didn't really understand it."

  • 読んだ（よんだ）= read
  • ことは = as for (the fact)
  • 読んだが（よんだが）= I read it, but
  • よく = well
  • 分からなかった（わからなかった）= didn't understand

2. With adjectives

With adjectives, it admits a quality but plays it down.

この店はおいしいことはおいしいけど、高い。

"This place is tasty, sure, but it's expensive."

  • この = this
  • 店（みせ）= restaurant
  • は = topic marker
  • おいしいことは = as for being tasty
  • おいしいけど = it is tasty, but
  • 高い（たかい）= expensive

How to form it

A + ことは + A + が／けど (the same word repeated)

  • 行った（いった）= went → 行ったことは行ったが（いったことはいったが）= I did go, but
  • 好き（すき）= liked → 好きなことは好きだが（すきなことはすきだが）= I do like it, but
  • 安い（やすい）= cheap → 安いことは安いけど（やすいことはやすいけど）= it is cheap, but

Key idea

～ことは～が says "yes, that's true..." and then immediately adds "but".
