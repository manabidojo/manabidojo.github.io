～限りだ: feeling extremely (happy, lonely)

～限りだ after an adjective of feeling says that the speaker feels this to the utmost. It is formal and expresses the speaker's own emotion.

In English it is "I feel so...", "extremely" or "nothing could be more...".

A feeling at full strength

It expresses the speaker's own feelings in a formal way and is common in speeches and greetings.

一人で寂しい限りだ。

"Being alone, I feel so lonely."

  • 一人で（ひとりで）= alone
  • 寂しい（さびしい）= lonely
  • 限りだ（かぎりだ）= extremely

皆様にお会いできて、嬉しい限りです。

"I'm truly delighted to meet you all."

  • 皆様（みなさま）= everyone
  • に = (meet) with
  • お会いできて（おあいできて）= being able to meet
  • 嬉しい（うれしい）= happy
  • 限りです（かぎりです）= extremely

How to form it

い-adjective + 限りだ, な-adjective + な + 限りだ

  • 羨ましい（うらやましい）= envious → 羨ましい限りだ（うらやましいかぎりだ）= I'm so envious
  • 心細い（こころぼそい）= uneasy → 心細い限りだ（こころぼそいかぎりだ）= I feel so uneasy
  • 残念（ざんねん）= regrettable → 残念な限りだ（ざんねんなかぎりだ）= it's truly a shame

Don't confuse it with ～限り ("as long as") or できる限り ("as much as possible").

Key idea

～限りだ expresses a feeling at full strength, in a formal and personal way.
