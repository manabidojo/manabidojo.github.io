～だから: so / therefore

～だから marks a reason that leads to the following statement. It is the copula だ (is) plus から (because), and it attaches directly to nouns and な-adjectives.

The clause before だから gives the cause; the clause after it gives the result. In English it maps to "because it's ..., so ..." or simply "so / therefore".

1. Giving a reason inside one sentence

State the cause, attach だから, then say what you will do or what follows from it.

雨だから、傘を持って行く。

"It's raining, so I'll take an umbrella."

  • 雨（あめ）= rain
  • だから = because it is, so
  • 傘（かさ）= umbrella
  • を = object marker
  • 持って行く（もっていく）= will take (along)

2. Starting a new sentence: "That's why ..."

だから can also stand at the beginning of a sentence, pointing back to what was just said: "that's why", "so".

明日は試験だ。だから、今日は早く寝る。

"Tomorrow is the exam. So today I'll go to bed early."

  • 明日（あした）= tomorrow
  • は = topic marker
  • 試験（しけん）= exam
  • だから = so / that's why
  • 今日（きょう）= today
  • 早く（はやく）= early
  • 寝る（ねる）= to go to bed

How to form it

Noun / な-adjective + だから (verbs and い-adjectives take から directly, without だ)

  • 雨（あめ）= rain → 雨だから（あめだから）= because it is raining
  • 静か（しずか）= quiet → 静かだから（しずかだから）= because it is quiet
  • 学生（がくせい）= student → 学生だから（がくせいだから）= because (someone) is a student

Key idea

～だから says "A is the reason, and B follows from it." The cause comes first, the conclusion comes second, the opposite order from English "B because A".
