～み: a felt quality (adjective to noun)

み replaces the い of some い-adjectives (and the end of a few な-adjectives) to turn them into a noun for a quality you feel or sense.

In English it is close to "-ness" or a felt sense of something. Compared with ～さ, which measures a degree, ～み describes a sensation or feeling.

1. A sensation

It is common for tastes and sensations.

このスープは甘みが強い。

"This soup is quite sweet."

  • この = this
  • スープ = soup
  • は = topic marker
  • 甘み（あまみ）= sweetness
  • が = subject marker
  • 強い（つよい）= strong

2. An emotional quality

It also names feelings and impressions.

彼の言葉には温かみがある。

"His words have a warmth to them."

  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • の = ('s)
  • 言葉（ことば）= words
  • には = in
  • 温かみ（あたたかみ）= warmth
  • がある = there is

～み vs ～さ

～さ measures a degree and works with almost any adjective: 甘さ (how sweet something is). ～み names a quality you feel and only works with certain words: 甘み (a sweet taste). 高さ is height, while 高み is a height you rise to; there is no 速み.

How to form it

い-adjective (drop い) + み (only certain adjectives)

  • 楽しい（たのしい）= fun → 楽しみ（たのしみ）= something to look forward to
  • 悲しい（かなしい）= sad → 悲しみ（かなしみ）= sorrow
  • 重い（おもい）= heavy → 重み（おもみ）= weight, significance

Key idea

～み names a quality as something felt: sweetness on the tongue, warmth in a voice.
