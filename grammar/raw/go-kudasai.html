お／ご～ください: an honorific "please ..."

お／ご～ください is the standard honorific way to ask someone to do something. It is formed with the honorific prefix お or ご, the verb's ます-stem (or a する-verb noun), and ください.

It maps to a formal English "please ..." and is what you hear in shops, stations, offices, and announcements. It is one step more respectful than plain ～てください: 待ってください is a normal polite request, while お待ちください shows extra deference to the listener.

1. お + verb stem + ください (native verbs)

Verbs of Japanese origin take お before the ます-stem.

少々お待ちください。

"Please wait a moment."

  • 少々（しょうしょう）= a moment (formal)
  • お待ちください（おまちください）= please wait (honorific)

2. ご + する-verb noun + ください (Sino-Japanese verbs)

Nouns of Chinese origin that pair with する (確認する, 注意する) usually take ご instead.

こちらの資料をご確認ください。

"Please check these materials."

  • こちら = this (polite)
  • の = noun-linking particle
  • 資料（しりょう）= materials, documents
  • を = object marker
  • ご確認ください（ごかくにんください）= please check (honorific)

How to form it

お + verb ます-stem + ください, or ご + する-verb noun + ください

  • 待つ（まつ）= to wait → お待ちください（おまちください）= please wait
  • 入る（はいる）= to enter → お入りください（おはいりください）= please come in
  • 確認する（かくにんする）= to check → ご確認ください（ごかくにんください）= please check

Key idea

お／ご～ください wraps a request in respect for the listener. Pick お for native verb stems and ご for する-verb nouns, and you have the polished "please" used throughout customer service and formal life.
