～なら: "if that's the case" conditional

～なら is a conditional that takes a situation already on the table, often something the listener just said, and adds the speaker's comment, advice, or judgment about it. It attaches to the plain form of a verb or adjective, or directly to a noun.

In English it covers both "if (you're going to / it's true that)" and the topic-like "as for ..., (then) ...".

1. Responding to a supposition

Use ～なら to react to a plan or situation you have just heard about.

日本へ行くなら、京都がおすすめだ。

"If you're going to Japan, I recommend Kyoto."

  • 日本（にほん）= Japan
  • へ = direction marker (to)
  • 行く（いく）= to go
  • なら = if (that's the case)
  • 京都（きょうと）= Kyoto
  • が = subject marker
  • おすすめ = recommendation
  • だ = is (plain copula)

2. Picking up a topic: "as for ..."

After a noun, なら singles out that topic and offers relevant information, much like "if it's ... you mean".

寿司なら、あの店が一番おいしい。

"If it's sushi, that restaurant is the best."

  • 寿司（すし）= sushi
  • なら = as for / if it's
  • あの = that (over there)
  • 店（みせ）= shop, restaurant
  • が = subject marker
  • 一番（いちばん）= the most, number one
  • おいしい = delicious

How to form it

Plain form / Noun + なら (no だ after a noun)

  • 行く（いく）= to go → 行くなら（いくなら）= if you're going
  • 寒い（さむい）= cold → 寒いなら（さむいなら）= if you're cold
  • 寿司（すし）= sushi → 寿司なら（すしなら）= if it's sushi

Key idea

～なら means "given that situation, here is my take". It accepts information as a premise and builds advice or a comment on top of it.
