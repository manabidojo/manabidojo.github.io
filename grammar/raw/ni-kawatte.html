～に代わって: in place of / on behalf of

代わる means "to take the place of". ～に代わって says that someone or something acts instead of another person or thing.

In English it is "in place of", "instead of" or "on behalf of".

1. Acting for someone else

Someone takes over another person's role, often in formal situations.

部長に代わって、私がご説明します。

"I'll explain in place of the manager."

  • 部長（ぶちょう）= department manager
  • に代わって（にかわって）= in place of
  • 私（わたし）= I
  • が = subject marker
  • ご説明します（ごせつめいします）= will explain (humble)

2. Something being replaced

It also describes new things replacing old ones.

手紙に代わって、メールを使う人が増えた。

"More people now use email instead of letters."

  • 手紙（てがみ）= letter
  • に代わって（にかわって）= instead of
  • メール = email
  • 使う（つかう）= to use
  • 増えた（ふえた）= increased

How to form it

Noun + に代わって (before a noun: に代わる + noun)

  • 父（ちち）= father → 父に代わって（ちちにかわって）= on behalf of my father
  • 社長（しゃちょう）= president → 社長に代わって（しゃちょうにかわって）= in place of the president
  • 石油（せきゆ）= oil → 石油に代わるエネルギー（せきゆにかわるエネルギー）= energy to replace oil

Key idea

～に代わって puts someone or something in another's place.
