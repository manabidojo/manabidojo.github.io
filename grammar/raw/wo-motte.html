～をもって: as of / by means of (formal)

～をもって is a formal way to mark a point in time when something ends or begins, or the means by which something is done.

In English the first is "as of" or "with (today)", the second "by means of" or "with". It appears in official notices, speeches and letters.

1. The end of something

Announcements use it for closings and endings.

本日をもって閉店いたします。

"We are closing as of today."

  • 本日（ほんじつ）= today (formal)
  • をもって = as of
  • 閉店いたします（へいてんいたします）= will close (humble)

2. By means of

It also names the means or basis of an action.

結果は書面をもってお知らせします。

"We will inform you of the results in writing."

  • 結果（けっか）= results
  • は = topic marker
  • 書面（しょめん）= writing, document
  • をもって = by means of
  • お知らせします（おしらせします）= will inform (humble)

How to form it

Noun + をもって (more formal: をもちまして)

  • 今月（こんげつ）= this month → 今月をもって（こんげつをもって）= as of this month
  • 拍手（はくしゅ）= applause → 拍手をもって（はくしゅをもって）= with applause
  • これ = this → これをもちまして = with this (we conclude)

これをもちまして is a classic phrase for closing a ceremony or meeting.

Key idea

～をもって is ceremonial: it marks an official ending point, or the means by which something is done.
