～ばいい／～たらいい: what you should do / all you have to do

～ばいい combines the conditional ～ば with いい ("good"). Literally "it's good if you do...", it gives advice or says that one simple action is enough. ～たらいい means the same and sounds a little more casual.

In English it maps to "you should", "all you have to do is", or in questions "what should I do?".

1. Giving simple advice

The action before ばいい is presented as the easy solution.

分からなければ、先生に聞けばいい。

"If you don't understand, just ask the teacher."

  • 分からなければ（わからなければ）= if (you) don't understand
  • 先生（せんせい）= teacher
  • に = to
  • 聞けば（きけば）= if (you) ask
  • いい = good

2. Asking what to do

With a question word, どうすればいい and どうしたらいい ask for advice.

駅までどうやって行ったらいいですか。

"How should I get to the station?"

  • 駅（えき）= station
  • まで = to
  • どうやって = how
  • 行ったら（いったら）= if (I) go
  • いいですか = is it good?

How to form it

Verb ば-form + いい, Verb た-form + ら + いい

  • 休む（やすむ）= to rest → 休めばいい（やすめばいい）= you should rest
  • 電話する（でんわする）= to call → 電話したらいい（でんわしたらいい）= you should call
  • どうする = what to do → どうすればいい = what should I do?

Key idea

～ばいい points to one simple solution: "just do this and it'll be fine".
