～場合は: in case of / if

場合 (ばあい) means "case" or "situation". ～場合は sets up a possible situation and what to do in it.

In English it is "in case of" or "if". It sounds more formal than ～たら, so it is common in rules, notices and instructions.

1. Rules and instructions

Notices use it to say what happens in a certain case.

雨の場合は、試合は中止です。

"In case of rain, the game is canceled."

  • 雨（あめ）= rain
  • の場合は（のばあいは）= in case of
  • 試合（しあい）= game, match
  • は = topic marker
  • 中止（ちゅうし）= canceled

2. A situation that might happen

After a verb, it describes a situation that may or may not happen.

遅れる場合は、連絡してください。

"If you're going to be late, please let us know."

  • 遅れる（おくれる）= to be late
  • 場合は（ばあいは）= in case
  • 連絡して（れんらくして）= contact (us)
  • ください = please

How to form it

Noun + の + 場合は, Plain form + 場合は (な-adjectives take な)

  • 地震（じしん）= earthquake → 地震の場合は（じしんのばあいは）= in case of an earthquake
  • 分からない（わからない）= don't understand → 分からない場合は（わからないばあいは）= if you don't understand
  • 必要（ひつよう）= necessary → 必要な場合は（ひつようなばあいは）= if necessary

Key idea

～場合は is the "in case of" of signs and instructions: it names a situation and says what to do in it.
