～きらいがある: has an (undesirable) tendency to

～きらいがある attaches to a verb in dictionary form (or a noun with の) and means that someone or something has a tendency to do something, usually a tendency the speaker views as undesirable. It maps to English "tends to" or "is apt to", with a critical undertone.

Despite the spelling, きらい here is not the adjective 嫌い ("dislike"); it is a separate noun meaning "a tendency". The pattern is formal and common in written commentary and evaluations. Unlike the everyday ～がちだ, which simply notes a frequent occurrence, ～きらいがある frames the tendency as a flaw being pointed out.

Criticizing a habitual leaning

Use ～きらいがある to comment, somewhat formally, on a person's or organization's problematic inclination. It describes general dispositions, not one-time events.

彼は物事を悲観的に考えるきらいがある。

"He has a tendency to think pessimistically."

  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • は = topic marker
  • 物事（ものごと）= things, matters
  • を = object marker
  • 悲観的に（ひかんてきに）= pessimistically
  • 考える（かんがえる）= to think
  • きらいがある = has a tendency to

この会社は伝統を重視しすぎるきらいがある。

"This company tends to place too much weight on tradition."

  • この = this
  • 会社（かいしゃ）= company
  • は = topic marker
  • 伝統（でんとう）= tradition
  • を = object marker
  • 重視しすぎる（じゅうししすぎる）= to overemphasize
  • きらいがある = has a tendency to

How to form it

Verb dictionary form + きらいがある / Noun + の + きらいがある

  • 考える（かんがえる）= to think → 考えるきらいがある（かんがえるきらいがある）= tends to think (that way)
  • 頼る（たよる）= to rely → 頼るきらいがある（たよるきらいがある）= is apt to rely (on others)
  • 悲観（ひかん）= pessimism → 悲観のきらいがある（ひかんのきらいがある）= has a pessimistic streak

Key idea

～きらいがある is a formal way of saying "X has a (regrettable) tendency to...". Use it to point out an ingrained leaning you think is a problem, not just something that happens often.
