なぜなら（～から）: the reason is / because

なぜなら ("if you ask why") introduces the reason after the statement. The reason usually ends with ～からだ.

In English it is "because" or "the reason is". It sounds rather formal and logical, typical of writing and speeches.

1. Giving the reason afterward

The conclusion comes first, then the reason with なぜなら.

今日は休む。なぜなら熱があるからだ。

"I'm taking today off. The reason is I have a fever."

  • 今日（きょう）= today
  • は = topic marker
  • 休む（やすむ）= to take off
  • なぜなら = the reason is
  • 熱（ねつ）= fever
  • が = subject marker
  • ある = to have
  • からだ = it's because

2. In an argument

In essays and debates, it backs up a claim.

私はこの案に反対だ。なぜなら費用がかかりすぎるからだ。

"I'm against this plan, because it costs too much."

  • 案（あん）= plan
  • 反対（はんたい）= opposed
  • なぜなら = the reason is
  • 費用（ひよう）= cost
  • かかりすぎる = costs too much
  • からだ = it's because

How to form it

Statement. なぜなら、reason + からだ. なぜなら never changes form.

  • 好き（すき）= liked → なぜなら好きだからだ（なぜならすきだからだ）= because I like it
  • 危ない（あぶない）= dangerous → なぜなら危ないからだ（なぜならあぶないからだ）= because it's dangerous
  • 時間がない（じかんがない）= no time → なぜなら時間がないからだ（なぜならじかんがないからだ）= because there's no time

In conversation, people usually just add ～から or ～んだ instead.

Key idea

なぜなら delivers the reason after the fact, in a clear, formal way.
