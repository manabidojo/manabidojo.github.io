～ことだから: knowing (someone), surely

Person + のことだから says that a judgment is based on what that person is like. The second clause is usually a confident guess.

In English it is "knowing (him / her)..." or "being who (he) is...".

1. A guess based on someone's character

The speaker predicts behavior from what they know about the person.

真面目な彼のことだから、きっと来るだろう。

"Knowing how serious he is, he'll surely come."

  • 真面目な（まじめな）= serious, diligent
  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • のことだから = knowing (him)
  • きっと = surely
  • 来るだろう（くるだろう）= will probably come

2. A negative prediction

It also works for bad habits.

いつも遅れる田中さんのことだから、今日も遅れるよ。

"Knowing Tanaka, who's always late, he'll be late today too."

  • いつも = always
  • 遅れる（おくれる）= to be late
  • 田中さん（たなかさん）= Mr. Tanaka
  • のことだから = knowing (him)
  • 今日も（きょうも）= today too

How to form it

Noun (a person) + のことだから

  • 母（はは）= mother → 母のことだから（ははのことだから）= knowing my mother
  • 彼女（かのじょ）= she → 彼女のことだから（かのじょのことだから）= knowing her
  • あの人（あのひと）= that person → あの人のことだから（あのひとのことだから）= knowing that person

Key idea

～のことだから reads the future from someone's character: being who they are, this is what they'll do.
