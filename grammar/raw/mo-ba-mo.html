～も～ば～も: both A and B

AもBばCも lists two things that are both true, often two sides of a person or situation. The first verb or adjective is in the ば-form.

In English it is "both... and..." or "not only... but also...". It often shows variety or contrast.

1. Two abilities or qualities

The pattern lists two things someone does or has.

彼は歌も歌えばダンスもする。

"He both sings and dances."

  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • は = topic marker
  • 歌（うた）= songs
  • も = also
  • 歌えば（うたえば）= sings, and
  • ダンス = dance
  • もする = also does

2. Ups and downs

It often lists good and bad things side by side.

人生にはいい時もあれば悪い時もある。

"In life there are good times and bad times."

  • 人生（じんせい）= life
  • には = in
  • いい時（いいとき）= good times
  • も = also
  • あれば = there are, and
  • 悪い時（わるいとき）= bad times
  • もある = there also are

How to form it

Noun + も + ば-form, noun + も + verb

  • 雨（あめ）= rain → 雨も降れば雪も降る（あめもふればゆきもふる）= it both rains and snows
  • 金（かね）= money → 金もあれば時間もある（かねもあればじかんもある）= has both money and time
  • 好きな人（すきなひと）= people who like it → 好きな人もいれば嫌いな人もいる（すきなひともいればきらいなひともいる）= some like it, some don't

Key idea

～も～ば～も puts two facts side by side: this is true, and that is true as well.
