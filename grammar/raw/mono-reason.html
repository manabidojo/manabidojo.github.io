～もの／～もん: because (casual excuse)

もの, or casually もん, at the end of a sentence gives a reason or excuse, often with a feeling of "that's just how it is". It usually follows ～んだ or ～だ, and often pairs with だって at the start.

In English it is close to a whiny "but..." or "because...". It sounds childish or cute, and is common with children, young women and in anime.

An excuse that should be enough

The speaker justifies themselves, often a little sulkily, as if the reason settles everything. It usually follows ～んだ or ～だ and pairs naturally with だって at the start of the sentence.

だって、眠かったんだもん。

"But I was sleepy!"

  • だって = but
  • 眠かった（ねむかった）= was sleepy
  • んだ = (explanation)
  • もん = because (excuse)

仕方ないよ、子供だもの。

"It can't be helped, he's just a kid."

  • 仕方ない（しかたない）= it can't be helped
  • よ = (you know)
  • 子供（こども）= child
  • だもの = because (he) is

How to form it

Plain form + もの／もん (often ～んだもん, ～だもん)

  • 知らない（しらない）= don't know → 知らないもん（しらないもん）= because I don't know
  • 好き（すき）= liked → 好きなんだもん（すきなんだもん）= because I like it
  • 嫌（いや）= disliked → 嫌だもん（いやだもん）= because I don't want to

Key idea

～もん ends a sentence with an excuse that the speaker thinks should be enough. It sounds young and a little stubborn.
