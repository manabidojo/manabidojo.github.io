～にする: deciding on / making it

にする after a noun has two common uses: choosing something, and making something into a certain state.

In English the first is "I'll have / I'll go with", the second is "make it...". In both, the speaker decides.

1. Deciding on something

When ordering or choosing, ～にします announces the decision.

飲み物はコーヒーにします。

"I'll have coffee for my drink."

  • 飲み物（のみもの）= drink
  • は = topic marker
  • コーヒー = coffee
  • にします = I'll go with

2. Making something a certain way

With な-adjectives, it describes changing a state on purpose.

部屋をきれいにしてください。

"Please tidy up your room."

  • 部屋（へや）= room
  • を = object marker
  • きれいに = clean
  • して = make
  • ください = please

How to form it

Noun / な-adjective + にする (い-adjectives take ～くする)

  • 赤（あか）= red → 赤にする（あかにする）= go with red
  • 静か（しずか）= quiet → 静かにする（しずかにする）= be quiet
  • 短い（みじかい）= short → 短くする（みじかくする）= make shorter

Key idea

～にする is a decision by the speaker: choosing an option, or turning something into a state.
