～になる: to become

～になる expresses a change of state: something turns into the noun or な-adjective before it. It is made with the particle に plus the verb なる ("to become").

It maps to English "to become" or "to turn into". Note that になる is only for nouns and な-adjectives; い-adjectives use ～くなる instead (寒い → 寒くなる, "to get cold").

1. Noun + になる

Use it when a person or thing becomes the noun: a new job, a new season, a new state.

兄は医者になった。

"My brother became a doctor."

  • 兄（あに）= older brother
  • は = topic marker
  • 医者（いしゃ）= doctor
  • に = result-of-change marker
  • なった = became

2. Na-adjective + になる

With a な-adjective, drop the な and attach になる directly.

この町は静かになりました。

"This town has become quiet."

  • この = this
  • 町（まち）= town
  • は = topic marker
  • 静か（しずか）= quiet
  • になりました = has become

How to form it

Noun / な-adjective + になる

  • 医者（いしゃ）= doctor → 医者になる（いしゃになる）= to become a doctor
  • 静か（しずか）= quiet → 静かになる（しずかになる）= to become quiet
  • 春（はる）= spring → 春になる（はるになる）= spring comes

Key idea

に points at the result, and なる says the change happened on its own. The subject ends up in a new state without anyone being mentioned as the cause.
