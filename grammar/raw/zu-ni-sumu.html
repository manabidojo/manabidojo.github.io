～ずに済む／～ないで済む: getting by without

済む means "to be settled, to end". ～ずに済む and ～ないで済む say that the speaker got by without having to do something unpleasant or unnecessary.

In English it is "get by without", "not have to after all" or "be spared".

Being spared something

Thanks to the circumstances, an unpleasant or unnecessary action turned out not to be needed. It usually expresses relief.

雨がやんだので、傘を買わずに済んだ。

"The rain stopped, so I got by without buying an umbrella."

  • 雨（あめ）= rain
  • が = subject marker
  • やんだ = stopped
  • ので = so
  • 傘（かさ）= umbrella
  • 買わずに（かわずに）= without buying
  • 済んだ（すんだ）= got by

早めに薬を飲んだから、学校を休まないで済んだ。

"I took medicine early, so I didn't have to miss school."

  • 早めに（はやめに）= early
  • 薬（くすり）= medicine
  • 飲んだ（のんだ）= took
  • から = so
  • 休まないで（やすまないで）= without missing
  • 済んだ（すんだ）= got by

How to form it

Verb ない-stem + ずに済む, Verb ない-form + で済む (する becomes せずに済む)

  • 払う（はらう）= to pay → 払わずに済む（はらわずにすむ）= not have to pay
  • 待つ（まつ）= to wait → 待たないで済む（またないですむ）= not have to wait
  • 心配する（しんぱいする）= to worry → 心配せずに済む（しんぱいせずにすむ）= be spared the worry

Key idea

～ずに済む is relief: something you expected to have to do turned out unnecessary.
