それでも: even so / still

それでも says that something still holds despite what was just said.

In English it is "even so", "still" or "nevertheless". Unlike それなのに, it often shows determination rather than complaint.

1. Not giving up

Despite the difficulties in the first sentence, the speaker keeps going.

何度も失敗した。それでも諦めない。

"I failed many times. Even so, I won't give up."

  • 何度も（なんども）= many times
  • 失敗した（しっぱいした）= failed
  • それでも = even so
  • 諦めない（あきらめない）= won't give up

2. A fact that remains true

It also states that something stays true despite a counterpoint.

高い。それでも、買う価値はある。

"It's expensive. Still, it's worth buying."

  • 高い（たかい）= expensive
  • それでも = still
  • 買う（かう）= to buy
  • 価値（かち）= value, worth
  • は = topic marker
  • ある = there is

How to form it

Sentence A. それでも、sentence B. それでも never changes form.

  • 反対された（はんたいされた）= I was opposed → それでも、行った（それでも、いった）= even so, I went
  • 疲れていた（つかれていた）= I was tired → それでも、勉強した（それでも、べんきょうした）= even so, I studied
  • 雨だった（あめだった）= it was raining → それでも、試合をした（それでも、しあいをした）= even so, we played

Key idea

それでも acknowledges an obstacle and moves past it: even so, this stays true.
