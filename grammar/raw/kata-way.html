～方: how to do something, or the way it is done

～方（かた）turns a verb into a noun meaning "the way of doing" that action. It is made by taking the ます-stem of a verb (the ます form with ます removed) and adding 方.

For English speakers, it usually matches "how to ...": 読み方 is "how to read", 作り方 is "how to make". Because the result is a noun, particles like を and が attach to it directly. Be careful not to confuse this 方（かた）with the 方（ほう）used in comparisons like ～たほうがいい.

1. Asking or explaining how to do something

Use ～方 when you want to ask about or explain the method for doing something.

この漢字の読み方を教えてください。

"Please tell me how to read this kanji."

  • この = this
  • 漢字（かんじ）= kanji
  • の = possessive marker (of)
  • 読み方（よみかた）= how to read, way of reading
  • を = object marker
  • 教えてください（おしえてください）= please tell/teach me

2. Describing the way someone does something

～方 can also describe the manner in which a person does something, not just a method you can learn.

彼の話し方が好きです。

"I like the way he talks."

  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • の = possessive marker (his)
  • 話し方（はなしかた）= way of talking
  • が = subject marker
  • 好きです（すきです）= like

How to form it

Verb ます-stem + 方（かた）

  • 読む（よむ）= to read → 読み方（よみかた）= how to read
  • 作る（つくる）= to make → 作り方（つくりかた）= how to make
  • 使う（つかう）= to use → 使い方（つかいかた）= how to use

Key idea

～方 packages an action into a noun meaning "the way of doing it". If you can ask "What's the way to do this?" in English, you can usually answer it in Japanese with a ～方 noun.
