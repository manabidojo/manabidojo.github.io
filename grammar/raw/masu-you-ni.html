～ますように: I hope / may it be (a wish)

～ますように at the end of a sentence turns it into a wish or prayer. It is what people write on shrine plaques or say when making a wish.

In English it is "I hope..." or "may...".

Making a wish

It works for the speaker's own hopes, like passing an exam, and for good wishes toward someone else.

試験に合格できますように。

"I hope I pass the exam."

  • 試験（しけん）= exam
  • に = (for)
  • 合格できます（ごうかくできます）= can pass
  • ように = (I hope)

早く元気になりますように。

"I hope you get well soon."

  • 早く（はやく）= soon
  • 元気に（げんきに）= healthy
  • なります = become
  • ように = (I hope)

How to form it

Verb ます-form + ように

  • 晴れる（はれる）= to be sunny → 晴れますように（はれますように）= I hope it's sunny
  • 会える（あえる）= can meet → また会えますように（またあえますように）= I hope we meet again
  • 叶う（かなう）= to come true → 夢が叶いますように（ゆめがかないますように）= may my dream come true

The negative is ～ませんように: 雨が降りませんように (I hope it doesn't rain).

Key idea

～ますように is a wish said out loud, like a small prayer for something to happen.
