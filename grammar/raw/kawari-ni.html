～かわりに: instead of / in exchange for

～かわりに comes from the verb 代わる（かわる）, "to take the place of". It attaches to a Noun + の (父の代わりに) or to a plain-form verb (働く代わりに) and has two related meanings: "instead of" (a substitute) and "in exchange for" (one thing balances another).

1. Instead of (substitution)

With this meaning, X does not happen (or is not used), and something else takes its place.

父の代わりに私が行きます。

"I will go instead of my father."

  • 父（ちち）= (my) father
  • の = links the noun to 代わりに
  • 代わりに（かわりに）= instead of, in place of
  • 私（わたし）= I
  • が = subject marker
  • 行きます（いきます）= will go

2. In exchange for (balance)

With a verb, 代わりに often means that one side gives something and gets something back, a trade or compensation.

働く代わりにお金をもらう。

"I receive money in exchange for working."

  • 働く（はたらく）= to work
  • 代わりに（かわりに）= in exchange for
  • お金（おかね）= money
  • を = object marker
  • もらう = receive

How to form it

Noun + の + 代わりに / Verb plain form + 代わりに

  • 父（ちち）= father → 父の代わりに（ちちのかわりに）= instead of my father
  • 働く（はたらく）= to work → 働く代わりに（はたらくかわりに）= in exchange for working
  • 行く（いく）= to go → 行く代わりに（いくかわりに）= instead of going

Key idea

～かわりに puts two things on a scale: one replaces the other, or one is given and the other received. If you see Noun + の代わりに, think "in place of"; with a verb, also consider "in return for".
