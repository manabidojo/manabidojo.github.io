それで: and then / so

それで is a connecting word that stands at the beginning of a sentence. It picks up what was just said and presents the next sentence as the result or the next step in the story. Literally it is それ ("that") + で ("by, because of").

In English it works like "so" or "and then".

1. Connecting a reason to its result

State a cause in one sentence, then start the next sentence with それで to give the result.

昨日は熱がありました。それで、学校を休みました。

"I had a fever yesterday. So I stayed home from school."

  • 昨日（きのう）= yesterday
  • は = topic marker
  • 熱（ねつ）= fever
  • が = subject marker
  • ありました = there was, (I) had
  • それで = so, because of that
  • 学校（がっこう）= school
  • を = object marker
  • 休みました（やすみました）= was absent, took the day off

2. Moving a story along in conversation

Listeners also use それで to ask for the next part of a story, "and then?".

それで、どうしたの？

"And then what did you do?"

  • それで = and then
  • どう = how, what
  • した = did (past of する, to do)
  • の = casual question particle

How to form it

Reason sentence. それで, result sentence. それで never changes form.

  • 雨が降った。それで、家にいた。（あめがふった。それで、いえにいた。）= It rained. So I stayed home.
  • 道が込んでいた。それで、遅れた。（みちがこんでいた。それで、おくれた。）= The roads were crowded. So I was late.

Key idea

それで means "because of that" or "after that": it points back at the previous sentence and uses it as the springboard for what comes next.
