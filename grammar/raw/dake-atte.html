～だけあって: as you'd expect from

～だけあって says that a result matches what you'd expect from someone's position, effort or reputation. It is usually praise.

In English it is "as you'd expect from..." or "being (that), it's no surprise that...".

1. Praise that fits the reputation

The first part gives the reason for the expectation, the second confirms it.

十年も住んでいただけあって、彼は町に詳しい。

"As you'd expect after ten years there, he knows the town well."

  • 十年も（じゅうねんも）= for as long as ten years
  • 住んでいた（すんでいた）= lived
  • だけあって = as you'd expect
  • 彼（かれ）= he
  • 町（まち）= town
  • に = about
  • 詳しい（くわしい）= knows well

2. Quality that fits the price

It also says that something lives up to its price or fame.

有名な店だけあって、料理はどれもおいしい。

"As you'd expect from a famous restaurant, every dish is delicious."

  • 有名な（ゆうめいな）= famous
  • 店（みせ）= restaurant
  • だけあって = as you'd expect
  • 料理（りょうり）= dishes
  • は = topic marker
  • どれも = every one
  • おいしい = tasty

How to form it

Plain form + だけあって (nouns take noun + だけあって, な-adjectives take な)

  • プロ = professional → プロだけあって = as you'd expect from a pro
  • 練習した（れんしゅうした）= practiced → 練習しただけあって（れんしゅうしただけあって）= having practiced (it shows)
  • 高い（たかい）= expensive → 高いだけあって（たかいだけあって）= as you'd expect for the price

Key idea

～だけあって confirms an expectation: the result is just what that background promised.
