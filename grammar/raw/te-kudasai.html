～てください: please do something

～てください is the standard way to make a polite request in Japanese. It is made by taking the て-form of a verb and adding ください.

It maps closely to English "please do ..." and is one of the most useful everyday patterns, from asking someone to wait to following a teacher's instructions.

1. Polite requests

Use ～てください to politely ask someone to do something for you.

ちょっと待ってください。

"Please wait a moment."

  • ちょっと = a little, a moment
  • 待ってください（まってください）= please wait

2. Soft instructions

Teachers, signs, and instructions also use ～てください to tell people what to do in a polite way.

ここに名前を書いてください。

"Please write your name here."

  • ここ = here
  • に = place marker (where to write)
  • 名前（なまえ）= name
  • を = object marker
  • 書いてください（かいてください）= please write

For people you must show extra respect, a humbler request such as ～ていただけますか (from ～ていただく) sounds softer than ～てください.

How to form it

Verb て-form + ください

  • 待つ（まつ）= to wait → 待ってください（まってください）= please wait
  • 見る（みる）= to look → 見てください（みてください）= please look
  • 来る（くる）= to come → 来てください（きてください）= please come

Key idea

～てください is the all-purpose polite "please do X". It is polite but still a direct request, so use it freely with equals and in instructions.
