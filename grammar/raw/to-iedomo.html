～といえども: even though / even if (formal)

～といえども is a formal way to say "even though (it is)...". It concedes a point, then states that something still applies.

In English it is "even though...", "even if..." or "although". It is common in speeches, writing and serious statements.

1. No exceptions

Even someone special or unusual is not exempt.

子供といえども、ルールは守らなければならない。

"Even children have to follow the rules."

  • 子供（こども）= child
  • といえども = even (if they are)
  • ルール = rules
  • は = topic marker
  • 守らなければならない（まもらなければならない）= must follow

2. Even a small amount

With small quantities, it stresses that even a little matters.

一日といえども、休むわけにはいかない。

"I can't take even a single day off."

  • 一日（いちにち）= one day
  • といえども = even
  • 休む（やすむ）= to take off
  • わけにはいかない = can't afford to

How to form it

Noun / plain form + といえども

  • 先生（せんせい）= teacher → 先生といえども（せんせいといえども）= even a teacher
  • 冗談（じょうだん）= joke → 冗談といえども（じょうだんといえども）= even as a joke
  • 春（はる）= spring → 春といえども（はるといえども）= even though it's spring

Key idea

～といえども grants a point and then insists: even so, this still applies.
