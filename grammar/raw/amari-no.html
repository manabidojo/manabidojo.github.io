あまりの～に: the sheer ... made it happen

あまりの～に is the prenominal version of あまり: あまりの goes before a noun of degree, and に marks that sheer extent as the cause of what follows. The noun is very often an い-adjective converted with ～さ (忙しい → 忙しさ, 暑い → 暑さ).

In English it comes out as "out of sheer ..." or "it was so ... that ...".

The sheer extent as a cause

The あまりの～に phrase states the overwhelming degree; the main clause states what it caused — often something involuntary or impossible.

あまりの忙しさに、食事をする時間もなかった。

"I was so busy that I had no time even to eat."

  • あまりの = sheer, excessive (before a noun)
  • 忙しさ（いそがしさ）= busyness (noun form of 忙しい)
  • に = particle marking the cause
  • 食事（しょくじ）= meal
  • を = object marker
  • する = to do, to have (a meal)
  • 時間（じかん）= time
  • も = even
  • なかった = there was not

あまりの暑さに、頭が痛くなった。

"It was so terribly hot that my head started to ache."

  • あまりの = sheer, excessive
  • 暑さ（あつさ）= heat (noun form of 暑い)
  • に = particle marking the cause
  • 頭（あたま）= head
  • が = subject marker
  • 痛くなった（いたくなった）= started to hurt

How to form it

あまりの + noun (often い-adjective stem + さ) + に + result clause

  • 忙しい（いそがしい）= busy → あまりの忙しさに（あまりのいそがしさに）= from sheer busyness
  • 暑い（あつい）= hot → あまりの暑さに（あまりのあつさに）= from the sheer heat
  • 嬉しい（うれしい）= happy → あまりの嬉しさに（あまりのうれしさに）= out of sheer joy

Key idea

あまりの～に bottles an extreme degree up into a single noun, then uses に to pour it out as the cause: the extent was so overwhelming that the result simply happened.
