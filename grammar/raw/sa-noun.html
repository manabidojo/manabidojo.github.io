～さ: turning an adjective into a noun of degree

～さ turns an adjective into a noun that names its degree or extent. Take an い-adjective, drop the final い, and add さ; for な-adjectives, add さ directly to the stem.

In English this matches noun pairs like "high → height", "long → length", or "kind → kindness". Once an adjective becomes a ～さ noun, you can measure it, compare it, or make it the topic of a sentence.

1. Measurable degree

The most common use is with adjectives of size and amount, where ～さ names the quality as something you can measure with numbers.

この山の高さは三千メートルです。

"This mountain's height is three thousand meters."

  • この = this
  • 山（やま）= mountain
  • の = possessive particle ("'s")
  • 高さ（たかさ）= height
  • は = topic marker
  • 三千（さんぜん）= three thousand
  • メートル = meters
  • です = is (polite)

2. Talking about a quality itself

～さ also works with qualities you cannot measure with a ruler. It lets you talk about the quality as a thing in its own right.

北海道の冬の寒さは有名です。

"The cold of Hokkaido's winters is famous."

  • 北海道（ほっかいどう）= Hokkaido
  • の = possessive particle ("'s")
  • 冬（ふゆ）= winter
  • 寒さ（さむさ）= cold, coldness
  • は = topic marker
  • 有名（ゆうめい）= famous
  • です = is (polite)

How to form it

い-adjective minus い + さ, or な-adjective stem + さ

  • 高い（たかい）= high → 高さ（たかさ）= height
  • 長い（ながい）= long → 長さ（ながさ）= length
  • 便利（べんり）= convenient → 便利さ（べんりさ）= convenience

Key idea

～さ turns a quality into a measurable noun. It converts "high" into "height", a noun you can measure, compare, and talk about.
