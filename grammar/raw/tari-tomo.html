～たりとも: not even (a single)

～たりとも after one + counter says "not even one". The sentence is negative and stresses that nothing at all is allowed or possible.

In English it is "not even a single..." or "not even one...". It is formal and emphatic.

Not even the smallest amount

With one + a counter, it rules out even a single unit of money, time or people.

一円たりとも無駄にできない。

"I can't waste even a single yen."

  • 一円（いちえん）= one yen
  • たりとも = not even
  • 無駄にできない（むだにできない）= can't waste

試合中は、一瞬たりとも気を抜けない。

"During the match, you can't relax for even a second."

  • 試合中（しあいちゅう）= during the match
  • は = topic marker
  • 一瞬（いっしゅん）= an instant
  • たりとも = not even
  • 気を抜けない（きをぬけない）= can't let your guard down

How to form it

One + counter + たりとも + negative

  • 一日（いちにち）= one day → 一日たりとも（いちにちたりとも）= not even a single day
  • 一人（ひとり）= one person → 一人たりとも（ひとりたりとも）= not a single person
  • 一秒（いちびょう）= one second → 一秒たりとも（いちびょうたりとも）= not even one second

Key idea

～たりとも shrinks the amount to one and still says no: not even that.
