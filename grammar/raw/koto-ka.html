～ことか: how very (exclamation)

～ことか at the end of a sentence is an exclamation of strong feeling. It usually comes with a question word like どれほど, どんなに or 何度.

In English it is "how (very)...!" or "you have no idea how...!". It sounds emotional and a bit literary.

An exclamation of feeling

With どれほど or どんなに it stresses how strong a feeling was; with 何度 it stresses how often something happened.

どれほど心配したことか。

"You have no idea how worried I was!"

  • どれほど = how much
  • 心配した（しんぱいした）= worried
  • ことか = (how very)

このドラマを何度見たことか。

"I can't count how many times I've watched this drama!"

  • この = this
  • ドラマ = drama
  • を = object marker
  • 何度（なんど）= how many times
  • 見た（みた）= watched
  • ことか = (how very)

How to form it

Question word (どれほど／どんなに／何度) + plain form + ことか (な-adjectives take な)

  • 嬉しい（うれしい）= happy → どんなに嬉しいことか（どんなにうれしいことか）= how happy (I am)!
  • 待つ（まつ）= to wait → どれほど待ったことか（どれほどまったことか）= how long (I) waited!
  • 静か（しずか）= quiet → 何と静かなことか（なんとしずかなことか）= how quiet it is!

Key idea

～ことか is a sigh or a cry: it says how big a feeling is without needing a number.
