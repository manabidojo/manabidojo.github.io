～始める: begin to do something

～始める（はじめる）attaches to the ます-stem of a verb and means that the action begins. The two verbs fuse into one compound verb: 泣く (to cry) → 泣き始める (to start crying).

It maps directly to English "start doing" / "begin to do". The compound conjugates as a regular る-verb, so the tense goes on 始める: 泣き始めた = "started crying".

Marking the start of an action

Use ～始める to point at the moment an action gets underway. Unlike ～出す (泣き出す), which suggests a sudden, often uncontrollable outburst, ～始める is neutral and works for both sudden and planned beginnings.

子供が泣き始めた。

"The child started crying."

  • 子供（こども）= child
  • が = subject marker
  • 泣き始めた（なきはじめた）= started crying

去年、日本語を勉強し始めました。

"I started studying Japanese last year."

  • 去年（きょねん）= last year
  • 日本語（にほんご）= Japanese (language)
  • を = object marker
  • 勉強し始めました（べんきょうしはじめました）= started studying

How to form it

Verb ます-stem + 始める

  • 泣く（なく）= to cry → 泣き始める（なきはじめる）= to start crying
  • 読む（よむ）= to read → 読み始める（よみはじめる）= to start reading
  • 降る（ふる）= to fall (rain) → 降り始める（ふりはじめる）= to start falling

Key idea

～始める focuses on the moment an action begins, without adding any special nuance about how or why it started.
