～にわたって: over / throughout (a whole span)

渡る means "to extend across". ～にわたって says that something covers the whole of a long period, a wide area or many items.

In English it is "over", "throughout" or "extending over". It stresses the size of the span.

1. A long period

It emphasizes how long something lasted.

会議は三日間にわたって行われた。

"The conference was held over three days."

  • 会議（かいぎ）= conference
  • は = topic marker
  • 三日間（みっかかん）= three days
  • にわたって = over
  • 行われた（おこなわれた）= was held

2. A wide area or scope

It also covers a large area or a broad range of topics.

台風は広い範囲にわたって被害をもたらした。

"The typhoon caused damage over a wide area."

  • 台風（たいふう）= typhoon
  • は = topic marker
  • 広い（ひろい）= wide
  • 範囲（はんい）= area, range
  • にわたって = over
  • 被害（ひがい）= damage
  • もたらした = brought about

How to form it

Noun (time / place / range) + にわたって (before a noun: にわたる + noun)

  • 十年（じゅうねん）= ten years → 十年にわたって（じゅうねんにわたって）= over ten years
  • 全国（ぜんこく）= the whole country → 全国にわたって（ぜんこくにわたって）= across the whole country
  • 多方面（たほうめん）= many fields → 多方面にわたる活動（たほうめんにわたるかつどう）= activities across many fields

Key idea

～にわたって stretches something across a large span and makes you feel how large it is.
