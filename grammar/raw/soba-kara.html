～そばから: as soon as (again and again)

そば means "side, right next to". ～そばから says that as soon as something is done, it is undone, and this repeats again and again.

In English it is "as soon as..." or "no sooner... than...", with a feeling of frustration at repetition.

Undone as soon as it's done

Something is done and immediately reversed, over and over, so the effort feels pointless.

覚えるそばから忘れてしまう。

"I forget things as soon as I learn them."

  • 覚える（おぼえる）= to memorize
  • そばから = as soon as
  • 忘れてしまう（わすれてしまう）= end up forgetting

片付けるそばから、子供が部屋を散らかす。

"As soon as I tidy up, the kids mess up the room again."

  • 片付ける（かたづける）= to tidy up
  • そばから = as soon as
  • 子供（こども）= children
  • が = subject marker
  • 部屋（へや）= room
  • を = object marker
  • 散らかす（ちらかす）= to mess up

How to form it

Verb dictionary form / た-form + そばから

  • 作る（つくる）= to make → 作るそばから（つくるそばから）= as soon as (it's) made
  • 稼ぐ（かせぐ）= to earn → 稼ぐそばから（かせぐそばから）= as soon as (I) earn it
  • 注意する（ちゅういする）= to warn → 注意したそばから（ちゅういしたそばから）= right after being warned

Key idea

～そばから describes a loop: no sooner done than undone, again and again.
