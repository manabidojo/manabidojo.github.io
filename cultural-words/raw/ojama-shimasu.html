お邪魔します (おじゃまします)

The core idea

お邪魔します is what you say when entering someone’s home or space. 邪魔 means “a disturbance” or “getting in the way”, so the phrase literally announces “I’m about to bother you”. English speakers would say “thanks for having me” or nothing at all, which is why subtitles often skip it.

邪魔 was originally a Buddhist word for demons that get in the way of your practice. The past form お邪魔しました is said when leaving.

In short

Said when entering someone’s home, like “sorry to bother you”.

Ways it’s used

Entering someone’s home

Said at the door, even if you’re expected.

「どうぞ、上あがって」「お邪魔じゃまします」

“Come on in.” “Thanks for having me.”

Leaving

The past form, on your way out.

今日きょうは長々ながながとお邪魔じゃましました。

Sorry for staying so long today. Thanks for having me.

Interrupting

Joining a conversation or entering a room.

少すこしお邪魔じゃましてもいいですか？

Mind if I interrupt for a moment?
