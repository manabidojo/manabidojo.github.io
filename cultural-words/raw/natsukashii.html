懐かしい (なつかしい)

The core idea

懐かしい is the warm feeling you get when something brings back the past. A song from high school, a snack you ate as a kid, an old friend’s voice. English usually says “nostalgic”, but that word sounds formal and a little sad, while 懐かしい is everyday and mostly happy.

It’s related to the verb 懐く, “to become attached to”, so the feeling is closer to fondness than to loss. People say it out loud as a quick reaction, often with a smile.

In short

A happy feeling when something reminds you of the past.

Ways it’s used

Reacting to something from the past

The most common use, as a quick exclamation.

うわ、懐なつかしい！このゲーム、昔むかしよくやった。

Oh wow! I used to play this game all the time.

Seeing someone again

Meeting an old friend after a long time.

懐なつかしい顔かおだね。

Now there’s a face I haven’t seen in a while.

Missing a time or place

Thinking back fondly.

学生がくせい時代じだいが懐なつかしいな。

I miss my school days.
