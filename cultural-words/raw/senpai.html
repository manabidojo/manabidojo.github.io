先輩 (せんぱい)

The core idea

先輩 means someone who started before you in the same group, like a school, a club or a company. It’s about when you joined, not age. A younger coworker who joined the company earlier is still your 先輩.

The relationship comes with expectations on both sides. A 先輩 is expected to guide and look after juniors, and sometimes treat them to food. Juniors are expected to show respect and use polite speech. It also works as a title, so students call an older club member 田中先輩 or simply 先輩.

In short

Someone who joined your school, club or company before you.

Ways it’s used

At school or in a club

Older students in the same school or club.

部活ぶかつの先輩せんぱいに挨拶あいさつした。

I said hello to an older member of my club.

At work

Anyone who joined the company earlier.

先輩せんぱいに仕事しごとを教おしえてもらった。

A coworker who’s been here longer showed me how to do the job.

As a title

Used on its own to call someone.

先輩せんぱい、ちょっといいですか？

Hey senpai, got a minute?
