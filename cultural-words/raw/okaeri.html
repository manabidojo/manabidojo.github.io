おかえり

The core idea

おかえり, or the politer おかえりなさい, is the reply to ただいま, said to someone coming home. It comes from 帰る, “to return”, so it’s literally “you’ve returned”. English says “welcome home” or just “hi”, and おかえり gets said every day, not only after long trips.

Because it means “you belong here”, it can carry a lot of emotion for someone returning after a long absence. In anime, a character finally hearing おかえり is often a big moment.

In short

Welcome home, said to someone coming back.

Ways it’s used

Greeting someone coming home

Every day.

「ただいま」「おかえり。ご飯はんできてるよ。」

“I’m home.” “Welcome back. Dinner’s ready.”

After a long time away

An emotional welcome.

長ながい間あいだ、お疲つかれ様さま。おかえりなさい。

You’ve been through so much. Welcome home.

At a themed café

Maid cafés greet customers this way.

おかえりなさいませ、ご主人しゅじん様さま。

Welcome home, master.
