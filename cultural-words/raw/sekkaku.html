せっかく

The core idea

せっかく points out that some effort, kindness or rare chance went into a situation, so it would be a shame to let it go to waste. English handles it with different phrases depending on the sentence, like “since we’re here” or “after all that effort”.

It shows up in two typical patterns. せっかく〜から, “since we’ve got this chance”, leads into making the most of it. せっかく〜のに, “after all that”, leads into disappointment.

In short

This took effort or luck, so let’s not waste it.

Ways it’s used

Making the most of a chance

Since you’re here anyway.

せっかく京都きょうとに来きたんだから、お寺てらも見みようよ。

Since we came all the way to Kyoto, let’s see some temples too.

Wasted effort

Something good that came to nothing.

せっかく作つくったのに、誰だれも食たべなかった。

I went to all the trouble of making it, and nobody ate any.

Turning someone down kindly

Acknowledging an offer you have to refuse.

せっかくですが、今回こんかいは遠慮えんりょしておきます。

It’s very kind of you, but I’ll have to pass this time.
