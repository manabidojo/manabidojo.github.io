しっかり

The core idea

しっかり means firm and steady. It’s used for things held tightly, things done thoroughly, and especially people who are dependable and mature. Saying a child is しっかりしている means they’re responsible for their age.

しっかりしろ is a common anime line, said to someone who’s falling apart or passing out, meaning “pull yourself together!” or “stay with me!”

In short

Firm and reliable.
