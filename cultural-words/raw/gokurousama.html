ご苦労様 (ごくろうさま)

The core idea

ご苦労様 thanks someone for their hard work, from 苦労, “hardship” or “toil”. It sounds a lot like お疲れ様, but it’s normally said by someone higher up to someone below, like a boss to staff or a customer to a delivery person. Saying it to your boss can come across as talking down.

Some people find that rule too strict, and usage varies. If in doubt, お疲れ様 is the safe choice.

In short

Thanks for your work, said by a boss to staff.

Ways it’s used

Boss to staff

Thanking someone below you.

ご苦労くろう様さま。今日きょうはもう帰かえっていいよ。

Thanks for your hard work. You can head home now.

Thanking a worker

Delivery people, security guards and so on.

配達はいたつ、ご苦労くろう様さまです。

Thanks for the delivery.

Sarcasm

Mocking pointless effort.

朝あさからそんなことしてたの？ご苦労くろう様さま。

You’ve been at that since this morning? Well, good for you.
