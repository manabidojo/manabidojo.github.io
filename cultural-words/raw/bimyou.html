微妙 (びみょう)

The core idea

The dictionary meaning of 微妙 is “subtle” or “delicate”, and you’ll still see that in writing. In conversation it’s mostly a polite way to say something isn’t great, a soft “meh”. When asked about food, a movie or a plan, 微妙 lets you avoid saying “bad” outright.

It also covers things that are hard to judge, like a close call or an awkward situation between people.

In short

A polite way to say something isn’t very good.

Ways it’s used

Meh

Not good, said softly.

この映画えいが、ちょっと微妙びみょうだった。

That movie was kind of meh.

Hard to say

A close or unclear situation.

間まに合あうかどうか微妙びみょうだね。

It’s hard to say if we’ll make it in time.

Subtle

The original meaning, more common in writing.

二ふたつの色いろは微妙びみょうに違ちがう。

The two colors are subtly different.

Awkward

A delicate relationship or situation.

あの二人ふたり、今いま微妙びみょうな関係かんけいなんだよ。

Things are a bit awkward between those two right now.
