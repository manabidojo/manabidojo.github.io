いちいち

The core idea

いちいち literally means “one by one”, from 一々. In conversation it’s usually a complaint about someone fussing over every little detail or reacting to everything. English “every single thing” or “do you always have to” carries the same annoyance.

In short

Every little thing, said with annoyance.

Ways it’s used

Fussing over details

Annoyance at nitpicking.

いちいち文句もんく言いわないで。

Stop complaining about every little thing.

Unnecessary checking

Asking about things that don’t need asking.

そんなこといちいち聞きかなくていいよ。

You don’t have to ask me about every little thing.

One by one

The neutral meaning.

名前なまえをいちいち確認かくにんする。

To check each name one by one.
