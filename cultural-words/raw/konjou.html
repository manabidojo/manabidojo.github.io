根性 (こんじょう)

The core idea

根性 is grit, the willpower to keep going through hardship without giving up. 根 is “root”, so it’s strength rooted deep inside someone. Coaches love the word, and so do sports anime.

It has an older meaning too, a person’s basic nature, which survives in insults like 根性が悪い (“nasty to the core”).

In short

The willpower to keep going when things are hard.

Ways it’s used

Grit

Not giving up.

最後さいごまで走はしり切きるなんて、根性こんじょうあるね。

You ran the whole way? You’ve got guts.

Lacking it

A criticism.

そんなことで諦あきらめるなんて、根性こんじょうがない。

Giving up over something like that? You’ve got no backbone.

Bad nature

The older meaning, as an insult.

あいつは根性こんじょうが悪わるい。

He’s a nasty piece of work.
