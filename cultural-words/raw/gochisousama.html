ごちそうさま

The core idea

ごちそうさま, often ごちそうさまでした, is said after a meal to thank whoever made or paid for it. ご馳走 means “a feast”, and 馳走 originally meant running around, from the idea of a host rushing about to gather food for guests.

People also say it to restaurant staff when leaving, and to someone who treated them. As a tease, ごちそうさま is said to a couple who are being lovey-dovey in public, like “okay, that’s enough, I’m full”.

In short

Said after eating, to give thanks for the meal.
