ただいま

The core idea

ただいま is what you say when you get home. It’s short for ただいま帰りました, “I’ve just now returned”, from ただ今, “right now”. The person at home answers おかえり.

Since ただいま also means “right now”, you’ll hear it in formal speech too, as in ただいま参ります (“I’ll be right there”). The context always makes it clear which one is meant.

In short

I’m home, or in formal speech simply “right now”.

Ways it’s used

Coming home

Said at the door.

ただいま！お腹なかすいた。

I’m home! I’m starving.

Back somewhere familiar

Returning anywhere that feels like home.

久ひさしぶりの日本にほん、ただいま！

Japan, I’m back!

Right now

The formal meaning.

ただいま参まいります。

I’ll be right with you.
