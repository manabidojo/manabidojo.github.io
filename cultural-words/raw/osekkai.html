お節介 (おせっかい)

The core idea

お節介 is unwanted help, getting involved in someone else’s business when they didn’t ask. It’s usually well-meant, which is what makes it hard to push back on. A person who does this is also called おせっかい, a busybody who means well.

The sharp reply is 余計なお世話, “mind your own business”, literally “unnecessary care”. Some people call themselves おせっかい with a smile before giving advice, which softens it.

In short

Help nobody asked for.

Ways it’s used

Meddling

Getting involved without being asked.

お節介せっかいかもしれないけど、言いわせて。

This might be none of my business, but hear me out.

A meddling person

Describing someone’s personality.

隣となりのおばさんはおせっかいだけど、優やさしい。

The lady next door is nosy, but she’s kind.

Mind your own business

The cold reply.

余計よけいなお世話せわだよ。

Mind your own business.
