後輩 (こうはい)

The core idea

後輩 is the other side of 先輩, someone who joined the same school, club or company after you. Like 先輩, it depends on when you started, not on age.

Being a 後輩 means using polite speech with your 先輩 and being ready to help out, but it also means you can rely on them for advice. Unlike 先輩, 後輩 is almost never used as a title. You call a 後輩 by their name.

In short

Someone who joined your school, club or company after you.

Ways it’s used

Describing someone

Talking about a junior at school or work.

彼かれは大学だいがくの後輩こうはいです。

He was a few years behind me at university.

Looking after juniors

Part of what a 先輩 is expected to do.

後輩こうはいの面倒めんどうを見みるのも仕事しごとのうちだ。

Looking after the new people is part of the job.

A new arrival

Someone who just joined.

新あたらしい後輩こうはいが入はいってきた。

We got a new person on the team.
