素直 (すなお)

The core idea

素直 describes someone who’s open and honest, who accepts things without stubbornness or hidden motives. 素 is “plain” and 直 is “straight”. Dictionaries offer “obedient”, “honest” and “naive”, and which one fits depends on who’s being described.

It’s high praise for a child or student, meaning they listen and learn well. Between adults, 素直になれ (“be honest with yourself”) usually means admitting your feelings, which is a staple of romance stories. Characters who can’t be 素直 are often the classic tsundere.

In short

Honest and open, without being stubborn.

Ways it’s used

Praising a child

Someone who listens and takes advice well.

この子こは素直すなおでいい子こだね。

He’s such a sweet, good-natured kid.

Just admit it

Doing the honest thing without fuss.

素直すなおに謝あやまったら？

Why don’t you just apologize?

Not being honest

The stubborn opposite.

素直すなおじゃないなあ。

Why can’t you just admit it?
