照れる (てれる)

The core idea

照れる is the shy, slightly embarrassed feeling you get from being praised, teased or noticed in a nice way. It’s embarrassment mixed with being pleased. English says “blush”, “get bashful” or “be flattered”, and each covers only part of it.

照れ隠し is the cover-up, acting grumpy or joking around to hide that you’re flustered. It’s a favorite move of anime characters.

In short

Embarrassed, but in a pleased way.

Ways it’s used

Being praised

Not knowing how to react.

そんなに褒ほめられると照てれるな。

You’re making me blush with all that praise.

Hiding it

照れ隠し, acting tough to cover it up.

照てれ隠かくしで怒おこったふりをした。

She pretended to be angry to hide how embarrassed she was.

Teasing someone

Pointing it out.

照てれてる照てれてる！

Look, you’re blushing!
