いっそ

The core idea

いっそ introduces a drastic option, usually after you’ve had enough of half-measures. It’s like “might as well just…” or “I’d sooner…”. The speaker is considering a big jump instead of putting up with how things are.

いっそのこと is a slightly stronger version with the same meaning.

In short

Might as well do something drastic.

Ways it’s used

A drastic decision

Instead of continuing to struggle.

こんなに迷まようなら、いっそ全部ぜんぶ買かっちゃおう。

If I can’t decide, I might as well just buy all of them.

Wishing for an extreme

Usually out of frustration.

いっそ会社かいしゃを辞やめちゃおうかな。

Maybe I should just quit my job.

Rather than this

Preferring a clear outcome to waiting.

いっそのこと、はっきり言いってくれたほうが楽らくだ。

Honestly, it’d be easier if you just told me straight.
