俺 (おれ)

The core idea

俺 is a casual way to say “I”, used mostly by men and boys. Among friends it sounds relaxed and confident, and in polite situations it sounds rough or arrogant. English only has one word for “I”, so subtitles can’t show the difference, yet the choice of 俺 tells you a lot about how a character sees himself and who he’s talking to.

Many men switch pronouns during the day, using 俺 with friends and 僕 or 私 at work. A few female characters use 俺 too, usually to come across as tomboyish.

In short

A casual, masculine “I”, used with friends and equals.

Ways it’s used

With friends

Relaxed, everyday speech among equals.

俺おれ、今日きょうはもう帰かえるわ。

I’m heading home for today.

Sounding confident

Characters who want to sound tough or cool lean on it.

俺おれに任まかせろ。

Leave it to me.

Switching pronouns

The same person often uses different words for “I” depending on who’s listening.

会社かいしゃでは「私わたし」だけど、家いえでは「俺おれ」だよ。

At work I say watashi, but at home I say ore.
