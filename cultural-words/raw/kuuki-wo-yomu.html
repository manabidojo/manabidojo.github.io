空気を読む (くうきをよむ)

The core idea

空気を読む literally means “to read the air”. It’s sensing the mood of a group and what’s expected without anyone saying it out loud, then acting on it. English “read the room” is close.

Someone who doesn’t do this is called KY, short for 空気が読めない (“can’t read the air”), a slang term that took off around 2007.

In short

Sensing the mood and what’s expected without being told.

Ways it’s used

Reading the mood

Sensing what a group expects.

空気くうきを読よんで、何なにも言いわなかった。

I read the room and didn’t say anything.

Criticizing someone

Someone who misses the mood.

少すこしは空気くうき読よめよ。

Read the room a little, would you?

Choosing not to

Ignoring the mood on purpose.

あえて空気くうきを読よまずに反対はんたいした。

I ignored the mood on purpose and spoke up against it.
