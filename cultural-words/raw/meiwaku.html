迷惑 (めいわく)

The core idea

迷惑 means trouble or inconvenience caused to other people. The kanji are “lost” and “confused”, and the word first meant being at a loss yourself, before it shifted to the trouble you cause others.

A lot of everyday etiquette is about not causing 迷惑, like keeping your voice down on trains. The phrase 迷惑をかける, “to cause trouble”, shows up in apologies all the time, even for small things.

In short

Trouble or bother caused to other people.

Ways it’s used

Apologizing

For causing trouble.

ご迷惑めいわくをおかけしてすみません。

I’m sorry for the trouble.

Being a nuisance

Behavior that bothers others.

電車でんしゃで電話でんわするのは迷惑めいわくだよ。

Talking on the phone on the train bothers people.

Unwanted

Something nobody asked for.

迷惑めいわくメールが多おおすぎる。

I get way too much spam.

Not wanting to be a burden

Worrying about relying on others.

みんなに迷惑めいわくをかけたくない。

I don’t want to be a burden on anyone.
