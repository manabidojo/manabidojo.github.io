いただきます

The core idea

いただきます is said before eating, often with hands together. It comes from いただく, the humble verb for “to receive”, so it means something like “I humbly receive this”. It thanks the people who made the food and, for many, the plants and animals that became it.

Subtitles usually render it as “let’s eat” or “thanks for the food”, or skip it. It’s also used more broadly to accept something, like a drink offered by a host.

In short

Said before eating, to give thanks for the food.

Ways it’s used

Before a meal

Often said together.

「さあ、食たべよう」「いただきます！」

“Okay, let’s eat.” “Thanks for the food!”

Accepting something offered

Taking a drink or a snack.

じゃあ、お茶ちゃをいただきます。

I’ll have some tea, then. Thank you.

Gladly accepting

When someone insists you have some.

では、遠慮えんりょなくいただきます。

Well, don’t mind if I do.
