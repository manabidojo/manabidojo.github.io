申し訳ない (もうしわけない)

The core idea

申し訳ない is a deep, formal apology. 申し訳 means “an excuse” or “an explanation”, so the phrase literally says “there is no excuse”. The polite forms 申し訳ありません and 申し訳ございません are the standard apologies at work and in customer service.

Compared with すみません or ごめんなさい, it signals that you take full responsibility. Said casually, 申し訳ない can also mean “I feel bad”, for example about putting someone to a lot of trouble.

In short

A deep, formal apology, literally “there is no excuse”.

Ways it’s used

Formal apology

At work or to a customer.

ご迷惑めいわくをおかけして、申もうし訳わけございません。

We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience.

Feeling bad

About someone’s trouble.

何度なんども来きてもらって申もうし訳わけない。

I feel bad making you come so many times.

Polite refusal

In customer service.

申もうし訳わけありませんが、本日ほんじつは満席まんせきです。

I’m very sorry, but we’re fully booked today.
