せめて

The core idea

せめて means “at least”, the minimum you’d settle for when you can’t get what you really want. It carries a feeling of reluctance or longing that the English phrase doesn’t always have.

It often goes with a wish or a request, as in せめて〜だけでも (“if only…” or “even just…”).

In short

If I can’t have it all, at least give me this.

Ways it’s used

A minimum request

Asking for at least something.

せめて連絡れんらくくらいしてよ。

You could have at least let me know.

A small wish

Settling for less.

せめて一目ひとめだけでも会あいたい。

I just want to see her, even for a moment.

Making up for something

Doing what little you can.

せめてこれくらいはさせてください。

Please, at least let me do this much.
