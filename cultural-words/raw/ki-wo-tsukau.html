気を遣う (きをつかう)

The core idea

気を遣う means paying attention to other people’s feelings and needs, and adjusting what you do so they’re comfortable. 気 is “mind” or “spirit” and 遣う is “to use”, so you’re spending your attention on others. It covers small kindnesses like refilling someone’s glass, and also the tiring side of always watching how you come across.

気を遣わないで (“please don’t go to any trouble”) is a common reply when someone is being considerate, especially when they bring a gift.

In short

Paying attention to other people’s feelings and needs.

Ways it’s used

Being considerate

Taking care of others.

彼女かのじょはいつも周まわりに気きを遣つかっている。

She’s always looking out for the people around her.

Don’t trouble yourself

Responding to someone’s kindness.

そんなに気きを遣つかわないでください。

Please don’t go to any trouble for me.

Exhausting

When it becomes tiring.

上司じょうしとの飲のみ会かいは気きを遣つかうから疲つかれる。

Drinks with my boss wear me out, because I’m on my best behavior the whole time.
