やっぱり

The core idea

やっぱり means things turned out the way you thought, or the way they usually are. It can confirm a guess (“I knew it”), show you’ve gone back to your first choice (“on second thought”), or state what you’ve concluded (“after all”).

やはり is the more formal version, and やっぱ the most casual. It comes up constantly in conversation, and subtitles often drop it, because English doesn’t mark “as expected” nearly as often.

In short

Just as I expected, or going back to your first choice.

Ways it’s used

I knew it

Confirming a suspicion.

やっぱり、そうだと思おもった。

I knew it.

Changing your mind back

Going back to your first idea.

やっぱりコーヒーにします。

Actually, I’ll have the coffee after all.

All things considered

Stating a conclusion.

やっぱり家いえが一番いちばんだね。

There’s no place like home.
