よろしくお願いします (よろしくおねがいします)

The core idea

よろしくお願いします is said whenever you’re starting a relationship or asking someone to take care of something. Literally it’s “please treat me well”, from よろしく (“well” or “suitably”) and お願いします (“I request”). Subtitles turn it into “nice to meet you”, “thanks in advance” or “looking forward to working with you”.

The short よろしく is casual, for friends or people your own age. よろしくお願いいたします is more formal, and very common at the end of business emails. It’s also used for passing on regards, as in 〜によろしく (“say hi to…”).

In short

Please treat me well, used in greetings and requests.

Ways it’s used

Meeting someone

Introductions.

田中たなかです。よろしくお願ねがいします。

I’m Tanaka. Nice to meet you.

Asking a favor

When someone takes on a task for you.

この件けん、よろしくお願ねがいします。

I’ll leave this in your hands. Thanks.

Casual

With friends, often at New Year.

今年ことしもよろしく！

Here’s to another great year!

Passing on regards

〜によろしく.

お母かあさんによろしく伝つたえてね。

Say hi to your mom for me.
