行ってらっしゃい (いってらっしゃい)

The core idea

行ってらっしゃい is the reply to 行ってきます, said by the person staying behind. It literally means “go and come back”, so it sends someone off while expecting them home again.

English uses “have a good day” or “take care”, but 行ってらっしゃい is a fixed part of leaving the house, the same every time. Hotel staff also say it to guests heading out for the day.

In short

Said to someone leaving home, like “have a good day”.

Ways it’s used

Seeing someone off

At the door in the morning.

気きをつけて、行いってらっしゃい。

Take care, have a good day.

Before a trip

Sending someone on a journey.

楽たのしんでね、行いってらっしゃい！

Have fun, see you when you’re back!

Service staff

The extra-polite version used in hotels.

行いってらっしゃいませ。

Have a nice day.
