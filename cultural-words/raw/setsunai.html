切ない (せつない)

The core idea

切ない is a quiet, aching sadness, usually about something you can’t have or can’t keep. Unrequited love, a goodbye, the last day of summer. It hurts, but there’s often something beautiful in it too.

English tends to reach for “bittersweet” or “heartbreaking”, and which one fits depends on how sharp the feeling is.

In short

A sad, painful feeling of wanting what you can’t have.

Ways it’s used

Unrequited love

Liking someone who can’t feel the same.

好すきな人ひとに恋人こいびとがいるって、切せつないね。

It hurts when the person you like is already taken.

A song or story

Something that tugs at you.

この曲きょく、なんか切せつない。

This song makes my chest ache a little.

Goodbyes

Parting from people or a time of your life.

卒業式そつぎょうしきって切せつないよね。

Graduation ceremonies are so bittersweet.
