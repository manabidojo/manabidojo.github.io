さすが

The core idea

さすが says that someone lived up to their reputation, that they did what you’d expect from a person like them. It’s praise with a sense of “of course you did”, as in “that’s our captain!” or “no wonder, you’re a pro”.

さすがに works differently. It means even someone or something with that reputation has limits, as in “that’s going a bit far, even for you”.

In short

Praise for living up to expectations, while さすがに means “even so, that’s too much”.

Ways it’s used

Living up to expectations

Praising someone’s skill or character.

さすが先輩せんぱい、すごいですね！

I knew you’d nail it, senpai!

As expected of something

About places and things too.

さすが日本にほんの電車でんしゃ、時間じかんぴったりだ。

Japanese trains, right on time as always.

Even so, too much

さすがに, admitting a limit.

さすがに三杯目さんばいめは無理むりだよ。

A third bowl is too much, even for me.
