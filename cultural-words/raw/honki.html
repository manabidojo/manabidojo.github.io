本気 (ほんき)

The core idea

本気 means being serious, really meaning something or putting in real effort. 本 is “true” and 気 is “spirit”, so it’s your true intent. Asking 本気？ is “are you serious?”, and 本気を出す means to stop holding back and go all out.

In anime, the moment a character says 本気を出す is usually when the fight turns. In romance, 本気 means real feelings, as opposed to just playing around.

In short

I really mean it, in love or in a fight.

Ways it’s used

Are you serious?

Checking someone means it.

それ、本気ほんきで言いってる？

Are you seriously saying that?

Going all out

No more holding back.

そろそろ本気ほんき出だすか。

Guess it’s time to get serious.

Real feelings

In romance.

彼かれのこと、本気ほんきで好すきなの。

I really, truly like him.
