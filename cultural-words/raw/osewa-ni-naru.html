お世話になる (おせわになる)

The core idea

お世話になる means being looked after by someone, receiving their help or care. 世話 is “care” or “looking after”. People say it to thank someone who helped them over a period of time, like a teacher, a host family or a colleague.

In business it turns into a fixed greeting, いつもお世話になっております, which opens most work emails and phone calls. It means roughly “thank you for your continued support”, even when the two people have never actually helped each other.

In short

Being helped or looked after by someone, also a business greeting.

Ways it’s used

Thanking someone for their help

Often in the past tense, when something ends.

三年間さんねんかん、お世話せわになりました。

Thank you for everything these past three years.

Business greeting

The standard opening of emails and calls.

いつもお世話せわになっております。

Thank you, as always, for your support.

Thanking someone in advance

When you’re about to rely on someone, like at a new job or a homestay.

これからお世話せわになります。

Thank you in advance for having me.
