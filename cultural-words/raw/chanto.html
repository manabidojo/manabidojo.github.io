ちゃんと

The core idea

ちゃんと means doing something properly, the way it’s supposed to be done. The dictionary says “properly” or “neatly”, but it often carries a social expectation. ちゃんとして means “act like a responsible adult”, and ちゃんとした人 is someone reliable who has their life in order.

Parents and teachers say it a lot, as in ちゃんと食べて and ちゃんと謝って. It’s the voice of someone who expects you to do things right.

In short

Properly, the way it should be done.

Ways it’s used

Properly

Doing something fully, without cutting corners.

ちゃんと手てを洗あらってね。

Make sure you wash your hands properly.

A reliable person

Someone who has their life in order.

彼かれはちゃんとした人ひとだよ。

He’s got his life together.

Getting it together

Telling someone to behave.

もう大人おとななんだから、ちゃんとしなさい。

You’re an adult now, so act like it.
