わざわざ

The core idea

わざわざ means someone went out of their way to do something they didn’t have to. It comes from わざ, as in わざと (“on purpose”). Whether it’s gratitude or criticism depends on who did it and why.

Toward someone who helped you, it’s warm thanks. Toward a pointless effort, it’s “why would you even bother?”

In short

You didn’t have to, but you did.

Ways it’s used

Thanking someone

For effort they didn’t need to make.

わざわざ来きてくれてありがとう。

Thanks for coming all this way.

Questioning the effort

Something that seems unnecessary.

わざわざそんなことしなくていいのに。

You really didn’t need to go to all that trouble.

Going out of your way

A neutral description.

雨あめの中なか、わざわざ遠とおい店みせまで行いった。

I went all the way to a faraway shop in the rain.
