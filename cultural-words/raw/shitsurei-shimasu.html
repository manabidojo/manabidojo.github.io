失礼します (しつれいします)

The core idea

失礼します literally means “I’m about to be rude”. 失礼 is “rudeness” or “a breach of manners”. It’s said before doing anything that might intrude, like entering an office, hanging up the phone or leaving a meeting early. English would say “excuse me”, “goodbye” or nothing at all.

The past form 失礼しました is an apology for something you already did, and お先に失礼します is the standard way to leave work before others.

In short

Excuse me, said when entering or leaving a room.

Ways it’s used

Entering a room

Knocking on an office door, for example.

失礼しつれいします。田中たなかです。

Excuse me, it’s Tanaka.

Leaving

Ending a visit, a meeting or a phone call.

では、失礼しつれいします。

Well then, goodbye.

Leaving work first

Said to coworkers who are still working.

お先さきに失礼しつれいします。

I’ll be heading out now.

Apologizing

The past form, for a small mistake.

失礼しつれいしました。

Sorry about that.
