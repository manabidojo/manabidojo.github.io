行ってきます (いってきます)

The core idea

行ってきます is said when leaving home, and literally means “I’ll go and come back”. It promises you’ll return, which is why it’s used for everyday departures to school or work rather than for long goodbyes.

The person staying home answers 行ってらっしゃい. Subtitles usually turn it into “I’m off” or “see you later”. It’s also used at work when stepping out for a while.

In short

Said when leaving home, meaning “I’ll be back”.

Ways it’s used

Leaving home

Every morning.

「行いってきます！」「行いってらっしゃい！」

“I’m off!” “Have a good day!”

Stepping out

Leaving work for a short errand.

郵便局ゆうびんきょくに行いってきます。

I’m just popping over to the post office.

Before a trip

Going somewhere and planning to come back.

じゃあ、東京とうきょうに行いってきます。

Well then, I’m off to Tokyo.
