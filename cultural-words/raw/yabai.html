やばい

The core idea

やばい started as slang for “dangerous” or “risky”, the kind of situation where you might get caught. Today it reacts to anything extreme, good or bad, a bit like “crazy” or “insane” in casual English.

Whether it’s praise or panic depends on the tone and the situation. Older speakers still mostly use it for trouble, while younger speakers use it for praise just as often.

In short

Something extreme, either very good or very bad.

Ways it’s used

Trouble

Something bad is about to happen.

やばい、遅刻ちこくする！

Oh no, I’m going to be late!

Amazing

Strong praise, especially among younger speakers.

このラーメン、やばい。

This ramen is insane.

Something’s off

Said about a person, it usually means they’re in a bad way or acting strangely.

あいつ、最近さいきんちょっとやばくない？

Isn’t he acting kind of off lately?
