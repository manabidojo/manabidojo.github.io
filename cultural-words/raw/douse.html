どうせ

The core idea

どうせ means “no matter what, it’ll end up the same way”, usually with a resigned or bitter tone. People say it when they’ve already decided how something will turn out, often badly. English “anyway” or “it’s not like…” come close.

どうせなら gives it a brighter turn. It means “if we’re doing it anyway, let’s do it properly”.

In short

It will end the same way anyway, usually badly.

Ways it’s used

Giving up in advance

Expecting the worst.

どうせ私わたしなんか無理むりだよ。

There’s no way someone like me could do it anyway.

Sulking

Common in arguments.

どうせ俺おれのことなんてどうでもいいんだろ。

Not like you care about me anyway.

If you’re going to anyway

どうせなら, making the most of it.

どうせなら一番いちばんいいのを買かおう。

If we’re buying one anyway, let’s get the best one.
