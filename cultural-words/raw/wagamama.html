わがまま

The core idea

わがまま means being self-centered, insisting on your own way without thinking about others. In kanji it’s 我が儘, roughly “as I please”. Translations say “selfish” or “spoiled”, but it can also be soft and affectionate.

わがままを言う often just means making a request, especially a small or personal one. Saying わがままを言ってすみません when asking for something is polite, a way of admitting you’re asking a favor.

In short

Selfish, or politely asking for something you want.

Ways it’s used

Selfish

Criticizing someone.

わがまま言いわないの！

Don’t be so difficult!

A small request

Politely asking for something.

わがままを言いってすみませんが、窓側まどがわの席せきにしてもらえますか？

Sorry to be a bother, but could I have a window seat?

Affectionate

Between people who are close.

たまにはわがまま言いってもいいんだよ。

It’s okay to ask for what you want once in a while.
