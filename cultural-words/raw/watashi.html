私 (わたし)

The core idea

私 is the standard, neutral “I”. It’s the safe choice in polite speech for everyone, and in casual speech it’s mostly used by women. So a man saying 私 with close friends sounds stiff, while a woman saying it sounds completely normal.

The same kanji has a more formal reading, わたくし, used in speeches and business calls. In casual speech, girls and young women often say あたし instead.

In short

The “I” that’s never wrong in polite speech, and the everyday “I” for most women.

Ways it’s used

Polite speech

Anyone, male or female, uses it at work or with strangers.

私わたしが担当たんとうします。

I’ll be in charge of this.

Casual speech among women

The everyday “I” for most women.

私わたしもそれ好すき！

I like that too!

Very formal

Read わたくし in speeches and on business calls.

私わたくし、営業部えいぎょうぶの田中たなかと申もうします。

My name is Tanaka, from the sales department.
