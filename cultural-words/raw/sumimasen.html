すみません

The core idea

すみません is used for “sorry”, “excuse me” and “thank you”, which surprises a lot of learners. It comes from 済む, “to be settled”, so it literally means “this isn’t settled”, the feeling that you owe someone something you can’t fully repay.

That’s why it works as thanks. When someone does you a favor, すみません acknowledges the trouble they went to. It’s also the standard way to get a waiter’s attention, and in speech it’s often shortened to すいません.

In short

Sorry, excuse me, or thank you.

Ways it’s used

Apologizing

A light “sorry”.

遅おくれてすみません。

Sorry I’m late.

Getting attention

In a restaurant or shop.

すみません、注文ちゅうもんいいですか？

Excuse me, could we order?

Thanking someone

When they went to some trouble.

わざわざすみません。

Thank you so much for going to the trouble.
