懲りる (こりる)

The core idea

懲りる means learning your lesson from a bad experience, so that you don’t want to go through it again. It’s always about something unpleasant that taught you.

The negative, 懲りない, describes someone who keeps making the same mistake no matter how often it goes wrong. It can be an insult or grudging admiration.

In short

To learn your lesson from a bad experience.

Ways it’s used

Learning the hard way

After something goes wrong.

一度いちど失敗しっぱいして懲こりた。

I failed once, and I learned my lesson.

Never learning

Someone who keeps doing it.

懲こりないやつだな。

You never learn, do you?

Not discouraged

Trying again anyway.

何度なんど振ふられても懲こりずに告白こくはくする。

He keeps confessing, no matter how many times he gets turned down.
