僕 (ぼく)

The core idea

僕 is a way to say “I” used mostly by boys and men. It’s softer than 俺 and less formal than 私, so it often comes across as gentle or a bit boyish. Plenty of adult men use it in everyday speech, especially where 俺 would feel too rough.

Adults also use 僕 to mean “you” when talking to a small boy, as in “how old are you, 僕?”. That use surprises a lot of learners.

In short

A softer “I”, mostly used by boys and men.

Ways it’s used

Everyday “I”

Used by many men in casual and semi-polite speech.

僕ぼくはコーヒーにします。

I’ll have coffee.

Gentle or shy characters

In anime, a quiet or kind boy often uses 僕.

僕ぼく、ちょっと緊張きんちょうしてる。

I’m a little nervous.

Talking to a little boy

Adults use 僕 as “you” with small boys.

僕ぼく、何歳なんさい？

How old are you, little guy?
