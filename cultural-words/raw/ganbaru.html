頑張る (がんばる)

The core idea

頑張る means to keep going and give something your full effort, especially when it’s hard. English needs different phrases for it depending on the moment, like “do your best”, “hang in there” or “good luck”.

頑張って is said to encourage others, 頑張ります is a promise to try hard, and 頑張った is praise for effort, often more than for results. When someone is already struggling a lot, hearing 頑張って can feel like pressure, so people sometimes say 無理しないで (“don’t push yourself”) instead.

In short

To try your hardest and not give up.

Ways it’s used

Encouraging someone

Before a test, a game or a new job.

明日あしたの試験しけん、頑張がんばってね！

Good luck on your test tomorrow!

Promising to try

A way of saying you’ll do your best.

次つぎはもっと頑張がんばります。

I’ll try harder next time.

Praising effort

Recognizing the work, whatever the result.

よく頑張がんばったね。

You did great.
