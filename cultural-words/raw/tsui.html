つい

The core idea

つい describes doing something without meaning to, before you could stop yourself. It’s used for small slips like eating one more snack or saying something you shouldn’t. English often says “I couldn’t help it” or “without thinking”.

With time or distance it means “just”, as in つい最近 (“just recently”) or つい先ほど (“only a moment ago”).

In short

I didn’t mean to, it just happened.

Ways it’s used

Slipping up

Doing something out of habit or impulse.

つい食たべすぎちゃった。

I ended up eating too much.

Saying too much

Something that came out before you thought.

つい本音ほんねが出でちゃった。

I said what I really thought without meaning to.

Just, only

With time.

つい最近さいきん引ひっ越こしたばかりです。

I only just moved here.
