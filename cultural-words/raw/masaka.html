まさか

The core idea

まさか expresses that something is too unlikely to believe, or that you hope it isn’t true. On its own it’s “no way!” or “you’re kidding”. At the start of a sentence it introduces a worrying guess, like “surely not…?”

まさかの in front of a noun means “the last thing anyone expected”, as in まさかの展開 (“an unexpected twist”).

In short

That can’t be true… can it?

Ways it’s used

Disbelief

Reacting to surprising news.

まさか！本当ほんとうに？

No way! Really?

A worrying guess

Suspecting something you don’t want to be true.

まさか、忘わすれたんじゃないよね？

You didn’t forget, did you?

Never expected

Something that actually happened against the odds.

まさか優勝ゆうしょうするとは思おもわなかった。

I never thought we’d actually win.
