なんとなく

The core idea

なんとなく means doing or feeling something without a clear reason. It’s an honest answer to “why?”, and nobody expects you to explain further. English says “for some reason”, “just because” or “I don’t know, kind of”, which all sound vaguer or more evasive than なんとなく does.

In kanji it’s 何となく, which is close to “somehow”.

In short

No real reason, it just felt that way.

Ways it’s used

No particular reason

Doing something on a whim.

なんとなく駅えきまで歩あるいてみた。

I just felt like walking to the station.

A vague feeling

Sensing something you can’t explain.

なんとなく嫌いやな予感よかんがする。

I’ve got a bad feeling about this, somehow.

Roughly understanding

Getting the general idea.

意味いみはなんとなくわかる。

I kind of get what it means.
