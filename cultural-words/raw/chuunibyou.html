中二病 (ちゅうにびょう)

The core idea

中二病 literally means “second-year-of-middle-school syndrome”, from 中二 (around age 14) and 病 (“illness”). It describes the over-the-top, self-dramatizing phase some teenagers go through, like believing they have secret powers, acting dark and mysterious, or thinking nobody understands them.

The term started as a joke on a Japanese radio show in 1999 and spread online. It’s usually used to tease, including about your own past, and in anime it’s a whole character type. English “edgy” covers part of it.

In short

An embarrassing teenage phase of acting dramatic and special.

Ways it’s used

Teasing someone

Calling out dramatic behavior.

その台詞せりふ、完全かんぜんに中二病ちゅうにびょうじゃん。

That line is so edgy.

Looking back at your own past

Embarrassment about your teen years.

昔むかしは中二病ちゅうにびょうだったから、ノートに呪文じゅもんを書かいてた。

I had a real edgy phase back then. I used to write spells in my notebook.

Describing a character

A common type in anime and manga.

中二病ちゅうにびょうキャラが出でてくるアニメが好すき。

I like anime with chuunibyou characters.
