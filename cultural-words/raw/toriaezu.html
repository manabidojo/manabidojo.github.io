とりあえず

The core idea

とりあえず means “for now”, doing the first or easiest thing and leaving the rest for later. It sets a temporary step without committing to what comes after.

The best-known example is the restaurant order とりあえずビール, “a beer to start, and we’ll see”.

In short

For now, as a first step.

Ways it’s used

First step

Starting with something simple.

とりあえず座すわろうか。

Let’s sit down first.

For the time being

A temporary solution.

とりあえずこれで大丈夫だいじょうぶです。

This should do for now.

Ordering

The classic opener at an izakaya.

とりあえずビールで。

Beer to start, please.
