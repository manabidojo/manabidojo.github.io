もやもや

The core idea

もやもや describes a vague, unsettled feeling you can’t quite put into words, like fog in your chest. It’s related to もや, “haze” or “mist”. People use it for doubt, frustration that hasn’t been resolved, or jealousy they can’t explain.

When the feeling finally lifts, it’s said to 晴れる, “clear up”, like the weather.

In short

Something’s bothering me, and I can’t say what.

Ways it’s used

Something doesn’t sit right

After a conversation or a decision.

話はなし合あったけど、まだもやもやする。

We talked it over, but something still doesn’t feel right.

Unclear feelings

Not understanding your own emotions.

二人ふたりが仲なかよくしてるのを見みると、なんかもやもやする。

Seeing those two getting along makes me feel weird somehow.

Clearing it up

Getting rid of the feeling.

話はなしたら、もやもやが晴はれた。

Talking about it cleared my head.
