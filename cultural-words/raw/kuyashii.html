悔しい (くやしい)

The core idea

悔しい is the frustration of losing, failing or being beaten when you know you could have done better. It mixes disappointment and anger at yourself with the urge to try again. English spreads that across words like “frustrating”, “gutting” or “I’m so mad”.

It’s also used when someone else gets the better of you, like losing an argument or being teased. Sports anime are full of it, usually right before a training montage.

In short

Frustrated after losing, and wanting to try again.

Ways it’s used

Losing

After a game or competition.

負まけて悔くやしい！

I’m so frustrated we lost!

Coming close

Almost succeeding.

あと一点いってんだったのに、悔くやしいなあ。

Just one more point. That really stings.

Being outdone

When someone gets the better of you.

言いい返かえせなくて悔くやしかった。

It drove me crazy that I couldn’t think of a comeback.
