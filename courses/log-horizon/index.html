Log Horizon vocabulary, episode by episode

25 lessons

ManabiDojo Academy teaches the Japanese of Log Horizon one episode at a time, on Crunchyroll. Each lesson teaches up to 10 words from the episode before you watch it.

Course data as of 14 October 2026.

Vocabulary difficulty

Hard

See the vocabulary breakdown →

Each lesson

  1. 1

    Practice the words

    Flashcards of the hardest words in the episode, so you know them before you watch. The ones you miss come back until you know them.

  2. 2

    Study the episode

    Watch with your usual subtitles. The sentences of the lesson also show in Japanese, and the video pauses after them so you can read them, look up any word and replay them with R.

  3. 3

    Pass the quiz

    10 sentences of the lesson play in the episode with a question each, 7 correct answers to pass.

Three levels

You pick a level when you start the course and can change it later. Each level has its own words and sentences in every lesson.

  • Level 1

    The most common words only

    129 words

    245 sentences. Words among the 500 most common.

  • Level 2

    A wider vocabulary

    202 words

    357 sentences. Words among the 1,500 most common.

  • Level 3

    The widest vocabulary

    213 words

    413 sentences. Words among the 3,000 most common.
