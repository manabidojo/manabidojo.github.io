Anime Extension

Learn Japanese as you watch anime

Interactive Japanese subtitles

Our extension adds interactive Japanese subtitles to your favorite shows. Click individual words to pull up meanings, translations and more.

AI Powered Explanations

Not sure what a line means? Let the AI break down complex sentences or grammar for you.

One-Click Flashcards

Create decks and flashcards for words you want to review later. If you prefer studying with Anki, you can export your decks to Anki format.

Interactive quizzes

Quiz yourself on words from the episode you are watching. Practice mode replays a line with one word left out, and you pick the missing word.

Web Extension

Learn Japanese from manga, YouTube, and the web

Parse any Japanese website

Click any word on the page for its dictionary entry, reading and kanji breakdown, right where you read. News, blogs, web novels.

Manga OCR

We detect Japanese text inside manga images and give you interactive Japanese subtitles.

YouTube OCR

Pause any YouTube video to turn hardcoded subtitles, signs, and slides into clickable Japanese chunks.

Collect words and level them up

Save any word you look up, from anime, manga or any page. The wordbook levels it up as you review it and turns it into practice.
